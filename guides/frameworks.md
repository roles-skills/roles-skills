# How the frameworks fit together

This reference combines several frameworks. Each one answers a different question.

| Question | Answered by | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What kind of job is this? | UK GDaD PCF roles, and ESCO occupations | Role pages, [PCF index](../docs/pcf.md), [ESCO index](../docs/esco.md) |
| How senior is it? | Bands, informed by PCF role levels and Civil Service grades | [Roles by band](../docs/bands.md), [band mapping](../research/band-mapping.md) |
| Why is it in that band? | The 16-factor job evaluation scores | Each role level, [job evaluation](../research/job-evaluation.md) |
| What skills does it need, and how well? | PCF skills and skills original to this reference, rated on the PCF's four-level scale | Each role level, [skills catalogue](../docs/skills.md) |
| What do other employers call these skills? | ESCO skills and knowledge, essential and optional | Each role page, [crosswalk](../data/crosswalks/pcf-esco.tsv) |
| How does it compare with other digital skills frameworks? | SFIA levels of responsibility | [`data/bands.yaml`](../data/bands.yaml), [SFIA note](../research/sfia.md) |

## UK GDaD PCF

The UK Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework describes 53 digital and data roles, 206 role levels, and 186 skills. Each skill is rated on four levels: Awareness, Working, Practitioner, and Expert. This reference uses it for the roles, levels, and skills of every digital and data role that it covers, and quotes it word for word. See the [research note](../research/uk-gdad-pcf.md).

## ESCO

ESCO, the European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations classification, describes 3,039 occupations and 13,939 skills and knowledge concepts, in 28 languages (v1.2.1). Each occupation has essential and optional skills. ESCO has no proficiency levels or seniority. This reference links every role to ESCO occupations, and every skill to its closest ESCO skills. See the [research note](../research/esco.md) and the [framework comparison](../research/framework-comparison.md).

## Bands and job evaluation

Bands run from 1 to 9, with Band 8 split into 8a, 8b, 8c, and 8d. Each band has a short competency outline and a range of job evaluation points. Each role level is scored on 16 factors, such as knowledge, freedom to act, and responsibility for people, and its total must fall within its band's range. The scores are illustrative.

## Skills original to this reference

Some skills are not in the PCF but matter in digital health care, or in corporate roles. This reference adds them using the same four-level scale. Examples include clinical risk management, health data interoperability, clinical coding, information governance, financial reporting, employee relations, and contract management. Each one is linked to its closest ESCO skills.
