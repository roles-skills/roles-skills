# Mbunifu wa mwingiliano

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu unaomlenga mtumiaji](../../#usanifu-unaomlenga-mtumiaji)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Interaction designer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/interaction-designer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [user interface designer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/96e20037-0a25-4bf6-a25e-808d1605d890) (ISCO-08 2513)

## Muhtasari

Wabunifu wa mwingiliano husanifu jinsi wagonjwa, walezi, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo wanavyotumia huduma za afya za kidijitali za taasisi, kuanzia programu tumizi na fomu zinazowalenga wagonjwa hadi skrini ambazo madaktari hutumia kando ya kitanda. Husanifu mwingiliano rahisi, unaofikika na salama, huujaribu na watumiaji halisi, na hutumia mifumo ya usanifu inayoshirikiwa ili huduma zihisi kuwa thabiti na zinazozoeleka.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Madaktari mara nyingi hutumia mifumo chini ya shinikizo la muda, wakikatizwa na kwenye vifaa vinavyoshirikiwa, hivyo miundo lazima iunge mkono kazi ya haraka na sahihi na kufanya hatua salama kuwa rahisi.
- Usanifu duni unaweza kusababisha madhara ya kitabibu, kama vile kuchagua mgonjwa asiye sahihi au kusoma dozi vibaya, hivyo wabunifu hutumia kanuni za mambo ya kibinadamu na hufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu.
- Watumiaji wengi ni wagonjwa, wana wasiwasi, ni wazee au walemavu, hivyo miundo lazima itimize viwango vya ufikivu na ifanye kazi kwa watu wenye ujuzi mdogo wa kidijitali au uelewa mdogo wa masuala ya afya.
- Tahadhari na maonyo yanafaa tu watu wanapoyatambua na kuyaamini, hivyo wabunifu husaidia kupunguza uchovu wa tahadhari na kuweka taarifa muhimu kwa usalama zikionekana.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> An interaction designer works out the best way to let users interact with services, in terms of both overall flow and at the level of individual design elements.

## Daraja 4: Mbunifu msaidizi wa mwingiliano

### Majukumu

- Kujifunza mbinu za usanifu wa mwingiliano na mfumo wa usanifu wa timu kupitia mafunzo na kufanya kazi kwa jozi.

- Kusaidia kujaribu miundo na watumiaji na kurekodi kinachofanya kazi na kisichofanya kazi.

## Daraja 5: Mbunifu mdogo wa mwingiliano

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Junior interaction designer**

> A junior interaction designer is a graduate with a degree in a relevant subject or an individual with some relevant work experience, or both.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - explain design decisions
> - work collaboratively
> - have a responsibility as part of a service
> - work independently after being given direction by more senior designers
> - independently identify user issues and important needs

### Majukumu

- Kubuni na kutengeneza mifano ya sehemu za huduma, kama vile fomu, mtiririko na skrini, kwa uelekezi wa mbunifu mwandamizi zaidi.
- Kujaribu miundo na wagonjwa, walezi na wafanyakazi, na kuibadilisha kulingana na unachojifunza.
- Kubuni mwingiliano unaofikika unaofanya kazi na teknolojia saidizi.
- Kueleza maamuzi ya usanifu kwa timu, ikiwemo jinsi yanavyopunguza uwezekano wa makosa.

## Daraja 6: Mbunifu wa mwingiliano

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Interaction designer**

> An interaction designer is a confident and competent designer who can develop designs based on evidence of user needs and organisational outcomes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be trusted to make good decisions
> - recognise when to ask for further guidance and support
> - contribute to the development of design concepts
> - interpret evidence-based research and incorporate this into your work

### Majukumu

- Kubuni mwingiliano kwa bidhaa ya afya ya kidijitali, kuanzia dhana za awali hadi miundo ya kina iliyojaribiwa.
- Kubuni kwa mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu, akijaribu na madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo katika matukio halisi, ikiwemo kukatizwa.
- Kuibua hatari zinazohusiana na usanifu kwa afisa wa usalama wa kitabibu na kuandika jinsi miundo inavyozidhibiti.
- Kutumia na kuchangia mfumo wa usanifu, ikiwemo mifumo ya taarifa zinazohusu usalama.
- Kufanya kazi kwa karibu na waendelezaji kuhakikisha miundo inajengwa kama ilivyokusudiwa na inabaki kufikika.

## Daraja 7: Mbunifu mwandamizi wa mwingiliano

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior interaction designer**

> A senior interaction designer is an experienced designer who works with minimal support and can influence and mentor others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with service managers and programme directors to develop design concepts
> - potentially have responsibility across complex services
> - help set direction and embed good practice within teams
> - make important decisions based on research and understand how this research impacts others

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa mwingiliano kwa huduma tata, kama vile mifumo ya kitabibu inayotumika katika mazingira mengi ya matunzo.

## Daraja 8b: Mbunifu kiongozi wa mwingiliano

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead interaction designer**

> A lead interaction designer is an expert practitioner who influences and mentors others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with service managers and programme directors to develop design concepts
> - set direction and assure the quality of design delivery across teams
> - lead multiple or highly complex services

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mwelekeo wa usanifu na kuhakikisha ubora wa usanifu wa mwingiliano katika timu au huduma kadhaa.
- Kuongoza mfumo wa usanifu, ikiwemo mifumo inayofikika na inayohusu usalama ambayo timu za taasisi nzima huitumia tena.
- Kuongoza kazi ya mambo ya kibinadamu kwenye huduma za kitabibu zenye hatari kubwa zaidi, pamoja na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kufanya kazi na viongozi waandamizi wa bidhaa na wa kitabibu kuendeleza dhana za usanifu kwa huduma kubwa.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja na kuendeleza wabunifu wa mwingiliano.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa usanifu wa mwingiliano

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of interaction design**

> A head of interaction design is an expert practitioner with broad industry experience, who can define and assure best practice while influencing, leading and mentoring others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - influence both design and organisational strategy and priorities
> - collaborate with counterpart colleagues across government
> - focus on ensuring the right conditions and environment for designers to work effectively

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza taaluma ya usanifu wa mwingiliano na kuweka mkakati, viwango na njia zake za kufanya kazi.
- Kushawishi mkakati wa usanifu na wa taasisi ili huduma ziweze kutumika, zifikike na ziwe salama.
- Kumiliki mfumo wa usanifu wa taasisi na kuhakikisha unatimiza mahitaji ya ufikivu na usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kujenga uwezo wa usanifu, ikiwemo uajiri, njia za taaluma na mafunzo.
- Kufanya kazi na wenzake katika mfumo wa afya na matunzo kuhusu mifumo ya usanifu inayoshirikiwa.
