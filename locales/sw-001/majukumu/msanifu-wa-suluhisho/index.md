# Msanifu wa suluhisho

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 7, 8a, 8b, 8c, 8d  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Solution architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/solution-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT system architect](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/e1c72b5f-4c5c-487c-a6df-e84b64a51dae) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa suluhisho husanifu jinsi huduma mpya au iliyobadilishwa itakavyofanya kazi kutoka mwanzo hadi mwisho, kuanzia mahitaji ya watumiaji na mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu hadi programu, data, miunganisho, uhifadhi na usalama. Huhakikisha kila suluhisho linalingana na usanifu wa taasisi, linatimiza mahitaji yake ya usalama, faragha na ushirikiano wa mifumo, na linaweza kujengwa, kuendeshwa na kusaidiwa kwa gharama nafuu.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Suluhisho mara nyingi huchanganya mifumo ya kitabibu iliyonunuliwa, huduma za ndani na majukwaa yanayoshirikiwa, hivyo wasanifu lazima wabuni kuvuka mipaka ya wasambazaji.
- Uchaguzi wa usanifu unaweza kuleta hatari za kitabibu, kama vile matokeo yaliyopotea au ulinganishaji usio sahihi wa wagonjwa, hivyo miundo ya suluhisho huingia kwenye hoja ya usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kila mtiririko mpya wa data za wagonjwa unahitaji msingi halali na tathmini ya athari za ulinzi wa data, hivyo wasanifu hufanya kazi kwa karibu na usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Ushirikiano wa mifumo na taasisi nyingine za afya na matunzo, kwa kutumia viwango kama HL7 FHIR, kwa kawaida ni hitaji kuu.
- Baadhi ya suluhisho hujumuisha programu ambayo ni kifaa tiba, jambo linalobadilisha mahitaji ya usanifu, uhakikisho na wasambazaji.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A solution architect designs solutions for problems that affect the organisation.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - ensure a problem and the desired outcomes are properly defined
> - ensure the scope of a solution meets the organisation's requirements
> - stay up to date on technology trends and approaches
> - understand organisational objectives and external drivers, for example, legislation or financial constraints
> - work with others to develop business and technical strategies
> - work within business and technical constraints
> - design and document solutions so they can be implemented by the organisation
> - comply with standards and governance
> - communicate and work effectively with stakeholders
> - manage risks and decisions in a transparent way

## Daraja 7: Msanifu msaidizi wa suluhisho

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate solution architect**

> An associate solution architect supports other solution architects to design solutions. You will usually work under supervision.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - support the design of solutions by working with stakeholders
> - help your team achieve its objectives

### Majukumu

- Kukusanya mahitaji kutoka kwa timu za bidhaa, madaktari na wasambazaji ili kuongoza miundo ya suluhisho.
- Kuchora michoro ya hali ya sasa na lengwa, kama vile mitazamo ya muktadha, mtiririko wa data na uunganishaji.
- Kuandaa rasimu za sehemu za miundo ya suluhisho na kumbukumbu za maamuzi chini ya usimamizi.
- Kukagua miundo dhidi ya viwango vya usanifu na mahitaji ya usalama na usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Kusaidia kuandaa miundo kwa ukaguzi katika mamlaka za usanifu.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Maarifa maalumu ya usanifu wa suluhisho, katika kiwango cha shahada ya uzamili au uzoefu unaolingana.
- Uzoefu wa uchambuzi, uendelezaji, au usanifu wa kiufundi.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi maalumu uliokuzwa sana, kwa kawaida hadi kiwango cha shahada ya uzamili au uzoefu unaolingana.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Hufanya kazi kwa sera ya taasisi; huamua jinsi matokeo yanavyofikiwa; ndiye mtaalamu wanayemshauriana naye wengine.
- **Wigo:** Bidhaa au huduma kadhaa, au kitengo maalumu.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza timu au eneo la mazoezi ya kitaaluma.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utoaji wa huduma au kitengo maalumu, na bajeti yake ikiwa anaisimamia.

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu wa suluhisho

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Solution architect**

> A solution architect is responsible for a single solution. They usually work independently on solutions where risk is low. They also often support or contribute to work led by more senior solution architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build relationships with stakeholders across different business or technical areas in the organisation
> - be proactive in identifying opportunities to improve the organisation
> - follow best practice for solution design
> - use emerging technologies and approaches
> - help your team achieve its objective

### Majukumu

- Kubuni suluhisho za mwanzo hadi mwisho kwa huduma zenye hatari ndogo, kama vile zana za wafanyakazi au huduma rahisi zinazowalenga wagonjwa.
- Kulinganisha chaguzi, ikiwemo kununua, kujenga na kutumia tena majukwaa yanayoshirikiwa, na kupendekeza moja kwa sababu wazi.
- Kubuni miunganisho, mtiririko wa data, na utambulisho na ufikiaji wa suluhisho, akitumia viwango huria vinapokuwepo.
- Kuchangia utambuzi wa hatari na tathmini za athari za ulinzi wa data kwa suluhisho.
- Kuwasilisha miundo kwa mamlaka za usanifu na kuzisaidia timu za utoaji kupitia ujenzi na kuanza kutumika.
- Kuwaongoza na kukagua kazi ya wasanifu wasaidizi wa suluhisho.

## Daraja 8b: Msanifu mwandamizi wa suluhisho

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior solution architect**

> A senior solution architect is responsible for a single, more complex, solution. They usually work on solutions where the risk is moderate. They may lead and coach other solution architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build relationships with senior stakeholders across different business or technical areas in the organisation
> - be proactive in identifying opportunities to improve the organisation
> - support multiple architecture projects
> - find and use emerging technologies and approaches
> - support others to follow best practice for solution architecture

### Majukumu

- Kubuni suluhisho tata zenye hatari ya wastani, kama vile mifumo ya kitabibu au huduma zinazotumika katika taasisi kadhaa.
- Kuongoza tathmini ya kiufundi ya bidhaa za wasambazaji katika ununuzi, ikiwemo ushirikiano wa mifumo, usalama na usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kubuni kwa ustahimilivu na mwendelezo wa biashara pale suluhisho linapounga mkono matunzo saa nzima.
- Kufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu ili maamuzi ya usanifu na udhibiti wa hatari virekodiwe katika hoja ya usalama.
- Kuwaongoza na kukagua kazi ya wasanifu wengine wa suluhisho.

## Daraja 8c: Msanifu kiongozi wa suluhisho

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead solution architect**

> A lead solution architect is responsible for a group of solution architecture projects, or a single more complex area. They may lead teams of more junior solution architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build relationships with senior stakeholders across multiple business or technical areas in the organisation
> - be proactive in identifying opportunities to improve the organisation
> - support multiple architecture projects or programmes
> - find and use emerging technologies and approaches
> - develop best practice for solution architecture

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa suluhisho kwa programu au eneo tata, kama vile kubadilisha rekodi ya mgonjwa ya kielektroniki.
- Kuhakikisha suluhisho katika programu zinaendana, zinashirikiana na kuoanishwa na usanifu lengwa.
- Kuzishauri bodi za programu kuhusu hatari za usanifu, utegemezi, uchaguzi wa wasambazaji na gharama.
- Kuhakikisha miundo ya wasanifu wengine wa suluhisho katika mamlaka za usanifu.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza timu ya wasanifu wa suluhisho.

## Daraja 8d: Msanifu mkuu wa suluhisho

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal solution architect**

> A principal solution architect can be responsible for a large programme or group of solution architecture projects, or a single, very complex or critical business area.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead teams of more junior solution architects
> - lead multiple architecture projects or programmes
> - build relationships with senior stakeholders across multiple business or technical areas in the organisation and its partners
> - be proactive in identifying opportunities to improve the organisation and its partners
> - work with technology partners to inform their roadmaps
> - take a leading role in the overall direction of business and digital capabilities
> - inspire other architects and help them understand how to meet organisational goals

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa suluhisho kwa programu kubwa zaidi au muhimu zaidi za taasisi.
- Kuweka viwango, mifumo na mbinu za usanifu wa suluhisho kwa taasisi.
- Kuwashauri wakurugenzi na taasisi washirika kuhusu usanifu wa huduma muhimu za afya na matunzo zinazoshirikiwa.
- Kutatua migongano migumu zaidi ya usanifu kati ya programu, wasambazaji na taasisi washirika.
- Kuongoza mazoezi ya usanifu wa suluhisho na kuendeleza watu wake.
