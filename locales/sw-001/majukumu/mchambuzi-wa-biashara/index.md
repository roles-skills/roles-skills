# Mchambuzi wa biashara

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Bidhaa na utoaji](../../#bidhaa-na-utoaji)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8a, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Business analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/business-analyst/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [business analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/60082a99-d8ef-4e84-9290-78902681b6ed) (ISCO-08 2421); [ICT business analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/fe0fa514-b48d-4a53-9757-a283c0baacf0) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wachambuzi wa biashara husaidia taasisi kuelewa matatizo katika huduma za afya na matunzo na kupata mabadiliko sahihi ya kuyatatua. Huchambua jinsi michakato ya kitabibu, ya kiutawala na inayowalenga wagonjwa inavyofanya kazi leo, hufafanua mahitaji ya watumiaji na ya biashara, na kusaidia timu kuhakikisha huduma mpya za kidijitali zinalingana na njia halisi za matunzo na kuleta manufaa yanayopimika.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Michakato huvuka mipaka ya taasisi na taaluma, hivyo wachambuzi huchora ramani za njia nzima za matunzo, ikiwemo makabidhiano kati ya madaktari, wafanyakazi wa matunzo, wagonjwa na taasisi nyingine.
- Mahitaji mara nyingi hubeba hatari za kitabibu, hivyo wachambuzi hufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kuhakikisha mahitaji yanayohusu usalama yananaswa, yanafuatiliwa na kujaribiwa.
- Mahitaji mengi yanahusu kushirikisha data za afya kati ya mifumo, hivyo wachambuzi wanahitaji uelewa wa vitendo wa viwango vya ushirikiano wa mifumo kama HL7 FHIR na istilahi za kitabibu kama SNOMED CT.
- Manufaa katika matunzo mara nyingi yanahusu usalama, ubora, muda wa wafanyakazi na uzoefu wa wagonjwa, si gharama tu, hivyo wachambuzi husaidia kufafanua vipimo ambavyo madaktari wanavitambua.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Business analysts help teams to:
> - analyse and understand a business problem or opportunity
> - undertake research and analysis to understand how a business or business area works, considering the people, organisation, processes, information, data and technology
> - identify areas for improvement, explore feasible options, analyse the effects of change and define success measures
> - identify and elaborate user and business needs to enable effective design, development and testing of services and business change
> - make decisions related to prioritisation and minimum viable product by using analysis led insights
> - ensure new products and services meet business and user needs, and are aligned with organisational goals
> - understand any business and policy constraints that need to be considered, and assess the implications

## Daraja 4: Mchambuzi wa biashara mwanafunzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Trainee business analyst**

> A trainee business analyst receives direction from more senior business analysts. At this level, you will work with others or under supervision to build and develop your skills.

- Kujifunza mbinu za uchambuzi wa biashara kupitia mafunzo yaliyopangwa, kuwafuata wenye uzoefu na kazi chini ya usimamizi.
- Kuandika maelezo na kuandaa rasimu za ramani za michakato kutoka kwenye warsha na wafanyakazi wa kitabibu na wa kiutawala.
- Kutunza kumbukumbu za mahitaji, hadithi za watumiaji na rekodi za ufuatiliaji chini ya uelekezi.
- Kusaidia kuandaa matukio ya majaribio kwa majaribio ya ukubalifu wa watumiaji.
- Kufuata kanuni za usimamizi wa taarifa anaposhughulikia nyaraka zenye taarifa za wagonjwa.

- **Uwajibikaji:** Utoaji wa huduma au mchakato uliobainishwa.

## Daraja 5: Mchambuzi mdogo wa biashara

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Junior business analyst**

> A junior business analyst receives direction from more senior business analysts but is responsible for the output of specific tasks. At this level, you will have limited skills and will work with others or under supervision. You will support stakeholder relationship management.

### Majukumu

- Kuchora ramani za michakato ya sasa na ya baadaye ya mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu na za kiutawala, kama vile rufaa, miadi, au ushughulikiaji wa matokeo ya vipimo.

- Kukusanya data za utendaji wa michakato, kama vile muda wa kusubiri au njia mbadala za mikono, ili kusaidia uchambuzi.
- Kuandaa na kuendesha matukio ya majaribio ya ukubalifu wa watumiaji pamoja na watumiaji wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.

## Daraja 6: Mchambuzi wa biashara

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Business analyst**

> A business analyst leads small to medium size projects and supports larger and more complex projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage stakeholder relationships
> - work independently
> - have a good understanding of your work area
> - develop the business analysis community by sharing best practice and mentoring others

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza uchambuzi wa miradi midogo na ya kati, kuanzia ufafanuzi wa tatizo hadi kupima manufaa.
- Kuendesha warsha na madaktari, wafanyakazi wa matunzo na mameneja kuelewa kero na kukubaliana chaguzi.
- Kuunda miundo ya njia za matunzo na mtiririko wa taarifa, ikiwemo data zinazobadilishana na mifumo mingine ya afya na matunzo.
- Kufafanua mahitaji yanayohusu usalama na data, na kuyafuatilia kupitia usanifu, ujenzi na majaribio.
- Kujenga hoja za manufaa kwa vipimo kama vile muda wa wafanyakazi uliookolewa, makosa yaliyoepukwa, au uzoefu wa wagonjwa.
- Kuwa mshauri wa wachambuzi wadogo wa biashara na wanafunzi.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Uzoefu mpana wa kuongoza kitengo au mazoezi ya uchambuzi wa biashara.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi wa kitaalamu, pamoja na uelewa mpana wa taasisi na sekta.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Huweka mkakati wa kitengo; anawajibika kwa mkurugenzi.
- **Wigo:** Kitengo au idara.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza kitengo kupitia ngazi kadhaa za usimamizi.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utendaji wa kitengo, nguvu kazi yake, na bajeti yake.
