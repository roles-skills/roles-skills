# Mbunifu wa huduma

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu unaomlenga mtumiaji](../../#usanifu-unaomlenga-mtumiaji)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Service designer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/service-designer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [user experience analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/faed411a-f920-4100-86a8-b877928b429c) (ISCO-08 2511); [user interface designer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/96e20037-0a25-4bf6-a25e-808d1605d890) (ISCO-08 2513)

## Muhtasari

Wabunifu wa huduma husanifu huduma za afya na matunzo kutoka mwanzo hadi mwisho, kuanzia pale mtu anapohitaji msaada kwa mara ya kwanza hadi mwisho wa matunzo yake, kupitia njia za kidijitali, simu, karatasi na ana kwa ana. Hufanya kazi na wagonjwa, walezi, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo kuchora ramani ya jinsi huduma zinavyofanya kazi kikweli, kupata mahali zinapowakosea watu, na kubuni huduma salama, rahisi na za haki zinazofanya kazi kuvuka mipaka ya taasisi.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Njia za matunzo huvuka taasisi na taaluma nyingi, hivyo wabunifu wa huduma huchora ramani za safari nzima, ikiwemo makabidhiano, kusubiri, na kazi ambayo wagonjwa na walezi hufanya kushikilia matunzo pamoja.
- Mabadiliko ya jinsi huduma inavyofanya kazi yanaweza kuunda hatari mpya za kitabibu, hivyo wabunifu wa huduma hufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kutambua hatari katika miundo mipya ya huduma kabla haijaanza kutumika.
- Huduma za kidijitali hazipaswi kuwatenga watu wasioweza au wasiotaka kuzitumia, hivyo wabunifu wa huduma husanifu njia zinazosaidiwa na zisizo za kidijitali pamoja na za kidijitali.
- Wafanyakazi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji ni sehemu ya huduma, hivyo miundo lazima ilingane na mtiririko wao wa kazi, mzigo wa kazi na wajibu wa kitaaluma, si safari ya mgonjwa tu.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Service designers design the end-to-end journey of a service. This helps a user complete their goal and government deliver a policy intent. In this role, your work may involve the creation of, or change to, transactions, products and content across both digital and offline channels provided by different parts of government.

## Daraja 6: Mbunifu wa huduma

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Service designer**

> A service designer is a confident and competent designer who can develop designs based on evidence of user needs and organisational outcomes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be trusted to make good decisions
> - recognise when to ask for further guidance and support
> - contribute to the development of design concepts
> - interpret evidence-based research and incorporate this into your work

### Majukumu

- Kubuni huduma za mwanzo hadi mwisho katika njia mbalimbali, ikiwemo njia zinazosaidiwa na zisizo za kidijitali kwa watu wanaozihitaji.
- Kuchora ramani za njia za matunzo katika taasisi na kubuni makabidhiano bora kati ya timu na huduma.
- Kubuni jinsi huduma inavyofanya kazi kwa wafanyakazi, ikiwemo nafasi, mzigo wa kazi na mtiririko wa kazi, pamoja na viongozi wa kiutendaji.
- Kufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kutambua hatari katika miundo mipya ya huduma na kubuni udhibiti.
- Kutengeneza mifano na kujaribu mabadiliko ya huduma na wagonjwa, walezi na wafanyakazi.

## Daraja 7: Mbunifu mwandamizi wa huduma

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior service designer**

> A senior service designer is an experienced designer who works with minimal support and can influence and mentor others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with service managers and programme directors to develop design concepts
> - potentially have responsibility across complex services
> - help set direction and embed good practice within teams
> - make important decisions based on research and understand how this research impacts others

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa huduma tata zinazovuka mazingira au taasisi kadhaa za matunzo.
- Kuunda maono ya huduma pamoja na viongozi wa kitabibu, wa kiutendaji na wa kidijitali.
- Kubuni miundo mipya ya huduma inayoboresha usalama, ufikiaji na usawa, na kuijaribu na watumiaji halisi.
- Kuhakikisha mabadiliko ya huduma yanatathminiwa kwa athari za hatari ya kitabibu, usawa na usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Kuwa mshauri wa wabunifu na kuingiza mazoezi ya usanifu wa huduma katika timu.

## Daraja 8b: Mbunifu kiongozi wa huduma

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead service designer**

> A lead service designer is an expert practitioner who influences and mentors others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with service managers and programme directors to develop design concepts
> - set direction and assure the quality of design delivery across teams
> - lead multiple or highly complex services

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mwelekeo wa usanifu wa huduma kadhaa au njia nzima ya matunzo, na kuhakikisha ubora wa usanifu katika timu.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa usanifu wa huduma

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of service design**

> A head of service design is an expert practitioner with broad industry experience, who can define and assure best practice while influencing, leading and mentoring others.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - influence both design and organisational strategy and priorities
> - collaborate with counterpart colleagues across government
> - focus on ensuring the right conditions and environment for designers to work effectively

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza taaluma ya usanifu wa huduma na kuweka mkakati, viwango na njia zake za kufanya kazi.

- Kujenga uwezo wa usanifu wa huduma, ikiwemo uajiri, njia za taaluma na mafunzo.
- Kuunda mazingira ya usanifu mzuri, kama vile ufikiaji wa watumiaji, muda wa utafiti na usanifu katika usimamizi.
- Kufanya kazi na wenzake katika mfumo wa afya na matunzo kubuni huduma zilizounganishwa.
