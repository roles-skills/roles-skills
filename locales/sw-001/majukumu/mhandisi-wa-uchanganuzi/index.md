# Mhandisi wa uchanganuzi

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 6, 7, 8a, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Analytics engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/analytics-engineer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [data engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2079755f-d809-49e6-8037-4de6180e54c0) (ISCO-08 2511); [business intelligence manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/207d7b18-6540-432e-8aa6-785ed434572f) (ISCO-08 2421)

## Muhtasari

Wahandisi wa uchanganuzi hugeuza data ghafi za afya na matunzo kuwa miundo safi ya data, iliyojaribiwa na kuandikwa ambayo wachambuzi, wanasayansi wa data na zana za kuripoti wanaweza kuitegemea. Wako kati ya uhandisi wa data na uchambuzi, wakitumia mazoezi ya uhandisi wa programu kama vile udhibiti wa matoleo, majaribio na ukaguzi wa msimbo katika mabadiliko yaliyo nyuma ya ripoti na dashibodi za taasisi.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Miundo ya data hubeba fasili za kitabibu na za kiutendaji, kama vile kinachohesabiwa kuwa kulazwa, kulazwa tena, au kukiuka kiwango cha muda wa kusubiri, hivyo fasili hukubaliwa na viongozi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.
- Misimbo ya kitabibu kutoka istilahi na uainishaji kama SNOMED CT na ICD hupangwa katika seti za marejeleo kwa ajili ya uchambuzi, ambazo zinahitaji udhibiti makini wa matoleo.
- Miundo inayotumika kwa ripoti za utendaji na ufadhili lazima iwe thabiti na inayoweza kukaguliwa, kwa sababu makosa yanaweza kuathiri maamuzi kuhusu huduma za wagonjwa.
- Miundo mara nyingi hushikilia data za kiwango cha mgonjwa zenye majina bandia, hivyo ufikiaji, uandishi wa nyaraka na upunguzaji wa data huingizwa ndani yake.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> An analytics engineer transforms data relevant to the organisation into structures that enable analysis and decision-making.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - work with subject matter experts to understand organisational processes and translate these into data structures optimised for analysis
> - work with data users to design and build data models they can use for effective analysis and decision-making, using modelling techniques such as Kimball or Inmon
> - support data quality improvement
> - develop standards for data transformation
> - create and maintain data documentation
> - refine requirements in response to feedback from users and changes in the organisation
> - provide ongoing support to users

## Daraja 4: Mhandisi wa uchanganuzi mwanafunzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Trainee analytics engineer**

> A trainee analytics engineer attends training and develops skills on the job.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - spend time shadowing other analytics engineers
> - build your knowledge of the organisation
> - learn skills for managing data
> - learn to use different applications, tools, templates and best practices
> - handle simple queries from users and document their data requirements
> - contribute to data documentation and user training

### Majukumu

- Kujifunza zana na mazoezi ya timu ya kuunda miundo ya data kupitia mafunzo na kufanya kazi kwa jozi.
- Kufanya mabadiliko madogo yaliyobainishwa vizuri kwenye miundo ya data chini ya usimamizi.
- Kuandika vipengee vya data na fasili zake katika katalogi ya data.
- Kujibu maswali rahisi kutoka kwa watumiaji wa data na kurekodi mahitaji yao.
- Kufuata kanuni za usimamizi wa taarifa anapofanya kazi na data za wagonjwa.

## Daraja 6: Mhandisi wa uchanganuzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Analytics engineer**

> An analytics engineer develops and tests data models assigned by more senior analytics engineers to help people in a defined area access and use data.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - draft documentation of data that meets standards
> - work with other analytics engineers to resolve issues and risks
> - support trainee analytics engineers
> - provide training and support for users of data sets
> - work with more experienced analytics engineers to develop your skills

### Majukumu

- Kujenga na kujaribu miundo ya data kwa eneo la huduma, kama vile shughuli za wagonjwa waliolazwa au matunzo ya jamii, kutoka kwenye miundo iliyokubaliwa.
- Kuandika majaribio ya kiotomatiki yanayokagua miundo ya data dhidi ya fasili zilizokubaliwa za kitabibu na za kiutendaji.
- Kutunza seti za marejeleo za misimbo ya kitabibu zinazotumika katika uchambuzi, zenye matoleo wazi.
- Kuandika miundo ili wachambuzi waelewe maana yake, vyanzo na mipaka yake.
- Kuwafunza na kuwasaidia watumiaji wa seti za data za timu.

## Daraja 7: Mhandisi mwandamizi wa uchanganuzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior analytics engineer**

> A senior analytics engineer oversees the development and testing of data models, including directing the work of other analytics engineers.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - oversee tasks and support less senior analytics engineers
> - build relationships with stakeholders within a defined area
> - coach and mentor less senior analytics engineers
> - ensure documentation of data meets standards
> - ensure issues and risks are resolved
> - oversee training and support for users of data sets
> - explore and develop new ways of working with data

### Majukumu

- Kusimamia usanifu na majaribio ya miundo ya data katika maeneo kadhaa ya huduma.
- Kukubaliana fasili za vipimo muhimu na viongozi wa kitabibu, wa kiutendaji na wa fedha, na kuhakikisha miundo inazitumia kwa uthabiti.
- Kuhakikisha miundo inayolisha ripoti za utendaji na ufadhili inaweza kukaguliwa na imejaribiwa vizuri.
- Kutatua masuala na hatari za data pamoja na wahandisi wa data na wamiliki wa data.
- Kuwaongoza na kuelekeza kazi ya wahandisi wengine wa uchanganuzi.

## Daraja 8a: Mhandisi kiongozi wa uchanganuzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead analytics engineer**

> A lead analytics engineer leads the design and deployment of data models for analysis.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead and support a team of analytics engineers to design, build and maintain data models
> - work with stakeholders and teams across the organisation to understand relationships between data and organisational processes, and use this to define data requirements
> - promote, build awareness and support understanding of analytics engineering
> - review the work of other analytics engineers
> - create standards for communication, data models and documentation
> - define and improve ways of working in the team

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza timu inayobuni, kujenga na kutunza miundo ya data ya uchambuzi ya taasisi.
- Kuweka viwango vya miundo ya data, majaribio, uandishi wa nyaraka na fasili za vipimo.
- Kufanya kazi na timu katika taasisi nzima kuelewa jinsi michakato ya kitabibu na ya kiutendaji inavyozalisha data.
- Kukagua kazi ya wahandisi wengine wa uchanganuzi na kuboresha njia za kufanya kazi za timu.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja wahandisi wa uchanganuzi.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa uhandisi wa uchanganuzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of analytics engineering**

> A head of analytics engineering oversees the development of a range of data models that meet analysis needs across the organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build relationships with senior stakeholders across the organisation to determine what the organisation needs
> - ensure the activities of the analytics engineering team align with strategic priorities
> - ensure the team works to standards for communication, data models and documentation
> - advocate for the analytics engineering role to senior leadership and other organisations
> - build analytics engineering capability by providing technical leadership and career development for the community
> - ensure appropriate technology is available for the community

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mwelekeo wa miundo ya data ya uchambuzi ya taasisi, ili uchambuzi katika taasisi nzima utumie seti moja ya fasili inayoaminika.
- Kuhakikisha vipaumbele vya uhandisi wa uchanganuzi vinaunga mkono mkakati wa taasisi na wajibu wake wa kuripoti.
- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu uaminifu wa data zilizo nyuma ya ripoti na maamuzi makubwa.
- Kuhakikisha jumuiya ina zana na majukwaa inayohitaji, na kusimamia bajeti inayohusiana.
- Kujenga uwezo wa uhandisi wa uchanganuzi kupitia uongozi wa kiufundi na maendeleo ya taaluma.
