# Msanifu wa biashara

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Business architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/business-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [enterprise architect](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2bef94db-0088-4507-982a-2ca717529adb) (ISCO-08 2511); [ICT business analysis manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/4b3201cb-ffc5-4f61-9fc3-5c0f0038fa5f) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa biashara hujenga mtazamo ulio wazi na ulioungana wa jinsi taasisi inavyotoa huduma za afya na matunzo, yaani uwezo wake, huduma, njia za matunzo, michakato na taarifa. Hutumia mtazamo huo kuonyesha mahali mabadiliko yanapohitajika, kuunganisha mkakati na programu, na kuhakikisha mabadiliko ya kidijitali yanaboresha matunzo kwa wagonjwa na maisha ya kazi ya madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Njia za matunzo huvuka timu, mazingira na taasisi, hivyo wasanifu wa biashara huunda miundo ya huduma kutoka mtazamo wa mgonjwa, si kwa idara tu.
- Mabadiliko katika michakato ya kitabibu yanaweza kuathiri usalama wa mgonjwa na mzigo wa kazi wa wafanyakazi, hivyo miundo hujengwa pamoja na madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo na kukaguliwa dhidi ya mazoezi halisi.
- Mkakati wa afya na matunzo huundwa na sera, udhibiti na kanuni za ufadhili, ambazo wasanifu lazima wazigeuze kuwa mabadiliko ya vitendo ya uwezo.
- Uwezo mwingi hushirikiwa na taasisi washirika, hivyo wasanifu husaidia kukubaliana nani anafanya nini na mahali ambapo taarifa lazima zitiririke.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A business architect develops an integrated view of an enterprise, such as a business area or organisation. They use this view to help their organisation put its strategy in place.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - understand and apply business architectural principles, techniques, and tools to business challenges
> - ensure design work from projects and programmes aligns with strategy
> - ensure business change is implemented effectively to meet strategy
> - aggregate and disaggregate complex information from across your organisation
> - communicate complex information about the organisation to stakeholders

## Daraja 6: Msanifu wa biashara mwanafunzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Trainee business architect**

> A trainee business architect supports the more senior business architect roles. They also receive training on the tasks they support so they can work on their own.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - develop your knowledge of the frameworks, practice, and principles of business architecture
> - work with other business architects to support the production of clear business architecture outputs
> - support during workshops and meetings with stakeholders in the organisation

### Majukumu

- Kujifunza mbinu za usanifu wa biashara, kama vile uundaji wa miundo ya uwezo, mnyororo wa thamani na michakato, kupitia mafunzo na mazoezi.

- Kuandaa rasimu za ramani za uwezo na miundo ya michakato kwa mwongozo wa wasanifu wa biashara.
- Kusaidia kuendesha warsha na madaktari, wafanyakazi wa matunzo na mameneja, na kuandika matokeo.

## Daraja 7: Msanifu msaidizi wa biashara

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate business architect**

> An associate business architect uses their knowledge and experience to address business scenarios. They continue to get support and training for their role.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - apply architectural methods following direction and wider strategy to produce outputs
> - break down complexity and produce clear outputs
> - explain complex business problems using clear language and artefacts
> - bring together disconnected information to inform decision making
> - recognise issues that cut across different areas of the organisation and present opportunities for improvement

### Majukumu

- Kuunda miundo ya uwezo, huduma na njia za matunzo kwa sehemu ya taasisi.
- Kuchambua miundo kupata mapengo, urudufu na kero kwa wagonjwa na wafanyakazi.
- Kuleta pamoja taarifa kutoka timu na taasisi tofauti ili kuongoza maamuzi ya mabadiliko.
- Kueleza matatizo tata ya huduma kwa wadau kwa kutumia miundo iliyo wazi na lugha rahisi.
- Kuweka hazina ya usanifu wa biashara ikiwa sahihi na iliyounganishwa na miundo ya programu na data.

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu wa biashara

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Business architect**

> A business architect uses their knowledge and experience to understand business scenarios, communicate the issues and recommend next steps. They support and guide other business architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - support executive level work and management of the business architecture practice
> - guide others in using appropriate business architectural tools and methods
> - understand and apply business architectural principles, techniques, and tools to business scenarios
> - create business architecture artefacts, including abstract representations of the organisation, knowing when to apply them
> - develop standard and scalable models to collect, aggregate, or disaggregate complex information

### Majukumu

- Kuendeleza usanifu wa sasa na lengwa wa biashara kwa eneo kubwa la huduma, kama vile huduma za dharura au huduma za jamii.
- Kugeuza mkakati na sera kuwa mabadiliko ya uwezo na ramani ya mabadiliko.
- Kukagua kama wigo wa programu na mradi unalingana na usanifu lengwa wa biashara.
- Kufanya kazi na viongozi wa kitabibu kutathmini jinsi mabadiliko yanayopendekezwa yanavyoathiri njia za matunzo, usalama na mzigo wa kazi wa wafanyakazi.
- Kuwaongoza wasanifu wengine wa biashara katika mbinu na zana.

## Daraja 8b: Msanifu kiongozi wa biashara

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead business architect**

> A lead business architect uses their expert knowledge and experience to direct the work addressing high level and complex business scenarios. They support and lead other business architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be accountable for outputs produced by the business architecture team
> - lead and direct the application of architectural principles, methods, and tools to business scenarios
> - use architectural methods to form a view of the whole enterprise in a way that is easily understood by stakeholders
> - work with stakeholders at executive levels
> - provide overall direction, guidance, and definition of business architecture to support strategy
> - develop and maintain a strong business architecture capability through coaching, mentoring, and training

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza mazoezi ya usanifu wa biashara na kuwajibika kwa matokeo yake.
- Kutunza mtazamo wa taasisi nzima wa uwezo, huduma na njia za matunzo, na kuufanya uwe rahisi kueleweka.
- Kuwashauri wakurugenzi kuhusu vipaumbele vya mabadiliko na jinsi majalada ya uwekezaji yanavyolingana na mkakati.
- Kufanya kazi na taasisi washirika kukubaliana uwezo na majukumu ya pamoja katika njia za matunzo.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza wasanifu wa biashara.
