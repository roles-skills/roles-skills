# Meneja wa huduma za TEHAMA

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Uendeshaji wa TEHAMA](../../#uendeshaji-wa-tehama)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 6, 7, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [IT service manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/it-service-manager/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT operations manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/f0ca39a8-1e30-487c-95f9-ef2d5e734adc) (ISCO-08 1330)

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa huduma za TEHAMA huhakikisha huduma za kidijitali za taasisi zinatolewa kwa viwango vilivyokubaliwa na zinaendelea kuboreka. Hufafanua viwango vya huduma, husimamia katalogi ya huduma, huripoti utendaji, na kuratibu timu na wasambazaji wanaoendesha kila huduma. Katika huduma za afya, hugeuza yale huduma za kitabibu zinayohitaji, kama vile upatikanaji saa nzima, kuwa malengo ya huduma na mipango ya msaada.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Viwango vya huduma kwa mifumo ya kitabibu vinaakisi hatari ya kitabibu, hivyo malengo ya rekodi ya mgonjwa ya kielektroniki au uandikaji wa dawa hutofautiana na yale ya mfumo wa kampuni.
- Huduma mara nyingi hutolewa na mchanganyiko wa timu za ndani, wasambazaji na huduma zinazoshirikiwa na taasisi washirika, hivyo mameneja wa huduma huratibu kuvuka mipaka ya taasisi.
- Mwendelezo wa biashara wa kitabibu hutegemea TEHAMA, hivyo mameneja wa huduma hufanya kazi na viongozi wa kitabibu na wa mipango ya dharura kuhusu mipango ya wakati wa mfumo kutopatikana na mwendelezo.
- Wafanyakazi wa kitabibu wana muda mdogo wa kuripoti matatizo, hivyo mameneja wa huduma hutumia ufuatiliaji, data za dawati la huduma na maoni ya kitabibu kugundua masuala mapema.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> IT service managers are responsible for managing the service delivery of information and communications technology (ICT) services, and working with teams from IT service operations.

## Daraja 4: Mchambuzi wa huduma za TEHAMA

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: IT service analyst**

> An IT service analyst is responsible for frontline technical support to end users on issues relating to hardware, software and peripherals. This will include responding to, documenting and resolving service desk incident tickets in a timely manner according to a service level agreement (SLA).
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have excellent problem solving skills to diagnose, evaluate and resolve complex problem situations or, when appropriate, escalate or route them to IT resolver groups
> - perform root cause analysis
> - develop checklists for typical problems
> - recommend procedures and controls for problem prevention
> - work independently, share information and assist others with requests for service

### Majukumu

- Kujibu, kuandika na kutatua matukio na maombi ndani ya viwango vya huduma vilivyokubaliwa.
- Kutoa ripoti za mara kwa mara za huduma, kama vile idadi ya tiketi, muda wa kujibu na upatikanaji wa mifumo ya kitabibu.
- Kukagua kama maingizo ya katalogi ya huduma na makala za maarifa ni sahihi na ya kisasa.
- Kutafuta mifumo katika matukio na kupendekeza maboresho.
- Kufuata kanuni za usimamizi wa taarifa anaposhughulikia data za tiketi na huduma.

## Daraja 6: Meneja wa huduma za TEHAMA

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: IT service manager**

> An IT service manager is responsible for defining service level agreements (SLAs) and operational level agreements (OLAs) across all relevant business functions.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage a fully functioning multi-sourced service support team with a customer-centric support approach across multiple locations, focused on service availability and performance
> - ensure that all service and support functions remain responsive to customer needs
> - ensure that the service catalogue and wider IT service operations knowledge library are maintained
> - ensure that adequate reporting and service standards are met for your specific service
> - ensure that all technical change is communicated and managed, with appropriate governance
> - ensure that a programme of continual service improvement is put in place across the wider service delivery function

### Majukumu

- Kufafanua na kukubaliana viwango vya huduma kwa huduma za kitabibu na za kampuni, kulingana na hatari ya kitabibu na hitaji la biashara.
- Kusimamia mipango ya msaada kwa jalada la huduma, ikiwemo wasambazaji na huduma zinazoshirikiwa.
- Kuripoti utendaji wa huduma kwa wamiliki wa huduma na viongozi wa kitabibu, na kukubaliana hatua za uboreshaji.
- Kuhakikisha kila huduma ina mipango iliyojaribiwa ya mwendelezo wa biashara na wakati wa mfumo kutopatikana.
- Kuweka katalogi ya huduma ikiwa ya kisasa, na kuhakikisha wafanyakazi wanaarifiwa kuhusu mabadiliko ya huduma zao.
- Kuendesha programu ya uboreshaji endelevu wa huduma kwa huduma zake.

## Daraja 7: Meneja mwandamizi wa huduma za TEHAMA

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior IT service manager**

> A senior IT service manager is responsible for service management across platforms, multiple channels and services to ensure service availability, performance and process maturity.

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usimamizi wa huduma katika majukwaa na njia kadhaa, na kuboresha ukomavu wa michakato ya usimamizi wa huduma.
- Kukubaliana viwango vya huduma na masharti ya mikataba na wasambazaji wakuu wa mifumo ya kitabibu, na kuwawajibisha.
- Kuwasilisha utendaji wa huduma na hatari kwa viongozi waandamizi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.
- Kuongoza mapitio ya huduma baada ya matukio makubwa, na kufuatilia hatua za uboreshaji hadi zikamilike.
- Kuoanisha mipango ya mwendelezo ya TEHAMA na mipango ya mwendelezo wa biashara ya kitabibu, akifanya kazi na viongozi wa mipango ya dharura na wa kitabibu.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza mameneja wa huduma za TEHAMA.

## Daraja 8b: Mkuu wa usimamizi wa huduma za TEHAMA

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of IT service management**

> A head of IT service management is responsible for setting the vision and strategy for service management, ensuring processes are owned and maturing.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead a team, ensuring adequate resources and capacity
> - represent the IT service management function at a senior level
> - act as an escalation point for business stakeholders

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono, mkakati na mfumo wa uendeshaji wa usimamizi wa huduma za TEHAMA katika taasisi.
- Kuhakikisha kila mchakato wa usimamizi wa huduma una mmiliki na unaendelea kukomaa.
- Kuwakilisha usimamizi wa huduma za TEHAMA katika ngazi ya juu, ikiwemo kwenye mikutano ya usimamizi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.
- Kuwa kituo cha juu cha kupeleka masuala kwa viongozi wa huduma za kitabibu na za kampuni.
- Kuhakikisha kitengo cha usimamizi wa huduma kina watu, zana na bajeti inayohitaji.
