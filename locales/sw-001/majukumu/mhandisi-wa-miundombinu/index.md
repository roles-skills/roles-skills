# Mhandisi wa miundombinu

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Uendeshaji wa TEHAMA](../../#uendeshaji-wa-tehama)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Infrastructure engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/infrastructure-engineer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT system administrator](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/9e2e6e1e-363b-4e1b-a673-7bc0f7343300) (ISCO-08 2522); [ICT network engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/cf2b03cd-feb7-4f47-90f6-ff1ed6016d3d) (ISCO-08 2523); [cloud engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/349ee6f6-c295-4c38-9b98-48765b55280e) (ISCO-08 2512)

## Muhtasari

Wahandisi wa miundombinu husanifu, hujenga, huendesha na kusaidia mitandao, seva, hifadhi, majukwaa ya wingu na uhifadhi ambavyo huduma za kidijitali za taasisi zinavitegemea. Huweka miundombinu hii ikipatikana, salama na yenye kurejeshwa, na hufanya kazi na wasambazaji na wasanifu kuibadilisha kwa usalama. Katika huduma za afya, hitilafu moja ya miundombinu inaweza kusimamisha mifumo ya kitabibu katika hospitali na huduma za jamii kwa wakati mmoja.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Mifumo ya kitabibu inahitaji upatikanaji wa juu sana, hivyo miundombinu husanifiwa kwa ustahimilivu na mabadiliko hupangwa kuzunguka shughuli za kitabibu.
- Wahandisi hushiriki katika zamu za wanaopatikana wakati wowote, kwa sababu hitilafu za miundombinu usiku au wikendi zinaweza kusimamisha matunzo ya wagonjwa.
- Mitandao huunganisha vifaa tiba, kama vile pampu za dawa, vifuatiliaji vya wagonjwa na vifaa vya picha, ambavyo vinahitaji ugawaji wa mtandao na mabadiliko makini kwa kufuata IEC 80001-1.
- Huduma za afya ni walengwa wa mara kwa mara wa programu za kutaka fidia, hivyo nakala rudufu lazima zilindwe, zijaribiwe na ziweze kurejeshwa ndani ya muda uliokubaliwa.
- Miundombinu mara nyingi huchanganya teknolojia ya zamani na mpya, ikiwemo mifumo ya zamani ya kitabibu isiyoweza kuwekewa marekebisho kwa urahisi, hivyo wahandisi huweka udhibiti mbadala.

## Daraja 6: Mhandisi wa miundombinu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Infrastructure engineer**

> Working in a team of infrastructure specialists and engineers, an infrastructure engineer supports and maintains infrastructure solutions and services as directed and according to departmental policy.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - ensure services are delivered and used as required
> - work with and support third parties to provide infrastructure services

### Majukumu

- Kujenga, kusanidi na kutunza seva, hifadhi, mitandao na huduma za wingu zinazohifadhi mifumo ya kitabibu na ya kampuni.
- Kuweka marekebisho ya usalama kwenye miundombinu haraka, akisawazisha hatari ya usalama dhidi ya haja ya kuweka mifumo ya kitabibu ikipatikana.
- Kuendesha na kujaribu nakala rudufu na urejeshaji, ikiwemo kwa mifumo muhimu ya kitabibu.
- Kusaidia mitandao iliyogawanywa kwa vifaa tiba na vifaa vingine vya kitabibu, akifanya kazi na timu za uhandisi wa kitabibu.
- Kuchunguza na kurekebisha hitilafu za miundombinu, ikiwemo kama sehemu ya zamu ya wanaopatikana wakati wowote.
- Kuendesha kiotomatiki kazi za kawaida kwa kutumia hati na miundombinu kama msimbo.

## Daraja 7: Mhandisi mwandamizi wa miundombinu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior infrastructure engineer**

> Working in a team of infrastructure specialists and engineers, a senior infrastructure engineer builds, administers, supports and maintains solutions as directed and according to departmental policy.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - ensure services are integrated, delivered and operated as required
> - work with and support third parties in providing infrastructure services
> - work with technical architects to translate architectural designs into operations
> - provide input into overall management - workforce, budget, technology roadmaps, projects and tasks
> - lead and direct infrastructure specialist teams in building, managing, supporting and maintaining solutions according to departmental policy (if taking a managerial path)

### Majukumu

- Kubuni na kujenga suluhisho za miundombinu kutoka miundo ya wasanifu, kwa ustahimilivu ambao huduma za kitabibu zinahitaji.
- Kupanga na kuongoza mabadiliko makubwa ya miundombinu, kama vile kuhamisha vituo vya data, kuboresha mitandao na uhamiaji wa wingu, kuzunguka shughuli za kitabibu.
- Kubuni na kujaribu urejeshaji baada ya maafa na urejeshaji baada ya programu za kutaka fidia kwa mifumo muhimu ya kitabibu.
- Kuongoza majibu ya kiufundi kwa matukio ya miundombinu na kusukuma marekebisho ya visababishi vyake.
- Kusimamia udhibiti mbadala kwa mifumo ya zamani na vifaa tiba visivyoweza kuwekewa marekebisho.
- Kuongoza na kuwaongoza wahandisi wa miundombinu.

## Daraja 8a: Mhandisi kiongozi wa miundombinu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead infrastructure engineer**

> A lead infrastructure engineer manages third party provision of infrastructure services and the provision of expertise to develop architectural solutions for infrastructure services, throughout the service product life cycle.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - oversee programmes and projects
> - work with technical architects to translate the architectural designs into operations and support technical architects in operationalising the designs
> - lead and direct infrastructure specialist teams in building, managing, supporting and maintaining solutions according to departmental policy (if taking a managerial path)

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza miundombinu ya jukwaa kuu au kundi la huduma, na kuweka mwelekeo wake wa kiufundi.
- Kusimamia wasambazaji wa miundombinu wa wahusika wengine, na kuwawajibisha kwa viwango vya huduma vya upatikanaji na urejeshaji.
- Kusimamia programu za miundombinu, kama vile kuboresha mtandao au kuhamia uhifadhi wa wingu.
- Kuhakikisha miundombinu inatimiza viwango vya usalama, usimamizi wa taarifa na mitandao ya vifaa tiba.
- Kuongoza timu za miundombinu na kuendeleza ujuzi wao.

## Daraja 8b: Mhandisi mkuu wa miundombinu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal infrastructure engineer**

> A principal infrastructure engineer is an accomplished technical leader.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - develop strategic and tactical engineering roadmaps for technologies and services in your area of responsibility, ensuring they're future-proofed and that the organisation derives maximum value from investment in technologies
> - potentially own the operational relationships with suppliers, ensuring services and products are delivered and aligned to industry best practice and regulatory and contractual requirements
> - work with technical architects to translate the architectural designs into operations and support technical architects in operationalising the designs
> - lead and direct infrastructure specialist teams in building, managing, supporting and maintaining solutions according to departmental policy (if taking a managerial path)

### Majukumu

- Kuendeleza mkakati na ramani za njia za miundombinu, ili uwekezaji utoe uhifadhi thabiti, salama na endelevu kwa huduma za kitabibu.

- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu hatari za miundombinu, ikiwemo teknolojia inayofikia mwisho wa maisha, sehemu moja za kushindwa na ustahimilivu wa mtandao.
- Kuweka malengo ya muda wa kurejesha na hatua ya kurejesha kwa mifumo ya kitabibu pamoja na wamiliki wa huduma, na kuhakikisha miundombinu inaweza kuyatimiza.
