# Mbunifu wa maudhui

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu unaomlenga mtumiaji](../../#usanifu-unaomlenga-mtumiaji)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Content designer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/content-designer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [web content manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/a74f6c51-37e5-423e-aa5b-044e98663027) (ISCO-08 2513); [technical communicator](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/8cb59bc4-1f39-477e-beea-effed12d186c) (ISCO-08 2641)

## Muhtasari

Wabunifu wa maudhui hufanya taarifa na huduma za afya na matunzo ziwe wazi, ili wagonjwa, walezi, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo waelewe wanachohitaji kujua na kufanya. Hubuni maudhui kutokana na ushahidi wa mahitaji ya watumiaji, huandika kwa lugha rahisi, hufanya kazi na wataalamu wa kitabibu kuyaweka sahihi, na kutunza maudhui katika maisha yake yote, kuanzia kuundwa hadi kukaguliwa na kustaafishwa.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Watu mara nyingi husoma maudhui ya afya wanapokuwa na wasiwasi, wagonjwa, au wana haraka, hivyo maudhui lazima yawe rahisi kueleweka na kutekelezwa kwa watu wenye uelewa mdogo wa masuala ya afya.
- Taarifa za afya zisizo sahihi au zilizopitwa na wakati zinaweza kusababisha madhara, hivyo wabunifu wa maudhui hufuata mchakato wa uhakikisho wa kitabibu wenye tarehe za ukaguzi na wakaguzi wa kitabibu waliotajwa.
- Baadhi ya mada ni nyeti au zenye hatari kubwa, kama vile dalili za dharura, afya ya akili, dawa na huduma za mwisho wa maisha, hivyo wabunifu wa maudhui hufanya kazi kwa karibu na madaktari na watafiti wa watumiaji kuhusu sauti na usahihi.
- Mifumo ya kitabibu pia inahitaji maudhui wazi, kama vile lebo, maelekezo na tahadhari, hivyo wabunifu wa maudhui husaidia kupunguza makosa katika mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu.
- Maudhui ya afya lazima yafikike na yajumuishe wote, ikiwemo watu wanaotumia teknolojia saidizi, wanaosoma kwa lugha nyingine, au wanaohitaji miundo rahisi kusoma.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Content designers make things easier for people to understand and use. This can involve working on a single piece of content or on the end-to-end journey of a service to help users complete their goal and government deliver a policy intent. In this role your work may involve the creation of, or change to, a transaction, product or single piece of content that stretches across digital and offline channels.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - make sure appropriate content is shown to a user in the right place and in the best format
> - start from discovery and work closely with user researchers, service designers and interaction designers

## Daraja 5: Mbunifu mdogo wa maudhui

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Junior content designer**

> A junior content designer has some relevant work experience in content creation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - explain content decisions
> - work collaboratively
> - work independently after being given direction by more senior content designers
> - independently identify user issues and needs
> - support content team administration and publishing processes
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government content community

## Daraja 6: Mbunifu wa maudhui

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Content designer**

> A content designer is responsible for creating, evaluating, updating and reviewing content at all stages of the end-to-end user journey.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be comfortable using evidence, data and research to make content decisions
> - build relationships across government to focus on the needs of the user and to influence stakeholders
> - contribute to and use the style guides and content patterns
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government content community

### Majukumu

- Kubuni maudhui katika safari nzima za watumiaji, kama vile kupata huduma, kujiandaa kwa miadi na matunzo ya baadaye.
- Kubuni maudhui kwa mifumo ya kitabibu, kama vile lebo, maelekezo na tahadhari, ili kupunguza uwezekano wa makosa.
- Kukubaliana usahihi na wataalamu wa kitabibu huku akiweka maudhui wazi na yanayoweza kutumika.
- Kujaribu maudhui na watumiaji, ikiwemo watu wenye uelewa mdogo wa masuala ya afya na wanaotumia teknolojia saidizi.
- Kuchangia mwongozo wa mtindo na mifumo ya maudhui.
