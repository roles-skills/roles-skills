> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa usimamizi wa data huhakikisha data za afya na matunzo za taasisi zinasimamiwa vizuri, zinaaminika, na zinatumika kwa usalama na kihalali. Huanzisha na kuendesha mifumo, sera na viwango vya umiliki wa data, ubora, metadata na ushirikishaji wa data, na hufanya kazi na wamiliki wa data, wenzake wa usimamizi wa taarifa na viongozi wa kitabibu ili data zitumike kwa kujiamini kwa matunzo, mipango na utafiti.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Data za afya ni data binafsi nyeti sana, hivyo usimamizi wa data hufanya kazi kwa karibu na usimamizi wa taarifa, ulinzi wa data na wajibu wa usiri.
- Data hushirikishwa na washirika wengi wa afya na matunzo kwa matunzo ya moja kwa moja, mipango na utafiti, na kila matumizi yanahitaji msingi halali ulio wazi, makubaliano na udhibiti.
- Viwango vya data za kitabibu, kama SNOMED CT, ICD na HL7 FHIR, na fasili za data zilizokubaliwa ni kiini cha kufanya data ziwe thabiti katika mifumo na taasisi.
- Ubora duni wa data unaweza kusababisha moja kwa moja madhara kwa wagonjwa, kama vile mizio iliyokosekana au ulinganishaji usio sahihi wa wagonjwa, pamoja na maamuzi yasiyo sahihi kuhusu huduma.
- Wagonjwa na umma wanatarajia uwazi kuhusu jinsi data zao zinavyotumika, hivyo usimamizi hujumuisha uwazi na kuheshimu chaguzi za wagonjwa kuhusu matumizi ya pili.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A data governance manager plans, monitors and enforces the management of data assets.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - catalogue, monitor and identify data assets that are essential to the organisation’s operations or services
> - assign ownership of data, ensuring there is a clear RACI (responsible, accountable, consulted and informed) matrix in the organisation
> - incorporate governance of data into routine processes
> - support or manage risk processes around data
> - advocate for better use and management of data within the organisation
> - encourage better data literacy within the organisation

## Daraja 7: Meneja wa usimamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Data governance manager**

> A data governance manager defines, implements and manages data governance frameworks, policies and standards.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work on the design and implementation of practical data governance solutions to support organisational needs
> - identify opportunities and make recommendations to improve the quality and trustworthiness of data throughout the organisation
> - implement and establish wider data management competencies within the organisation (such as data quality, metadata management, master data management, data modelling, and development of data standards and data strategy)
> - apply data governance to optimise the safety and value of data throughout the data life cycle
> - work within the organisation to minimise data risks and increase data literacy

### Majukumu

- Kuendesha mfumo wa usimamizi wa data wa taasisi, ikiwemo umiliki wa data, uangalizi na vikundi vya kufanya maamuzi.
- Kutunza katalogi ya data, faharasa ya biashara na viwango vya data, ikiwemo fasili zilizokubaliwa za kitabibu na za kiutendaji.
- Kufuatilia ubora wa data kwa seti kuu za data na kufanya kazi na wamiliki wa data kurekebisha visababishi.
- Kufanya kazi na wenzake wa usimamizi wa taarifa kuhusu makubaliano ya ushirikishaji wa data na tathmini za athari za ulinzi wa data kwa matumizi mapya ya data.
- Kurekodi na kusimamia hatari na masuala ya data, na kuyapeleka kwa mmiliki sahihi.
- Kuboresha uelewa wa data miongoni mwa wafanyakazi wanaounda na kutumia data za afya.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Shahada au sifa ya kitaaluma katika data, usimamizi wa taarifa, au somo linalohusiana, au uzoefu unaolingana.
- Uzoefu wa usimamizi wa data au uongozi wa data katika taasisi tata.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi maalumu uliokuzwa sana, kwa kawaida hadi kiwango cha shahada ya uzamili au uzoefu unaolingana.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Hufanya kazi kwa sera ya taasisi; huamua jinsi matokeo yanavyofikiwa; ndiye mtaalamu wanayemshauriana naye wengine.
- **Wigo:** Bidhaa au huduma kadhaa, au kitengo maalumu.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza timu au eneo la mazoezi ya kitaaluma.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utoaji wa huduma au kitengo maalumu, na bajeti yake ikiwa anaisimamia.

## Daraja 8b: Meneja kiongozi wa usimamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead data governance manager**

> A lead data governance manager undertakes complex work related to the principles and practices of data governance, and co-ordinates with data owners to lead wider data management competencies.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead the formation, implementation and management of data governance frameworks, policies and standards
> - implement and establish wider data management competencies within the organisation (such as data quality assurance, metadata management, master data management, data modelling, and development of data standards and data strategy)
> - be responsible for understanding and managing the organisational data risks and issues, and co-ordinating with data owners to accept or resolve them

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu na uendeshaji wa mifumo, sera na viwango vya usimamizi wa data katika taasisi nzima.
- Kuratibu wamiliki wa data na waangalizi kusimamia hatari za data, kuzikubali au kuzitatua, na kuziripoti kwa kamati za juu.
- Kuongoza kazi pana ya usimamizi wa data, kama vile data kuu za wagonjwa, wafanyakazi na maeneo, na data za marejeleo.
- Kukubaliana mipango ya usimamizi wa data kwa data zinazoshirikishwa na washirika wa afya na matunzo na watafiti.
- Kushauri programu jinsi mifumo mipya na matumizi mapya ya data yanavyotimiza viwango vya data na kanuni za usimamizi.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja wafanyakazi wa usimamizi wa data.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa usimamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of data governance**

> The head of data governance works with the chief data officer across the organisation and government, providing strategic oversight and leadership in all aspects of data governance and use. They ensure high standards are maintained in-line with regulations and ethical requirements.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with the chief data officer to provide strategic oversight of data governance throughout the organisation
> - set and monitor the adherence to data governance policies, frameworks and standards
> - be accountable for understanding and managing the organisational data risks and issues, and co-ordinating with data owners to accept or resolve them
> - act as a senior advisor across government on the best practice of data security development, in accordance with the data enterprise architecture
> - maintain effective long-term relationships at a senior level with important decision-makers and influencers
> - advocate improved data risk management and practices within the organisation
> - encourage better data literacy and understanding of data governance within the organisation

### Majukumu

- Kufanya kazi na mkuu wa data na mmiliki mtendaji wa hatari za taarifa kuweka mkakati wa usimamizi na matumizi ya data.
- Kuweka sera na viwango vya usimamizi wa data na kufuatilia jinsi taasisi inavyovifuata vizuri.
- Kuwajibika kwa kusimamia hatari za data za taasisi, na kuripoti kuzihusu kwa bodi.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi kuhusu usimamizi wa data kwa washirika wa afya na matunzo, wadhibiti na vikundi vya wagonjwa.
- Kukuza uwazi kwa wagonjwa na umma kuhusu jinsi data zao zinavyotumika.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza kitengo cha usimamizi wa data.
