# Mchambuzi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Uhakikisho wa ubora na majaribio](../../#uhakikisho-wa-ubora-na-majaribio)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 8a  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Quality assurance test analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/quality-assurance-test-analyst/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT test analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/0ab5c12c-c1c1-4772-8345-aa287d0e391d) (ISCO-08 2519)

## Muhtasari

Wachambuzi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora hukagua kama huduma za afya za kidijitali za taasisi zinafanya kile wagonjwa, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo wanachohitaji, kwa usalama na kwa uaminifu. Huchambua mahitaji na miundo kupata hatari mapema, hubuni na kuendesha majaribio ya kiutendaji na yasiyo ya kiutendaji, na kuripoti kasoro kwa uwazi. Katika huduma za afya hii inajumuisha kujaribu mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu, usahihi wa data, na udhibiti unaowalinda wagonjwa dhidi ya madhara.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Kasoro katika mifumo ya kitabibu zinaweza kuwadhuru wagonjwa, kwa mfano kupitia dozi isiyo sahihi ya dawa, matokeo yaliyokosekana, au rekodi iliyoonyeshwa kwa mgonjwa asiye sahihi, hivyo majaribio hupewa vipaumbele kwa hatari ya kitabibu.
- Majaribio hutoa ushahidi kwa hoja za usalama wa kitabibu na kumbukumbu za hatari, hivyo mipango ya majaribio hufuatiliwa hadi hatari zilizotambuliwa na udhibiti wake.
- Data za majaribio hazipaswi kamwe kufichua wagonjwa halisi, hivyo wachambuzi hutumia data bandia au zilizofichwa utambulisho ipasavyo.
- Huduma nyingi hubadilishana data na mifumo mingine ya afya na matunzo, hivyo majaribio hukagua ujumbe dhidi ya viwango kama HL7 FHIR na maana ya misimbo ya kitabibu.
- Watumiaji hujumuisha madaktari walio chini ya shinikizo la muda, wagonjwa wenye mahitaji mbalimbali, na watumiaji wa teknolojia saidizi, hivyo majaribio ya urahisi wa matumizi na ufikivu ni muhimu.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A quality assurance test analyst interprets testing needs, creating and executing appropriate tests to ensure quality and alignment with requirements.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - plan, design and execute tests
> - apply testing techniques and standards to learn about systems and mitigate risk
> - analyse artefacts and prototypes
> - test outcomes and record defects
> - seek to improve testing practices to enhance quality and reliability
> - recommend testing approaches

## Daraja 4: Mchambuzi msaidizi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate quality assurance test analyst**

> An associate quality assurance test analyst works with other test professionals to learn quality assurance testing and analysis techniques.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - follow and apply test approaches, plans and strategies, under supervision
> - analyse artefacts, such as user stories, prototypes, processes and designs, with support
> - use basic functional and non-functional testing techniques
> - increase your understanding of how to design and execute basic tests, develop reports, record outcomes and support the resolution of defects

### Majukumu

- Kuendesha hati za majaribio kwa mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu na za kiutawala chini ya usimamizi, akirekodi matokeo kwa usahihi.
- Kuibua ripoti za kasoro zilizo wazi, ikiwemo hatua, data na skrini zilizohusika.
- Kutumia data bandia za majaribio na kamwe kutotumia data halisi za wagonjwa katika mazingira ya majaribio.
- Kujifunza mbinu za majaribio za timu, zana na mchakato wa usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kushiriki katika taratibu za timu kama vile mikutano ya kusimama, mipango na tafakari.

## Daraja 5: Mchambuzi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Quality assurance test analyst**

> A quality assurance test analyst designs and executes appropriate functional and non-functional tests. They apply multiple testing techniques to inform the approach to mitigate risk.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - apply the most appropriate test approaches and plans based on risk
> - analyse artefacts, such as user stories, prototypes, processes and designs
> - determine what to test following an agreed approach
> - ensure test coverage aligns with business risk
> - follow appropriate standards to mitigate risk early
> - execute tests, develop reports, record outcomes and support the resolution of defects

### Majukumu

- Kuchambua hadithi za watumiaji, miundo na mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu kuamua nini cha kujaribu, kulingana na hatari kwa wagonjwa na huduma.
- Kubuni na kuendesha majaribio ya kiutendaji, ya ufikivu na ya urahisi wa matumizi, ikiwemo matukio ya kitabibu ya mwanzo hadi mwisho.
- Kufuatilia majaribio hadi hatari na udhibiti wa usalama, na kutoa ushahidi wa majaribio kwa hoja za usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kukagua data zinazoonyeshwa kwa watumiaji dhidi ya mifumo chanzo na misimbo ya kitabibu iliyokubaliwa.
- Kuunda na kutunza data bandia za majaribio zinazofanana na halisi.
- Kufuatilia kasoro hadi zitatuliwe na kujaribu upya marekebisho.

## Daraja 6: Mchambuzi mwandamizi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior quality assurance test analyst**

> A senior quality assurance test analyst influences and guides others in test analysis, coordinating quality assurance activities in a defined area. They assess the business impact of testing outcomes and use insights to improve quality assurance processes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - use the most appropriate tools and techniques to analyse artefacts such as products, services, processes and change, to identify risks early
> - work with others to support teams in developing approaches, plans and strategy, considering environments, data, tools and resources
> - communicate chosen approaches, tools, techniques and outcomes to stakeholders
> - guide others in quality assurance tools and techniques
> - contribute to, and enable others to follow, quality assurance and testing standards

### Majukumu

- Kuratibu uhakikisho wa ubora kwa bidhaa au eneo, akikubaliana mbinu za majaribio, mazingira, data na zana.
- Kutambua hatari za kitabibu na za kiutendaji mapema kwa kukagua mahitaji, miundo na mabadiliko pamoja na madaktari na timu za bidhaa.
- Kupanga na kuongoza majaribio ya ukubalifu wa watumiaji pamoja na madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo.
- Kuripoti matokeo ya majaribio na hatari zilizobaki kwa wamiliki wa bidhaa na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu, ili waweze kufanya maamuzi ya toleo.
- Kuwaongoza wachambuzi wengine wa majaribio na kuwasaidia kufuata viwango vya majaribio na usalama wa kitabibu.

## Daraja 8a: Mchambuzi kiongozi wa majaribio wa uhakikisho wa ubora

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead quality assurance test analyst**

> A lead quality assurance test analyst sets the strategy for test analysis and influences test analysis practices across a broad area. They develop, monitor and evaluate test analysis standards and methods, and make strategic decisions that improve test analysis in the organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead on setting standards and good practices across the test analysis community
> - lead and guide teams across the organisation in test analysis
> - define and implement test strategies
> - mentor and coach other quality assurance test analysts
> - negotiate and escalate major risks, issues and defects

### Majukumu

- Kuweka viwango vya uchambuzi wa majaribio na mazoezi mazuri katika taasisi, ikiwemo majaribio yanayotegemea hatari ya mifumo ya kitabibu.
- Kufafanua mikakati ya majaribio kwa programu kubwa, kama vile mabadiliko ya rekodi ya mgonjwa ya kielektroniki au huduma mpya zinazowalenga wagonjwa.
- Kuhakikisha majaribio yanatoa ushahidi unaohitajika kwa hoja za usalama wa kitabibu na, inapohusika, mafaili ya kiufundi ya vifaa tiba.
- Kujadili na kupeleka juu hatari kubwa, masuala na kasoro kwa viongozi waandamizi wa bidhaa, wa kitabibu na wa wasambazaji.
- Kuwa mshauri na kuwaongoza wachambuzi wa majaribio na kuongoza jumuiya ya uchambuzi wa majaribio.
