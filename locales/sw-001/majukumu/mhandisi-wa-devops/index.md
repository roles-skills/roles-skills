# Mhandisi wa DevOps

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Development operations (DevOps) engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/development-operations-devops-engineer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [cloud DevOps engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/cc867bee-ab5c-427f-9244-f7a204d9574b) (ISCO-08 2512); [cloud engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/349ee6f6-c295-4c38-9b98-48765b55280e) (ISCO-08 2512)

## Muhtasari

Wahandisi wa DevOps hujenga na kuendesha zana, mifereji, mazingira na majukwaa yanayoziwezesha timu za taasisi kutoa huduma za afya za kidijitali kwa usalama na mara kwa mara. Huendesha kiotomatiki ujenzi, majaribio, matoleo na miundombinu, hufanya huduma zionekane na kustahimili, na kuzisaidia timu kuweka mifumo ya kitabibu ikipatikana wagonjwa na madaktari wanapoihitaji.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Huduma nyingi zinaunga mkono matunzo saa nzima, hivyo usambazaji, ufuatiliaji na urejeshaji lazima vipunguze muda wa kutopatikana, ikiwemo nje ya saa za kazi.
- Mifereji ya matoleo hutoa ushahidi kwa hoja za usalama wa kitabibu, kama vile matokeo ya majaribio, kumbukumbu za mabadiliko na toleo kamili lililosambazwa.
- Mazingira yanaweza kushikilia taarifa za siri za afya, hivyo wahandisi hudhibiti ufikiaji, hulinda siri na kuweka data halisi nje ya mazingira ya majaribio.
- Huduma za afya ni walengwa wa kawaida wa mashambulizi ya mtandao, hivyo mifereji na majukwaa hujenga ndani uchanganuzi wa usalama, uwekaji wa marekebisho na nakala rudufu zilizojaribiwa.
- Baadhi ya huduma lazima zitimize viwango vikali vya upatikanaji na urejeshaji baada ya maafa, vilivyokubaliwa na viongozi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Commonly referred to as ‘DevOps engineers’, development operations engineers support the development and operation of software through tools, environments and practices.
> 
> In this role, you will be responsible for underpinning good development processes including managing tools and testing environments, central code control, maintaining development standards and writing software that automates systems.

## Daraja 4: Mhandisi mwanagenzi wa DevOps

## Daraja 6: Mhandisi wa DevOps

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: DevOps engineer**

> A DevOps engineer delivers automated software components that form part of a development operations toolchain.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - transform technical requirements into DevOps processes
> - build complex stories without additional support
> - manage live test environments
> - identify and resolve issues that are preventing delivery

- Kubuni na kujenga mifereji, mazingira na vijenzi vya jukwaa kwa kujitegemea.

- Kuchunguza na kurekebisha matatizo katika huduma za moja kwa moja, ikiwemo msaada nje ya saa za kazi pale huduma inapohitaji.

## Daraja 7: Mhandisi mwandamizi wa DevOps

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior DevOps engineer**

> A senior DevOps engineer delivers and integrates software to form a complete DevOps toolchain. They are responsible for planning and designing large groups of stories.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - transform technical requirements into an effective DevOps toolchain to enable product delivery
> - provide coaching and mentoring to more junior colleagues
> - ensure that deployment strategies for products are repeatable, scalable and highly available
> - have deep technical knowledge, providing support to delivery teams and solving complex problems
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa mnyororo wa zana za utoaji na jukwaa kwa bidhaa au kundi la huduma.
- Kuweka viwango vya matoleo, ili usambazaji kwa huduma za kitabibu uweze kurudiwa, kubatilishwa na kuwa na ushahidi mzuri.
