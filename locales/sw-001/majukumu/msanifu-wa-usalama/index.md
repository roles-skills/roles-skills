# Msanifu wa usalama

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 8a, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Security architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/security-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT system architect](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/e1c72b5f-4c5c-487c-a6df-e84b64a51dae) (ISCO-08 2511); [cybersecurity risk manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/7754d570-9519-48c2-b1c9-8e165f8bca0f) (ISCO-08 2529)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa usalama husanifu usalama wa huduma za afya za kidijitali za taasisi, ili taarifa za wagonjwa zibaki za siri, sahihi na zinapatikana matunzo yanapozihitaji. Huweka mifumo na udhibiti wa usalama, hutathmini hatari za usalama za miundo mipya na wasambazaji, na kuzisaidia timu kufanya maamuzi yanayotegemea hatari yanayoweka huduma salama na zinazoweza kutumika kwa madaktari na wagonjwa.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Taasisi za afya ni walengwa wa mara kwa mara wa programu za kutaka fidia na wizi wa data, na shambulio linaweza kusimamisha huduma za kitabibu, hivyo upatikanaji ni muhimu kama usiri.
- Udhibiti wa usalama haupaswi kuzuia matunzo ya dharura, hivyo miundo husawazisha uthibitishaji imara na ufikiaji wa haraka katika mazingira ya kitabibu.
- Vifaa tiba vilivyounganishwa na mifumo ya zamani ya kitabibu mara nyingi haviwezi kuwekewa marekebisho haraka, hivyo wasanifu husanifu udhibiti mbadala kama ugawaji.
- Wafanyakazi wengi, taasisi washirika na wasambazaji wanahitaji ufikiaji wa kumbukumbu za wagonjwa, hivyo utambulisho, ufikiaji unaotegemea nafasi na ukaguzi ni kiini.
- Miundo ya usalama lazima iunge mkono wajibu wa usimamizi wa taarifa na viwango vilivyochapishwa vya usalama, kama vile ISO/IEC 27001.

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu wa usalama

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Security architect**

> A security architect creates and designs security for a system or service, maintains security documentation and develops architecture patterns and security approaches to new technologies.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - recommend security controls and identify solutions that support a business objective
> - provide specialist advice and recommend approaches across teams and various stakeholders
> - communicate widely with other stakeholders
> - advise on important security-related technologies and assess the risk associated with proposed changes
> - inspire and influence others to execute security principles
> - help review other people’s work

### Majukumu

- Kubuni udhibiti wa usalama kwa huduma na mifumo, ikiwemo utambulisho, usimbaji fiche, uandishi wa kumbukumbu na ulinzi wa mtandao.
- Kutathmini usalama wa miundo mipya na bidhaa za wasambazaji, na kurekodi hatari na udhibiti unaopendekezwa.
- Kuendeleza mifumo ya usalama ambayo timu za utoaji zinaweza kuitumia tena, kama vile kwa kuingia kwa wagonjwa na kuingia mara moja kwa wafanyakazi.
- Kushauri kuhusu udhibiti mbadala kwa vifaa tiba na mifumo ya zamani ya kitabibu isiyoweza kusasishwa kwa urahisi.
- Kufanya kazi na wenzake wa usimamizi wa taarifa na usalama wa kitabibu ili hatari za usalama, faragha na usalama wa mgonjwa zitathminiwe pamoja.
- Kuwaongoza wahandisi wa usalama na wasanifu wengine katika usanifu salama.

## Daraja 8b: Msanifu kiongozi wa usalama

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead security architect**

> A lead security architect undertakes complex work of a high risk level, often working on several projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - interact with senior stakeholders across departments
> - reach and influence a wide range of people across larger teams and communities
> - research and apply innovative security architecture solutions to new or existing problems and be able to justify and communicate design decisions
> - develop vision, principles and strategy for security architects for one project or technology
> - work out subtle security needs
> - understand the impact of decisions, balancing requirements and deciding between approaches
> - produce particular patterns and support quality assurance
> - be the point of escalation for architects in lower grade roles
> - lead the technical design of systems and services

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa usalama kwa miradi kadhaa yenye hatari kubwa au eneo kuu, kama vile mifumo ya kitabibu au uhifadhi wa wingu.
- Kuweka kanuni na viwango vya usalama kwa miundo katika eneo lake na kuvihakikisha katika mamlaka za usanifu.
- Kuwashauri wamiliki wakuu wa hatari kuhusu hatari za usalama na chaguzi za kuzisimamia.
- Kubuni usalama kwa data zinazoshirikishwa na taasisi washirika na wasambazaji.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza wasanifu wa usalama.

## Daraja 8c: Msanifu mkuu wa usalama

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal security architect**

> A principal security architect works on services of high complexity and risk, making decisions to enable the business to achieve its needs.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work on projects with high strategic impact, setting a strategy that can be used in the long term and across the breadth of the organisation
> - communicate with a broad range of senior stakeholders and be responsible for defining the vision, principles and strategy for security architects
> - recommend security design across several projects or technologies, up to an organisational or inter-organisational level
> - have a deep and evolving level of technical expertise, so you can act as an exemplar
> - make and influence important business and architectural decisions
> - research, identify, validate and adopt new technologies and methodologies
> - be a recognised expert and demonstrate this expertise by solving unprecedented issues and problems
> - further the profession, demonstrating and sharing best practice within and outside the organisation

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono, kanuni na mkakati wa usanifu wa usalama wa taasisi.
- Kuishauri timu ya watendaji na bodi kuhusu hatari ya usalama wa mtandao kwa matunzo ya wagonjwa na huduma.
- Kuhakikisha ustahimilivu wa mtandao, ikiwemo urejeshaji baada ya shambulio kubwa, umebuniwa ndani ya huduma muhimu za kitabibu.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi katika jumuiya za usalama wa mtandao na viwango za taasisi mbalimbali.
- Kuongoza mazoezi ya usanifu wa usalama na kuendeleza watu wake.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Uzoefu mpana wa kuongoza usanifu wa usalama katika taasisi mbalimbali.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi wa kitaalamu, pamoja na uelewa mpana wa taasisi na sekta.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Huweka mkakati wa kitengo; anawajibika kwa mkurugenzi.
- **Wigo:** Kitengo au idara.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza kitengo kupitia ngazi kadhaa za usimamizi.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utendaji wa kitengo, nguvu kazi yake, na bajeti yake.
