# Meneja wa utoaji

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Bidhaa na utoaji](../../#bidhaa-na-utoaji)  
**Madaraja:** 5, 7, 8a, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Delivery manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/delivery-manager/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT project manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/8b6388a4-4904-471b-9331-d3b1211f5525) (ISCO-08 1330)

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa utoaji huzisaidia timu za fani mbalimbali kutoa huduma za afya za kidijitali zinazowafaa wagonjwa, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo. Huunda mazingira ya timu kufanya kazi vizuri, huondoa vikwazo, husimamia hatari na utegemezi, na kuweka utoaji ukiendelea kwa kasi endelevu, kwa kutumia njia za agile na lean.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Matoleo ya huduma zinazounga mkono matunzo ya wagonjwa lazima yapitie ukaguzi wa usalama wa kitabibu na usimamizi wa taarifa, hivyo mameneja wa utoaji huyaingiza katika mipango ya timu na ufafanuzi wa kukamilika.
- Utoaji mara nyingi hutegemea madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo wenye muda mdogo mbali na wagonjwa, hivyo mameneja wa utoaji hupanga utafiti, majaribio na mafunzo kuzunguka ratiba za kitabibu.
- Mabadiliko ya huduma za kitabibu zinazoendelea yanahitaji muda makini, hivyo mameneja wa utoaji hupanga kuanza kutumika pamoja na timu za kiutendaji ili kuepuka vipindi vya kilele vya kitabibu na kuhakikisha msaada upo.
- Timu mara nyingi hutegemea wasambazaji wa mifumo ya kitabibu na taasisi nyingine za afya na matunzo, hivyo mameneja wa utoaji husimamia utegemezi na mikataba ya nje.

## Daraja 5: Meneja msaidizi wa utoaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate delivery manager**

> At this role level, you will:
> - work on a small or mature Agile team
> - learn on the job
> - potentially work alongside a delivery manager on a larger team, shadowing and providing support, or working to deliver an element under guidance and mentorship

### Majukumu

- Kuwezesha taratibu za timu kama vile mikutano ya kusimama, mipango, maonyesho na tafakari.
- Kuweka ubao wa timu, kumbukumbu ya hatari na masuala, na rekodi za utegemezi zikiwa za kisasa.
- Kufuatilia kazi za usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na ufikivu zinazohitajika kabla ya kila toleo.
- Kupanga vikao na madaktari, wafanyakazi wa matunzo na wagonjwa kwa ajili ya utafiti na majaribio, akizunguka ratiba za kitabibu.
- Kumfuata na kumsaidia meneja wa utoaji katika timu kubwa, akitoa sehemu za kazi kwa mwongozo.

## Daraja 7: Meneja wa utoaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Delivery manager**

> A delivery manager is accountable for the performance of the team.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build and maintain teams, ensuring they are motivated, collaborating and working well
> - identify obstacles and help the team to overcome them
> - focus the team on what is most important to the delivery of products and services
> - encourage and facilitate continuous improvement of the delivery team
> - coach and mentor both team members and others to apply the most appropriate Agile and Lean tools and techniques
> The complexity or breadth of products or teams will vary in this role, depending on the context.

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza utoaji wa bidhaa au huduma ya afya ya kidijitali kupitia ugunduzi, alfa, beta na huduma ya moja kwa moja.
- Kupanga matoleo pamoja na timu za kiutendaji na za kitabibu, ikiwemo msaada wa kuanza kutumika, mafunzo na mipango ya kurudi nyuma.
- Kuhakikisha uhakikisho wa usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na ufikivu unafanyika sambamba na utoaji, si mwishoni.
- Kusimamia bajeti ya timu, mikataba ya wasambazaji na upatikanaji wa rasilimali.
- Kuripoti maendeleo, hatari na manufaa kwa bodi za usimamizi kwa maneno rahisi.
- Kujenga utamaduni mzuri wa timu na kuwaongoza wanachama wa timu katika njia za kufanya kazi za agile.

## Daraja 8a: Meneja mwandamizi wa utoaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior delivery manager**

> A senior delivery manager is accountable for the effective delivery of complex, high risk products and services. The role is similar to a delivery manager role, but senior delivery managers have more experience across a range of products and services, throughout the entire product life cycle, and have greater responsibility and accountability as the main point of escalation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have strong communication skills and engage senior stakeholders
> - coach and mentor delivery managers

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza utoaji wa huduma tata au zenye hatari kubwa, kama zile zinazotumika katika huduma za dharura au zinazobadilisha mfumo mkuu wa kitabibu.
- Kuwa kituo kikuu cha kupeleka juu hatari za utoaji, masuala na utegemezi katika timu kadhaa.
- Kupanga usambazaji mkubwa katika maeneo na taasisi, pamoja na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu na viongozi wa kiutendaji.
- Kusimamia bajeti, mipango ya kibiashara na utendaji wa wasambazaji kwa huduma.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja na kuendeleza mameneja wa utoaji.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa usimamizi wa utoaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of (Agile) delivery management**

> A head of (Agile) delivery management is an experienced practitioner who exemplifies what good looks like across the delivery roles.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - represent and champion the role within your department, across government and in industry
> - lead the community of practice for this role and build capability and excellence (in an Agile, Lean practice)
> - be responsible for the recruitment of the right people to the right teams
> - support professional development and continuous improvement of your community
> - work with other heads of roles to promote effective cross-functional delivery
> - be a skilled team leader who can confidently communicate the value of the role to digital and non-digital stakeholders
> - be credible and influential across departments

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza taaluma ya usimamizi wa utoaji na kuweka viwango vya taasisi vya utoaji wa agile.
- Kuajiri, kuendeleza na kupeleka mameneja wa utoaji katika jalada lote.
- Kuhakikisha mbinu za utoaji zinatimiza mahitaji ya usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na uhakikisho bila kupunguza kasi ya timu bila sababu.
- Kuwashauri viongozi watendaji na wa kitabibu kuhusu hatari za utoaji, uwezo na vipaumbele.
- Kusimamia bajeti na mpango wa nguvu kazi wa taaluma.
