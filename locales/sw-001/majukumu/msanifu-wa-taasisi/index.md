# Msanifu wa taasisi

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 8a, 8b, 8c, 8d  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Enterprise architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/enterprise-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [enterprise architect](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2bef94db-0088-4507-982a-2ca717529adb) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa taasisi huipa taasisi mtazamo ulioungana wa huduma, taarifa, programu na teknolojia zake, na kuutumia kugeuza mkakati wa afya na matunzo kuwa mpango thabiti wa mabadiliko. Huweka kanuni za usanifu, viwango na ramani za njia, huongoza maamuzi ya uwekezaji, na kuhakikisha mabadiliko ya kidijitali yanaunga mkono matunzo salama na yaliyounganishwa kwa wagonjwa katika taasisi mbalimbali.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Njia za matunzo huvuka taasisi na mifumo mingi, hivyo usanifu lazima uunge mkono ushirikishaji wa kumbukumbu, matokeo na rufaa kuvuka mipaka.
- Mkusanyiko mara nyingi hujumuisha mifumo ya kitabibu ya muda mrefu, vifaa tiba na mikataba ya wasambazaji ambayo ni vigumu kubadilisha, hivyo ramani za njia lazima zipange mpito kwa uangalifu.
- Maamuzi ya usanifu huathiri usalama wa mgonjwa, faragha na ustahimilivu wa huduma zinazofanya kazi saa nzima.
- Viwango huria kama HL7 FHIR, SNOMED CT na wasifu wa IHE ni muhimu ili kuepuka kufungwa kwa muuzaji mmoja na kuwezesha ushirikiano wa mifumo.
- Ufadhili una vikwazo na unachunguzwa kwa karibu, hivyo wasanifu lazima waonyeshe thamani ya fedha na utumiaji tena wa majukwaa yanayoshirikiwa pale yanapokuwepo.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Enterprise architects are leaders working across different levels within an organisation to translate the business strategy into business change and technical delivery.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - identify priorities for change to enable delivery at pace
> - lead and influence the delivery of cross-cutting capabilities that enable change
> - own the enterprise architecture vision, strategy and roadmaps from a business, technology and data perspective, including ‘as is’, ‘to be’ and transitional states
> - consult and support collaboration across the business
> - understand the organisation’s ecosystem and its interdependencies, including reference architectures
> - take a strategic view across all architectural domains, portfolios and programmes
> - guide the organisation to make appropriate business, technology and data decisions by recommending reuse, sustainability and scalability, to achieve value for money and reduce risk
> - establish architectural principles, policies and standards
> - collaborate and consult with stakeholders to assure business, technology and data decisions are aligned with enterprise architecture strategy
> - develop the architecture community
> - carry out horizon scanning across industry, identifying emerging trends and their potential impact and opportunity for the organisation

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu wa taasisi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Enterprise architect**

> An enterprise architect supports other architects in developing the strategy.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - network and communicate with stakeholders across domains or enterprises, and identify opportunities for improvement
> - use best practice
> - use emerging technologies and approaches
> - learn to identify influences or risks
> - play a part in successful delivery of the team’s objectives

### Majukumu

- Kuunda miundo ya huduma, taarifa, programu na teknolojia za sasa na lengwa za taasisi kwa eneo, kama vile uchunguzi wa kitabibu au matunzo ya jamii.
- Kuweka hazina ya usanifu ikiwa ya kisasa, ikiwemo katalogi za programu na mtiririko wa data.
- Kukagua mapendekezo dhidi ya kanuni na viwango vya usanifu, na kutoa mapendekezo kwa mamlaka za usanifu.
- Kutambua mahali mifumo inapojirudia uwezo au kutegemea teknolojia isiyoungwa mkono, na kuibua hatari.
- Kufanya kazi na wasanifu wa suluhisho na viongozi wa kitabibu kuelewa jinsi njia za matunzo zinavyotumia mifumo.
- Kuwaongoza wasanifu wa suluhisho jinsi miundo inavyolingana na usanifu wa eneo.

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi wa kitaalamu wa taaluma na usimamizi wake.

- **Uwajibikaji:** Eneo la huduma, wafanyakazi wake, na bajeti yake.

## Daraja 8b: Msanifu mwandamizi wa taasisi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior enterprise architect**

> A senior enterprise architect supports lead architects in ensuring the strategy is developed, agreed and followed.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - network and communicate with stakeholders across domains or enterprises
> - identify opportunities for improvement
> - support a community or team
> - use best practice
> - use emerging technologies and approaches
> - play an active part in the team and understand how to deliver the team’s objectives to meet organisational goals
> - identify influences or risks
> - play a part in successful delivery of the long-term strategy

### Majukumu

- Kuendeleza usanifu lengwa na ramani za njia za mpito kwa eneo moja au zaidi la taasisi.
- Kuunda hoja za kibiashara ili uwekezaji ulingane na usanifu lengwa na kutumia tena majukwaa yanayoshirikiwa.
- Kuongoza ukaguzi wa usanifu katika mamlaka za usanifu na kutoa mapendekezo kuhusu mapendekezo muhimu.
- Kupanga kubadilisha au kustaafisha mifumo ya zamani ya kitabibu na ya kampuni, ikiwemo uhamishaji wa data na hatari za usalama.
- Kuhakikisha mahitaji ya ushirikiano wa mifumo, usalama na usimamizi wa taarifa yanajengwa ndani ya ramani za njia.
- Kuwaongoza wasanifu wa taasisi na wa suluhisho.

## Daraja 8c: Msanifu kiongozi wa taasisi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead enterprise architect**

> A lead enterprise architect plays a high level role in ensuring the strategy is developed, agreed and followed.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - network and communicate with senior stakeholders across enterprises, and seek opportunities for improvement
> - support at least one community or team, or a combination of both
> - find and use best practice and emerging technologies and approaches
> - lead teams including enterprise architects and help them understand how to deliver the team’s objectives to meet organisational goals
> - horizon scan for influences or risks
> - support successful delivery of the long-term strategy
> - take a leading role in the overall direction of business and digital capabilities

### Majukumu

- Kumiliki usanifu wa taasisi kwa sehemu kubwa ya taasisi, kama vile mifumo ya kitabibu au miundombinu inayoshirikiwa.
- Kuongoza au kuwa mwenyekiti wa mamlaka ya usanifu na kuweka viwango ambavyo miradi lazima itimize.
- Kuwashauri wakurugenzi kuhusu vipaumbele vya uwekezaji, mkakati wa wasambazaji na hatari za teknolojia.
- Kufanya kazi na taasisi washirika kuoanisha usanifu katika njia za matunzo na kumbukumbu zinazoshirikiwa.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza wasanifu wa taasisi na kukuza jumuiya ya mazoezi ya usanifu.

## Daraja 8d: Msanifu mkuu wa taasisi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal enterprise architect**

> A principal enterprise architect leads at the highest level and is responsible for ensuring the strategy is developed, agreed and followed.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - network and communicate with senior stakeholders across enterprises, and actively seek opportunities for improvement
> - support multiple communities and teams
> - find and use best practice
> - find and use emerging technologies and approaches
> - inspire other enterprise architects and help them understand how to meet organisational goals
> - horizon scan for external influences or risks
> - support successful delivery of the long-term strategy
> - be responsible for the overall direction of business and digital capabilities
> - be responsible for the creation, maintenance and consumption of a Digital Twin

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono, kanuni na mkakati wa usanifu wa taasisi, ulioambatana na mkakati wake wa afya na matunzo.
- Kuishauri timu ya watendaji na bodi kuhusu usanifu, hatari na thamani ya uwekezaji mkubwa wa kidijitali.
- Kuongoza mazoezi ya usanifu, usimamizi na viwango vya taasisi.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi katika majukwaa ya usanifu na ushirikiano wa mifumo ya taasisi mbalimbali.
- Kujenga uwezo wa usanifu katika taasisi, ikiwemo uajiri na njia za taaluma.
