# Mhandisi wa data

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Madaraja:** 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Data engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/data-engineer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [data engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2079755f-d809-49e6-8037-4de6180e54c0) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wahandisi wa data hujenga na kuendesha mifereji na majukwaa yanayohamisha data za afya na matunzo kutoka kwenye mifumo ya kitabibu na ya kiutendaji hadi mahali ambapo zinaweza kuchambuliwa na kutumika kwa usalama. Husanifu mtiririko wa data, huunganisha vyanzo vinavyotumia viwango tofauti, na kuhakikisha data ni sahihi, za wakati, salama, na zinapatikana tu kwa watu wanaostahili kuziona.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Mifumo chanzo hujumuisha rekodi za wagonjwa za kielektroniki, na mifumo ya maabara, picha za kitabibu na famasia, inayotumia viwango kama HL7 toleo la 2, HL7 FHIR, SNOMED CT na ICD.
- Mifereji mara nyingi hubeba data zinazotambulisha wagonjwa, hivyo wahandisi hujenga ndani ufichaji wa utambulisho kwa majina bandia, udhibiti wa ufikiaji na ukaguzi tangu mwanzo.
- Baadhi ya mitiririko ya data inaunga mkono matunzo ya moja kwa moja, kama vile tahadhari au orodha za wagonjwa, hivyo mfereji uliofeli au uliochelewa unaweza kuathiri wagonjwa na unahitaji tathmini ya usalama wa kitabibu.
- Data mara nyingi zinahitaji kuunganishwa katika mazingira ya matunzo, jambo linalotegemea ulinganishaji wa wagonjwa unaoaminika na vitambulisho thabiti.

## Daraja 6: Mhandisi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Data engineer**

> A data engineer delivers the designs set by more senior members of the data engineering community.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - implement data flows to connect operational systems, data for analytics and business intelligence (BI) systems
> - document source-to-target mappings
> - re-engineer manual data flows to enable scaling and repeatable use
> - support the build of data streaming systems
> - write ETL (extract, transform, load) scripts and code to ensure the ETL process performs optimally
> - develop business intelligence reports that can be reused
> - build accessible data for analysis

### Majukumu

- Kujenga na kutunza mifereji ya data kutoka mifumo ya kitabibu na ya kiutendaji hadi jukwaa la data, akifuata miundo iliyokubaliwa.
- Kuchora ramani ya data chanzo, ikiwemo data za kitabibu zenye misimbo, kwenda miundo lengwa na kuandika ramani hizo.
- Kujenga ufichaji wa utambulisho kwa majina bandia, uthibitishaji na ukaguzi wa ubora wa data ndani ya mifereji.
- Kufuatilia mifereji na kurekebisha hitilafu, akipa kipaumbele mitiririko inayounga mkono matunzo ya moja kwa moja.
- Kutumia udhibiti wa ufikiaji na uandishi wa kumbukumbu za ukaguzi unaotimiza kanuni za usimamizi wa taarifa.

## Daraja 7: Mhandisi mwandamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior data engineer**

> A senior data engineer designs and leads the implementation of data flows to connect operational systems, data for analytics and business intelligence (BI) systems.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - recognise opportunities to reuse existing data flows
> - lead the build of data streaming systems
> - optimise the code to ensure processes perform optimally
> - lead work on database management

- Kubuni mtiririko wa data na huduma za mtiririko wa moja kwa moja zinazoleta pamoja data kutoka mifumo mingi ya afya na matunzo.

- Kuongoza kazi ya utendaji wa hifadhidata na jukwaa ili data zipatikane huduma zinapozihitaji.
- Kutathmini hatari za usalama wa kitabibu na usimamizi wa taarifa za mtiririko wa data pamoja na wataalamu husika.

## Daraja 8a: Mhandisi kiongozi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead data engineer**

> A lead data engineer is responsible for the design and implementation of numerous complex data flows to connect operational systems, data for analytics and business intelligence (BI) systems.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - recognise and share opportunities to reuse existing data flows between teams
> - be responsible for the build of data-streaming systems
> - co-ordinate teams and set best practice and standards
> - apply knowledge of systems integration to your work
> - champion data engineering across government

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu na uendeshaji wa jukwaa la data la taasisi na mtiririko wake mwingi wa data.
- Kuweka viwango vya uhandisi kwa mifereji, majaribio, usalama na uandishi wa nyaraka.
- Kupanga uunganishaji wa data na washirika wa afya na matunzo, ikiwemo kumbukumbu za matunzo zinazoshirikiwa na huduma za data za kikanda.
- Kuhakikisha mtiririko wa data unaounga mkono matunzo ya moja kwa moja ni thabiti na una hoja za usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kuratibu timu za uhandisi wa data na kusimamia moja kwa moja wahandisi.

## Daraja 8b: Mkuu wa uhandisi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of data engineering**

> A head of data engineering leads multi-functional delivery teams to deliver robust data services for their department, other government departments and private sector partners.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - inspire best practice for data products and services within your teams
> - build data engineering capability by providing technical leadership and career development for the community
> - work with other senior team members to identify, plan, develop and deliver data services

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mkakati wa majukwaa ya data na huduma za uhandisi wa data za taasisi.
- Kuongoza timu za fani mbalimbali zinazotoa huduma za data kwa timu za ndani na washirika wa afya na matunzo.
- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu hatari, gharama na uwekezaji wa jukwaa la data.
- Kuhakikisha huduma za data zinatimiza mahitaji ya usimamizi wa taarifa, usalama na usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kujenga uwezo wa uhandisi wa data kupitia uajiri, maendeleo na njia za taaluma.
