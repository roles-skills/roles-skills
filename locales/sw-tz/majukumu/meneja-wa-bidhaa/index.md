# Meneja wa bidhaa

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Bidhaa na utoaji](../../#bidhaa-na-utoaji)  
**Madaraja:** 6, 7, 8a, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Product manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/product-manager/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT product manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/a79170ad-0b7b-4697-864f-1748359aa10a) (ISCO-08 1330); [product manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/9f508305-80ce-4111-8722-f2c9b4a44890) (ISCO-08 1223)

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa bidhaa huhakikisha bidhaa za afya za kidijitali za taasisi zinakidhi mahitaji ya wagonjwa, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo na kutoa thamani kwa mfumo wa afya na matunzo. Huweka maono na ramani ya njia ya bidhaa, huweka vipaumbele vya kazi ya timu za fani mbalimbali kwa ushahidi, na kuwajibika kwa matokeo ambayo bidhaa zao zinafikia, ikiwemo athari kwa matunzo.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Maamuzi ya bidhaa yanaweza kuathiri usalama wa mgonjwa, hivyo mameneja wa bidhaa humiliki hatari ya kitabibu ya bidhaa yao pamoja na afisa wa usalama wa kitabibu na kuhakikisha hatari zinasimamiwa kabla ya toleo.
- Bidhaa huhudumia makundi mengi ya watumiaji wenye mahitaji tofauti, kama vile wagonjwa, walezi, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa kiutawala, na mameneja wa bidhaa lazima wayasawazishe kwa haki.
- Baadhi ya bidhaa zinaweza kudhibitiwa kama vifaa tiba, hivyo mameneja wa bidhaa wanahitaji kujua ni lini udhibiti unatumika na kupanga kuuhusu.
- Matokeo katika matunzo hujumuisha usalama, ubora, ufikiaji, usawa na muda wa wafanyakazi, hivyo mameneja wa bidhaa hufafanua vipimo vya mafanikio ambavyo viongozi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji wanavitambua.
- Bidhaa mara nyingi hutegemea viwango vya data za afya vya kitaifa na kikanda na majukwaa yanayoshirikiwa, hivyo mameneja wa bidhaa hufanya kazi kuvuka mipaka ya taasisi.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A product manager makes sure that their products provide value and achieve the right outcomes by balancing user and business needs.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - work with multidisciplinary teams, bringing together their views, user needs and business needs, to frame the problem
> - work with multidisciplinary teams to provide the right outcomes for users and the organisation
> - make decisions on priorities for your teams
> - ensure teams comply with appropriate standards

## Daraja 6: Meneja msaidizi wa bidhaa

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate product manager**

> An associate product manager is responsible for features, components or part of a product, contributing to the delivery of value.
> 
> This can be an entry-level role for civil servants who are looking to move into a digital role or those on emerging talent schemes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with product managers to advocate for user needs throughout the organisation, prioritising delivery of greatest value
> - manage a smaller product with support, or a subset of features of a larger product
> - learn product management approaches and methods

### Majukumu

- Kumiliki orodha ya kazi ya kipengele au sehemu ya bidhaa, akifanya kazi na meneja wa bidhaa.
- Kugeuza ushahidi kutoka utafiti wa watumiaji na wagonjwa, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo kuwa hadithi za watumiaji zilizopewa vipaumbele.
- Kufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kurekodi na kukagua hatari za vipengele anavyovimiliki.
- Kufuatilia vipimo vya bidhaa, kama vile kupitishwa, viwango vya kukamilisha na simu za msaada, na kuripoti vinavyoonyesha.
- Kuwakilisha bidhaa kwenye maonyesho na kwa wadau wa kiutendaji.

## Daraja 7: Meneja wa bidhaa

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Product manager**

> A product manager is responsible for product quality and value, working with the team to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They might recruit and manage people in the product profession, ensuring people with the right skills are hired, supported and developed.
> 
> This can be an entry-level role for new product managers who have been working in other digital roles.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead a product through different phases of the product life cycle, from discovery, through development, delivery and continuous improvement, to retirement
> - support lead and senior product managers
> - prioritise work for your teams and be able to measure outcomes

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono, ramani ya njia na vipimo vya mafanikio kwa bidhaa ya afya ya kidijitali.
- Kuweka vipaumbele vya kazi ya timu, akisawazisha mahitaji ya watumiaji, usalama wa kitabibu, sera, vikwazo vya kiufundi na gharama.
- Kukubali hatari ya kitabibu kwa mabadiliko ya bidhaa pamoja na afisa wa usalama wa kitabibu, na kusimamisha matoleo yasiyo salama.
- Kufanya kazi na viongozi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji kuhakikisha bidhaa inalingana na njia za matunzo na inapitishwa.
- Kuhakikisha bidhaa inatimiza viwango vya ufikivu, usimamizi wa taarifa na huduma.
- Kupima matokeo ya bidhaa, ikiwemo manufaa yake kwa wagonjwa na wafanyakazi.

## Daraja 8a: Meneja mwandamizi wa bidhaa

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior product manager**

> A senior product manager can work on a group of products that share a value proposition, or a single product that is complex, high risk or sensitive. They are responsible for product quality and value, working with teams to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They might also be involved in parts of programme management activity.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead products through different phases of the product life cycle, from discovery, through development, delivery and continuous improvement, to retirement
> - make decisions based on the overall quality and value of your products
> - manage associate product managers and product managers
> - be involved in recruiting associate product managers, product managers and contractors

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza kundi la bidhaa zinazohusiana, au bidhaa moja tata, yenye hatari kubwa au nyeti, kama ile inayotumika katika huduma za dharura.
- Kujenga mkakati wa bidhaa kutoka ushahidi kuhusu mahitaji ya wagonjwa, mazoezi ya kitabibu na sera ya afya na matunzo.
- Kuhakikisha bidhaa inatimiza udhibiti wa vifaa tiba pale unapotumika.
- Kusimamia bajeti ya bidhaa na kutoa hoja ya uwekezaji kwa manufaa ya matunzo.
- Kuwaongoza na kuwasimamia moja kwa moja mameneja wa bidhaa.

## Daraja 8b: Meneja kiongozi wa bidhaa

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead product manager**

> A lead product manager is responsible for a product portfolio or end-to-end service, and the delivery of product quality and value. They work with teams to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They are a member of the management team for their product area and provide product leadership and direction across multiple teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage people and manage products
> - maintain the high level roadmap
> - be involved in a range of programme management activities relevant to the product portfolio
> - be involved in recruiting product managers at all levels and contractors
> - provide leadership in your programmes and across the local community, working closely with the head of product, where they exist

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza jalada la bidhaa au huduma kutoka mwanzo hadi mwisho, kama vile bidhaa zote zinazounga mkono njia moja ya matunzo.
- Kuweka mwelekeo wa bidhaa katika timu kadhaa na kuhakikisha bidhaa zinafanya kazi pamoja kwa wagonjwa na wafanyakazi.
- Kufanya kazi na viongozi wa kitabibu na afisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kusimamia hatari ya kitabibu katika jalada.
- Kushikilia bajeti ya jalada na kuripoti manufaa kwa bodi za juu za usimamizi.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza mameneja wa bidhaa katika eneo la bidhaa.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa bidhaa

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of product**

> A head of product provides support to all product managers in the organisation and to the product community across government. They lead on product strategy, working closely with lead product managers and their business units. They are responsible for setting and raising standards for product management in their organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - coach others in product management
> - work with organisation management and other communities to represent product community interests
> - oversee every member of the community, including their role, assignments, development, pay and performance
> - manage recruitment, development, moves and exits from the community, covering civil servants and contractors
> - line manage senior and lead product managers

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mkakati wa bidhaa wa taasisi, akifanya kazi na viongozi watendaji, wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji.
- Kuweka na kuinua viwango vya usimamizi wa bidhaa, ikiwemo jinsi bidhaa zinavyosimamia hatari ya kitabibu na kupima matokeo.
- Kuongoza taaluma ya bidhaa, ikiwemo uajiri, njia za taaluma na kujenga uwezo.
- Kuamua vipaumbele vya uwekezaji katika bidhaa, akitumia ushahidi wa hitaji, thamani na usawa.
- Kuwakilisha bidhaa za taasisi kwa washirika katika mfumo wa afya na matunzo.
