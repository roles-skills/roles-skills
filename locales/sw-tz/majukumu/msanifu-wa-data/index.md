# Msanifu wa data

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 8a, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Data architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/data-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [database designer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/8d9ec84d-cf2d-4179-87bc-335cda54a427) (ISCO-08 2521); [data warehouse designer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/1562c7a3-c7d9-419d-b9b6-db26610bcf84) (ISCO-08 2521)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa data husanifu jinsi taasisi inavyopanga, kuhifadhi, kuhamisha na kusimamia data zake za afya na matunzo, ili ziweze kutumika kwa usalama kwa matunzo ya moja kwa moja, mipango ya huduma na utafiti. Husanifu miundo ya data, majukwaa ya data na mtiririko wa data, huweka viwango vya data, na kuhakikisha maana ya kitabibu, ubora na usiri vinatunzwa kutoka mwanzo hadi mwisho.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Data za afya lazima zitunze maana yake ya kitabibu, hivyo miundo ya data hutumia istilahi za kitabibu kama SNOMED CT na uainishaji kama ICD.
- Data zinazotumika kwa matunzo ya moja kwa moja na data zinazotumika kwa mipango au utafiti zina misingi tofauti ya kisheria, hivyo wasanifu husanifu kwa ajili ya utenganishaji, kuficha utambulisho na ufikiaji unaodhibitiwa.
- Data hutoka kwenye mifumo mingi ya kitabibu na ya matunzo yenye ubora tofauti, hivyo wasanifu husanifu kwa ajili ya ukaguzi wa ubora wa data, ulinganishaji wa wagonjwa na ufuatiliaji wa asili ya data.
- Kumbukumbu za matunzo zinazoshirikiwa na kubadilishana data hutegemea miundo ya pamoja kama rasilimali za HL7 FHIR na seti za data zilizokubaliwa za kitaifa au za kimataifa.
- Kumbukumbu lazima zihifadhiwe kwa vipindi virefu vya uhifadhi, hivyo miundo lazima ipange kwa ajili ya kuhifadhi kwenye jalada na kufikia data za kihistoria.

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Data architect**

> A data architect designs and builds data models to fulfil the strategic data needs of the organisation, as defined by chief data architects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - design, support and provide guidance for the upgrade, management, decommission and archive of data in compliance with data policy
> - provide input into data dictionaries
> - define and maintain the data technology architecture, including metadata, integration and business intelligence or data warehouse architecture

### Majukumu

- Kubuni miundo ya kimantiki na ya kimwili ya data kwa data za kitabibu na za kiutendaji, akitumia istilahi za kitabibu inapohitajika.
- Kubuni mtiririko wa data na vijenzi vya jukwaa la data, kama vile ghala, ziwa-ghala na tabaka za uunganishaji.
- Kufafanua metadata, maingizo ya kamusi ya data na asili ya data kwa seti za data za eneo lake.
- Kubuni udhibiti wa kuficha utambulisho na ufikiaji ili data zitumike tu kwa kusudi lake halali.
- Kufanya kazi na wenzake wa ubora wa data na uwekaji misimbo ya kitabibu kurekebisha visababishi vya kimuundo vya data duni.
- Kuwaongoza wahandisi wa data na wachambuzi kuhusu miundo ya data na viwango.

## Daraja 8b: Msanifu mwandamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior data architect**

> A senior data architect delivers the vision for the organisation as set by the chief data architect.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - design data models and metadata systems
> - help chief data architects to interpret an organisation’s needs
> - provide oversight and advice to other data architects who are designing and producing data artefacts
> - design and support the management of data dictionaries
> - make sure that your teams are working to the standards set for the organisation by the chief data architects
> - work with technical architects to make sure that an organisation’s systems are designed in accordance with the appropriate data architecture

### Majukumu

- Kubuni usanifu wa data kwa eneo kuu, kama vile kumbukumbu ya matunzo inayoshirikiwa au jukwaa la uchanganuzi.
- Kuhakikisha miundo ya data katika timu zote inafuata viwango na miundo ya data ya taasisi.
- Kubuni jinsi data zinavyoshirikishwa na taasisi washirika, ikiwemo viwango, udhibiti na makubaliano ya ushirikishaji wa data.
- Kushauri wenzake wa usimamizi wa taarifa na usalama wa kitabibu kuhusu hatari za data za miundo mipya.
- Kukagua na kuongoza kazi ya wasanifu wa data.

## Daraja 8c: Msanifu mkuu wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Chief data architect**

> A chief data architect sets the vision for the organisation’s use of data as directed by the appropriate governance body.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - oversee the design of multiple data models and have a broad understanding of how each model fulfils the needs of the organisation
> - be accountable for supporting and aligning to the organisation’s data strategy
> - champion data architecture both internally and through collaborating and communicating at the most senior levels across government
> - set the standards and ways of working for the data architecture community
> - be accountable for assuring data models at the level of a project or enterprise
> - provide advice to project teams and oversee the management of the full data product life cycle
> - be responsible for ensuring that the organisation’s systems are designed in accordance with the enterprise data architecture

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono na ramani ya njia ya usanifu wa data ya taasisi, kulingana na mkakati wake wa data.
- Kumiliki muundo wa data wa taasisi nzima, viwango vya data na kamusi ya data.
- Kushauri timu ya watendaji na vyombo vya usimamizi wa data kuhusu usanifu wa data, hatari na uwekezaji.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi katika kazi ya viwango vya data na ushirikiano wa mifumo ya taasisi mbalimbali.
- Kuongoza mazoezi ya usanifu wa data na kuendeleza watu wake.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Uzoefu mpana wa kuongoza usanifu wa data katika taasisi mbalimbali.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi wa kitaalamu, pamoja na uelewa mpana wa taasisi na sekta.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Huweka mkakati wa kitengo; anawajibika kwa mkurugenzi.
- **Wigo:** Kitengo au idara.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza kitengo kupitia ngazi kadhaa za usimamizi.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utendaji wa kitengo, nguvu kazi yake, na bajeti yake.
