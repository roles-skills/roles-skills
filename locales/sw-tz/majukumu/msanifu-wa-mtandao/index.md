# Msanifu wa mtandao

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu](../../#usanifu)  
**Madaraja:** 6, 7, 8a  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Network architect](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/network-architect/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT network architect](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/e0b544dd-b621-4126-a55e-054af25c6ea0) (ISCO-08 2523)

## Muhtasari

Wasanifu wa mtandao husanifu mitandao ya ndani, ya eneo pana, isiyo na waya na ya wingu inayounganisha maeneo ya taasisi, wafanyakazi, mifumo ya kitabibu na vifaa tiba. Huhakikisha mitandao ni ya haraka, thabiti na salama vya kutosha kwa matunzo yasiyokoma, na hupanga jinsi mitandao inavyobadilika huduma zinapohamia kwenye wingu na kuunganishwa na taasisi washirika.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Mifumo ya kitabibu, simu na vifaa tiba hutegemea mtandao, hivyo kukatika kunaweza kusimamisha matunzo; miundo inahitaji ustahimilivu na kuhamia mfumo mbadala kulikojaribiwa.
- Mitandao mara nyingi hubeba vifaa tiba vilivyounganishwa ambavyo ni vigumu kuviwekea marekebisho, hivyo wasanifu husanifu ugawaji na ufuatiliaji kuvilinda.
- Wafanyakazi hufanya kazi hospitalini, kliniki, mazingira ya jamii na nyumbani kwa wagonjwa, hivyo miundo lazima iunge mkono ufikiaji salama wa simu na wa mbali.
- Mitandao huunganishwa na mitandao ya afya na matunzo inayoshirikiwa na taasisi washirika, ambazo huweka mahitaji yao ya muunganisho na usalama.
- Mabadiliko katika maeneo ya kitabibu yanahitaji mipango makini kuzunguka matunzo ya wagonjwa, kwa mfano kupanga kazi nje ya vipindi vyenye shughuli nyingi.

## Daraja 6: Msanifu msaidizi wa mtandao

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate network architect**

> An associate network architect works with network architects on designs and specifications, with guidance.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be aware and knowledgeable of new technology solutions and produce network design policy
> - draft recommendations for network support, creating and maintaining network plans and planning infrastructure runs
> - ensure designs meet SLAs (service level agreements) and develop an understanding of common tools
> - have an understanding of one or two of the following network technologies: LAN (local area network), WAN (wide area network), wifi, data centre LAN, SD (software-defined) networks, telephony, mobile solutions infrastructure, unified comms, network management solutions, network specific security and remote access technologies and approaches
> - collaborate with engineers to ensure systems are practically designed

### Majukumu

- Kuandaa rasimu za miundo ya mtandao, michoro na vipimo kwa mwongozo wa wasanifu wa mtandao.
- Kukagua maeneo na kukusanya mahitaji ya maeneo ya kitabibu, ikiwemo wigo wa mtandao usio na waya na miunganisho ya vifaa.
- Kuweka rekodi za muundo wa mtandao, anwani na vifaa vilivyounganishwa zikiwa za kisasa.
- Kukagua kama miundo inatimiza viwango vya huduma na usalama kabla ya ukaguzi.

- **Uongozi:** Anaweza kuongoza timu ndogo au kuwa mshauri wa wenzake.

## Daraja 7: Msanifu wa mtandao

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Network architect**

> A network architect has responsibility for network designs and specifications to support business strategies using common tools.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - research new technologies and solutions, and ensure appropriate levels of assurance
> - plan, direct and co-ordinate activities, engaging with other stakeholders to manage and implement a programme
> - agree SLAs (service level agreements) and engage with third party vendors
> - have an understanding of most of the following network technologies: LAN (local area network), WAN (wide area network), wifi, data-centre LAN, SD (software-defined) networks, telephony, mobile solutions infrastructure, unified comms, network management solutions, network-specific security and remote access technologies and approaches
> - coach and mentor associate network architects where appropriate
> - collaborate with engineers to ensure systems are practically designed

### Majukumu

- Kubuni suluhisho za mtandao wa ndani, wa eneo pana, usio na waya na wa wingu kwa maeneo na huduma.
- Kubuni ugawaji wa mtandao kwa vifaa tiba, mifumo ya kitabibu na ufikiaji wa wageni.
- Kupanga mabadiliko ya mtandao katika maeneo ya kitabibu pamoja na viongozi wa huduma ili matunzo yasivurugike.
- Kukubaliana viwango vya huduma na wasambazaji na mahitaji ya muunganisho na mitandao ya washirika.
- Kutathmini teknolojia mpya za mtandao na kufaa kwake na viwango vya usalama vya taasisi.

## Daraja 8a: Msanifu kiongozi wa mtandao

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead network architect**

> A lead network architect is accountable for designs and networks representing their business area.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be a point of escalation
> - actively input into governance forums that determine IT strategy
> - cascade messages, standards and best practice from other architecture teams and design authorities to the network architecture team
> - act as a subject matter expert for network architects
> - represent network architects internally and externally
> - have an understanding of all network technology including LAN (local area network), WAN (wide area network), wifi, data centre LAN, SD (software-defined) networks, telephony, mobile solutions infrastructure, unified comms, network management solutions, and network specific security and remote access technologies and approaches
> - champion new technologies and solutions
> - coach and mentor network architects and associate network architects
> - be a strategic thinker who can communicate network concepts to technical and non-technical stakeholders

### Majukumu

- Kumiliki usanifu wa mtandao wa taasisi, ikiwemo maeneo yake, vituo vya data na miunganisho ya wingu.
- Kuweka viwango vya usanifu wa mtandao na kuhakikisha miundo katika mamlaka ya usanifu.
- Kubuni ustahimilivu na urejeshaji wa mtandao kwa huduma muhimu za kitabibu, na kuhakikisha unajaribiwa.
- Kushauri kuhusu uwekezaji wa mtandao, mikataba ya wasambazaji na hatari za mtandao za huduma mpya.
- Kuwa kituo cha kitaalamu cha kupeleka masuala juu na kuwaongoza wasanifu na wahandisi wengine wa mtandao.
