# Meneja wa majaribio

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa majaribio hupanga, huongoza na kuhakikisha majaribio ya huduma na programu za afya za kidijitali za taasisi. Huamua ni majaribio kiasi gani yanahitajika na ya aina gani, husimamia timu za majaribio, mazingira na wasambazaji, na kuwaambia wafanya maamuzi wazi hatari zipi zinabaki kabla huduma haijaanza kutumika. Katika huduma za afya hii inajumuisha kuhakikisha majaribio yanaunga mkono hoja ya usalama wa kitabibu na mahitaji yoyote ya udhibiti.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Maamuzi ya kuanza kutumika kwa mifumo ya kitabibu hutegemea ushahidi wa majaribio na hatari zilizobaki, ambazo mameneja wa majaribio huripoti kwa wamiliki wa bidhaa, maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu na bodi za programu.
- Mabadiliko makubwa, kama vile masasisho ya rekodi ya mgonjwa ya kielektroniki, yanahitaji majaribio katika idara, mifumo na wasambazaji wengi, na mara nyingi muda wa wafanyakazi wa kitabibu kwa majaribio ya ukubalifu.
- Majaribio lazima yalingane na huduma za kitabibu, kama vile kupanga majaribio ya mpito na mazoezi kamili ili kupunguza usumbufu kwa matunzo ya wagonjwa.
- Programu inayodhibitiwa na mabadiliko yanayohusu usalama yanahitaji majaribio yanayotimiza viwango kama IEC 62304 na mchakato wa usimamizi wa hatari za kitabibu wa taasisi.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A test manager is responsible for planning, monitoring and documenting testing. They implement a test strategy to ensure quality principles are delivered on time and within budget.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - create and implement test plans and approaches to mitigate risk
> - monitor and control test activities, ensuring compliance with standards
> - communicate risks and assess and report timely test status’ to stakeholders
> - define metrics for monitoring and controlling test activities
> - provide direction on complex testing issues, outlining and influencing delivery of projects and programmes
> - identify opportunities for continuous improvement
> - lead and support teams, providing coaching in good testing practices

## Daraja 7: Meneja wa majaribio

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Test manager**

> A test manager works with a team to create appropriate test approaches and plans, considering environments, data, tools, resources, timelines, requirements and risk. They contribute to test strategies and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead and support a team in testing activities
> - determine and apply appropriate test types, techniques and standards to mitigate risk early
> - manage, maintain and continuously improve test activities, ensuring compliance with regulatory and organisational standards
> - identify and communicate risks
> - oversee the reporting and recording of outcomes, including defects
> - contribute to and agree testing standards, and enable others to follow them

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza mipango ya majaribio ya bidhaa au programu, inayohusu mazingira, data, zana, rasilimali, ratiba na hatari ya kitabibu.
- Kupanga majaribio ya ukubalifu wa watumiaji pamoja na viongozi wa kitabibu na wa kiutendaji, na kupata muda wa madaktari kushiriki.
- Kusimamia timu ya wachambuzi na wahandisi wa majaribio, ikiwemo wajaribu wa wasambazaji.
- Kuhakikisha majaribio yanatimiza mahitaji ya usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na udhibiti.
- Kuripoti maendeleo ya majaribio, kasoro na hatari zilizobaki kwa wamiliki wa bidhaa, maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu na bodi za programu.
- Kuboresha michakato ya majaribio na kukubaliana viwango vya majaribio na timu za utoaji.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Uzoefu mkubwa wa usimamizi wa majaribio kwa huduma tata za kidijitali, katika kiwango kinacholingana na shahada ya uzamili.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi maalumu uliokuzwa sana, kwa kawaida hadi kiwango cha shahada ya uzamili au uzoefu unaolingana.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Hufanya kazi kwa sera ya taasisi; huamua jinsi matokeo yanavyofikiwa; ndiye mtaalamu wanayemshauriana naye wengine.
- **Wigo:** Bidhaa au huduma kadhaa, au kitengo maalumu.
- **Uongozi:** Huongoza timu au eneo la mazoezi ya kitaaluma.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utoaji wa huduma au kitengo maalumu, na bajeti yake ikiwa anaisimamia.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa majaribio

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of test**

> A head of test sets the strategy for test quality and influences testing practices across a broad area. They develop, monitor and evaluate testing standards, practices, methods and specifications, and make strategic decisions that improve testing in an organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead and influence testing activities to ensure the organisation implements quality test frameworks
> - select and guide the implementation of testing tools and technologies
> - define good practices, standards and ways of working, continuously improving testing capability
> - escalate risks to senior stakeholders
> - contribute to cross-government test communities to improve test practices
> - lead the organisation in adhering to test industry compliance and regulatory standards

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mkakati, viwango na mifumo ya majaribio ya taasisi kwa huduma za afya za kidijitali.
- Kuhakikisha majaribio ya taasisi yanatimiza mahitaji ya usalama wa kitabibu, vifaa tiba na udhibiti mwingine.
- Kuchagua zana na teknolojia za majaribio, na kusimamia bajeti ya majaribio na mikataba ya wasambazaji.
- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu hatari za ubora na usalama kabla ya matoleo makubwa na maamuzi ya kuanza kutumika.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza mameneja, wachambuzi na wahandisi wa majaribio, na kujenga uwezo wa majaribio wa taasisi.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- **Uongozi:** Huongoza kitengo kupitia ngazi kadhaa za usimamizi.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Utendaji wa kitengo, nguvu kazi yake, na bajeti yake.
