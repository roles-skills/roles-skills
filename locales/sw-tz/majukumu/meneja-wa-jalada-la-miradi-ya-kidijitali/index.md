# Meneja wa jalada la miradi ya kidijitali

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Bidhaa na utoaji](../../#bidhaa-na-utoaji)  
**Madaraja:** 5, 6, 8a, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Digital portfolio manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/digital-portfolio-manager/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [programme manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/c94f3565-d987-431f-a46b-6f4e018e0bd7) (ISCO-08 1213)

## Muhtasari

Mameneja wa jalada la miradi ya kidijitali huisaidia taasisi kuchagua, kufuatilia na kusimamia jalada lake la miradi, programu na bidhaa za kidijitali ili iwekeze katika kazi inayowanufaisha zaidi wagonjwa, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo. Huendesha usimamizi na kuripoti wa jalada, huwasaidia viongozi kuweka vipaumbele kwa ushahidi mzuri, na kuhakikisha jalada linatoa manufaa lililoahidi.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Kazi ya kidijitali inashindania ufadhili na matunzo ya mstari wa mbele, hivyo mameneja wa jalada huwasaidia viongozi kuonyesha manufaa ya uwekezaji wa kidijitali kwa wagonjwa na wafanyakazi.
- Usimamizi wa jalada lazima ujumuishe uhakikisho wa usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na usalama wa mtandao, hivyo mameneja wa jalada huvifuatilia pamoja na muda, gharama na wigo.
- Miradi mingi ya kidijitali hutegemea uwezo wa timu za kitabibu na za kiutendaji, kama vile madaktari wa kujaribu au wafanyakazi wa kufunzwa, hivyo mameneja wa jalada hutazama migongano ya mahitaji kwa huduma.
- Ufadhili mara nyingi hutoka vyanzo kadhaa vyenye kanuni na tarehe zao za kuripoti, hivyo mameneja wa jalada huweka kuripoti kuwa sahihi na kwa wakati.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A digital portfolio manager co-ordinates and manages a group , or ‘portfolio’, of digital projects. In this role, you will use an iterative and flexible approach to help define governance processes for this portfolio.

## Daraja 5: Mchambuzi wa jalada la miradi ya kidijitali

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Digital portfolio analyst**

> A digital portfolio analyst supports the tracking, analysis and delivery of outcomes across a portfolio of digital projects. This role level may be called ‘associate digital portfolio manager’ in your organisation.
> 
> As you will be learning basic portfolio management skills, you will report to a more senior portfolio manager.

### Majukumu

- Kukusanya na kukagua data za hali, hatari, fedha na manufaa kutoka miradi katika jalada.
- Kutunza sajili ya jalada na dashibodi, na kutambua mapengo au kutolingana katika kuripoti.
- Kuandaa nyaraka na ripoti kwa bodi za usimamizi wa jalada.
- Kufuatilia hatua muhimu za uhakikisho, kama vile idhini ya usalama wa kitabibu na tathmini za athari za ulinzi wa data, katika miradi.
- Kuwasaidia mameneja wa jalada kwa uchambuzi wa vipaumbele na mipango.

## Daraja 6: Meneja wa jalada la miradi ya kidijitali

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Digital portfolio manager**

> A digital portfolio manager helps to co-ordinate and manage a portfolio of digital projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - help to create environments that enable teams to deliver
> - support the prioritisation of a group of projects and programmes
> - help teams to follow governance and delivery processes
> - help stakeholders understand the performance of work and any related risks
> - support more senior portfolio managers and leadership in making improvements

### Majukumu

- Kuratibu jalada la miradi na programu za kidijitali, kama vile kazi zote za kidijitali za kurugenzi moja.
- Kuzisaidia timu kufuata michakato ya usimamizi, uhakikisho na utoaji, na kuifanya iwe sawia.
- Kuwasaidia viongozi kuweka vipaumbele vya kazi kwa kutumia ushahidi wa hitaji, manufaa, hatari na uwezo.
- Kuripoti utendaji wa jalada, hatari na manufaa kwa uwazi kwa bodi na wadau.
- Kutambua migongano ya mahitaji kwa timu za kitabibu na za kiutendaji katika miradi na kusaidia kuitatua.

## Daraja 8a: Meneja mwandamizi wa jalada la miradi ya kidijitali

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior digital portfolio manager**

> A senior digital portfolio manager is responsible for co-ordinating and managing a portfolio of digital projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - track, analyse and help deliver outcomes in your portfolio
> - give guidance and deal with issues to help teams to deliver faster
> - manage governance and assurance processes, and ensure standards are met
> - provide information to stakeholders on work performance and related risks

### Majukumu

- Kusimamia jalada la kidijitali la taasisi, au sehemu kubwa yake, na michakato yake ya usimamizi na uhakikisho.
- Kufuatilia na kuchambua matokeo na manufaa katika jalada, na kuchukua hatua juu ya masuala ili kusaidia timu kutoa.
- Kuhakikisha jalada linatimiza viwango, ikiwemo uhakikisho wa usalama wa kitabibu, usimamizi wa taarifa na usalama wa mtandao.
- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu vipaumbele, uwekezaji na maridhiano, kwa ushahidi ulio wazi.
- Kusimamia kuripoti kwa jalada kwa wafadhili na vyombo vya nje.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja wachambuzi na mameneja wa jalada.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa jalada la kidijitali

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of portfolio**

> A head of portfolio leads a team of digital portfolio managers. You will ensure the portfolio of digital projects delivers the best possible outcomes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead the tracking and governing of digital projects
> - ensure the right controls are used
> - address risks and deal with, or escalate, issues
> - provide leaders with insights into the portfolio's future so they can make informed decisions
> - build and lead a diverse, skilled and motivated team of digital portfolio managers
> - ensure the portfolio team is meeting the needs of the organisation
> - support the professional development of the portfolio community, including line management and coaching

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza kitengo cha jalada la kidijitali na timu ya mameneja wa jalada.
- Kuweka mkakati, udhibiti na usimamizi wa jalada, na kuhakikisha vinatumika.
- Kuwapa viongozi watendaji uelewa wa mustakabali wa jalada, ili wafanye maamuzi ya uwekezaji.
- Kuhakikisha manufaa ya jalada kwa wagonjwa, wafanyakazi na taasisi yanafikiwa na kuripotiwa.
- Kushughulikia hatari za ngazi ya jalada na kupeleka masuala kwa timu ya watendaji.
- Kusimamia bajeti na uwezo wa kitengo.
