# Mtaalamu wa ufikivu

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu unaomlenga mtumiaji](../../#usanifu-unaomlenga-mtumiaji)  
**Madaraja:** 5, 6, 7, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Accessibility specialist](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/accessibility-specialist/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [ICT accessibility tester](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/50af07f0-7a75-424e-a66d-5a9deea10f4c) (ISCO-08 2519)

## Muhtasari

Wataalamu wa ufikivu husaidia taasisi kufanya huduma zake za afya za kidijitali ziweze kutumiwa na watu wengi iwezekanavyo, wakiwemo watu wenye ulemavu na watu wenye magonjwa ya muda mrefu. Hujaribu huduma dhidi ya viwango vya ufikivu na kwa teknolojia saidizi, hushauri na kuzifunza timu, na kusaidia kuingiza ufikivu katika jinsi huduma, mifumo ya kitabibu na taarifa za afya zinavyobuniwa, kununuliwa na kujengwa.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Watu wengi wanaohitaji huduma za afya na matunzo zaidi ni walemavu, wazee, au wanaoishi na magonjwa ya muda mrefu, hivyo huduma zisizofikika zinaweza kuwazuia watu kupata matunzo.
- Madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo pia wana mahitaji ya ufikiaji, hivyo mifumo ya ndani ya kitabibu, ikiwemo ile inayonunuliwa kutoka kwa wasambazaji, lazima ifikike pia.
- Taarifa za afya mara nyingi ni tata na huchapishwa katika miundo mingi, kama vile nyaraka, barua na video, hivyo wataalamu huzisaidia timu kuzifanya zote zifikike, ikiwemo muundo rahisi kusoma na miundo mbadala.
- Matatizo ya ufikivu katika mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu yanaweza kuwa matatizo ya usalama, kwa mfano pale onyo halitangazwi na kisoma skrini, hivyo wataalamu hufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kuhusu hatari hizi.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> An accessibility specialist provides support, advice and guidance to other roles in the Government Digital and Data profession about how to create accessible digital services.
> 
> You will use knowledge of accessibility guidelines, provide technical expertise and bring the voice of disabled users to ensure that product teams make digital services that can be used by everyone.

## Daraja 5: Mtaalamu mdogo wa ufikivu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Junior accessibility specialist**

> A junior accessibility specialist learns on the job by assisting with accessibility testing.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - develop skills while working under supervision to provide accessibility testing
> - understand and use different types of testing
> - be aware of but not responsible for assistive technology testing
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government accessibility community

### Majukumu

- Kujaribu kurasa za wavuti, programu tumizi na nyaraka dhidi ya viwango vya ufikivu, chini ya usimamizi.
- Kurekodi na kueleza matatizo ya ufikivu kwa uwazi ili timu ziyarekebishe.
- Kusaidia majaribio kwa teknolojia saidizi, kama vile visoma skrini na vikuzaji.
- Kusaidia kuweka matamko ya ufikivu na kumbukumbu za matatizo yakiwa ya kisasa.

### Sifa na uzoefu wa kawaida

- Uzoefu fulani wa majaribio ya kidijitali, usanifu au uundaji, au uzoefu unaolingana.

### Muhtasari wa daraja

- **Maarifa:** Ujuzi wa kitaaluma au kiufundi, kwa kawaida kutokana na shahada au uzoefu unaolingana.
- **Uhuru wa kutenda:** Hufanya kazi kuelekea malengo mapana ndani ya viwango vya kitaaluma; hupanga kazi yake mwenyewe.
- **Wigo:** Kazi yake ya kitaaluma ndani ya timu au bidhaa.
- **Uongozi:** Anaweza kuwaongoza na kukagua kazi za wafanyakazi wa msaada na wanagenzi.
- **Uwajibikaji:** Ubora wa kazi yake ya kitaaluma.

## Daraja 6: Mtaalamu wa ufikivu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Accessibility specialist**

> An accessibility specialist can support teams in creating accessible services.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - coach and mentor more junior colleagues
> - support in providing training materials and learning content
> - perform accessibility audits
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government accessibility community

### Majukumu

- Kufanya ukaguzi wa ufikivu wa huduma zinazowalenga wagonjwa, mifumo ya kitabibu na taarifa za afya.
- Kujaribu kwa teknolojia saidizi mbalimbali na kusaidia utafiti na watumiaji wenye ulemavu.
- Kushauri timu kuhusu marekebisho na mifumo inayofikika ya fomu, tahadhari na taarifa zinazohusu usalama.
- Kukagua bidhaa za wasambazaji kwa ufikivu wakati wa ununuzi na ukubalifu.
- Kuunda mafunzo na miongozo, na kuwaongoza wenzake wadogo.

## Daraja 7: Mtaalamu mwandamizi wa ufikivu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior accessibility specialist**

> A senior accessibility specialist works with people at a range of role levels to embed accessibility into the output of teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - coach and mentor more junior colleagues
> - input into accessibility strategy with business areas or teams
> - create and deliver accessibility training
> - engage with teams across different levels and capabilities
> - potentially manage or lead individuals or sub-teams
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government accessibility community

### Majukumu

- Kuingiza ufikivu katika njia za kufanya kazi za timu, kuanzia ugunduzi hadi huduma ya moja kwa moja.
- Kuchangia mkakati wa ufikivu, ikiwemo jinsi ufikivu unavyohakikishwa katika ununuzi na tathmini za huduma.
- Kufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu pale matatizo ya ufikivu yanapoweza kuathiri usalama wa mgonjwa.
- Kuunda na kutoa mafunzo ya ufikivu kwa wabunifu, waendelezaji, wabunifu wa maudhui na wasambazaji.
- Kuongoza au kusimamia wataalamu wengine wa ufikivu.

## Daraja 8b: Mkuu wa ufikivu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of accessibility**

> A head of accessibility is an expert practitioner who can create an accessibility vision or strategy for an organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - define and assure best practice while influencing, leading and mentoring others
> - influence and develop organisational strategy and priorities, working with counterpart colleagues across government
> - ensure the right conditions and environment for accessibility specialists to work effectively
> - develop departmental capability by supporting internal movement of staff between roles, shaping career paths and recruiting talent
> - understand and own the overall approach to accessibility risk (including how accessibility risk is communicated and how corporate legal compliance risk is managed within the organisation)
> - engage with and contribute to the cross-government accessibility community

### Majukumu

- Kuweka maono, mkakati na viwango vya ufikivu vya taasisi, kwa huduma zinazowalenga wagonjwa na za ndani.
- Kuhakikisha utiifu wa sheria na viwango vya ufikivu katika huduma zote, na kuripoti kuhusu hilo kwa viongozi wakuu.
- Kuhakikisha mahitaji ya ufikivu ni sehemu ya ununuzi na mikataba ya wasambazaji wa mifumo ya kitabibu.
- Kushawishi vipaumbele vya taasisi ili wagonjwa na wafanyakazi wenye ulemavu wasitengwe.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza timu ya ufikivu na jumuiya ya mazoezi.
