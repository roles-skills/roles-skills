# Msanidi programu

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Uundaji wa programu](../../#uundaji-wa-programu)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Software developer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/software-developer/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [software developer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/f2b15a0e-e65a-438a-affb-29b9d50b77d1) (ISCO-08 2512); [ICT application developer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/bd272aee-adc9-4a06-a15c-a73b4b4a46a7) (ISCO-08 2514)

## Muhtasari

Wasanidi wa programu hujenga, hujaribu na kutunza programu iliyo nyuma ya huduma za afya za kidijitali za taasisi, kama vile programu tumizi zinazowalenga wagonjwa, mifumo ya kitabibu, huduma za uunganishaji na majukwaa ya data. Hufanya kazi katika timu za bidhaa za fani mbalimbali, huandika msimbo salama, wa kuhifadhiwa, unaofikika na unaotunzika, na kusaidia huduma zao katika uendeshaji wa moja kwa moja.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Programu inaweza kuathiri matunzo ya wagonjwa, hivyo wasanidi hufuata mchakato wa usimamizi wa hatari za kitabibu na kuchangia ushahidi kwa hoja za usalama.
- Msimbo mara nyingi hushughulikia taarifa za siri za afya, hivyo wasanidi hutumia usimamizi wa taarifa na mazoezi ya uandishi salama wa msimbo kama kawaida.
- Huduma nyingi hubadilishana data na mifumo mingine ya afya na matunzo, kwa kutumia viwango huria vya ushirikiano wa mifumo kama HL7 FHIR.
- Baadhi ya programu zinaweza kudhibitiwa kama kifaa tiba, hivyo wasanidi wanahitaji kujua ni lini kuomba ushauri wa udhibiti.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A software developer designs, runs and improves software that meets user needs.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - be responsible for writing clean, secure code following a test-driven approach
> - create code that is open by default and easy for others to reuse

## Daraja 4: Mwanagenzi msanidi wa programu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Apprentice developer**

> An apprentice developer attends certified training and develops skills on the job.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - spend a substantial portion of time shadowing others
> - work with other developers to write code and tests
> - build your knowledge
> - follow a test-driven approach
> - write code that is automatically tested

### Majukumu

- Kujifunza lugha, zana na njia za kufanya kazi za timu kupitia mafunzo yaliyopangwa na kufanya kazi kwa jozi.

- Kufuata taratibu za uandishi salama wa msimbo, usimamizi wa taarifa na usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kushiriki katika taratibu za timu kama vile mikutano ya kusimama, mipango na tafakari.
- Kujitahidi kupata uanagenzi au sifa inayolingana.

> A junior developer learns on the job by delivering software components.

## Daraja 6: Msanidi wa programu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Developer**

> A developer delivers software components that form part of a product.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - develop software to meet user needs
> - follow best practice guidelines and help to improve those guidelines
> - write clean, secure and well-tested code
> - coach and mentor more junior colleagues
> - operate the services you build and identify issues in production

### Majukumu

- Kubuni, kujenga na kujaribu vipengele na huduma kwa kujitegemea, akichagua njia zinazofaa.
- Kukagua msimbo na kusaidia kutunza viwango vya uhandisi vya timu.

- Kushiriki katika utambuzi wa hatari na kusaidia kuweka udhibiti wa usalama wa kitabibu ndani ya msimbo na majaribio.
- Kuchunguza na kurekebisha matatizo katika huduma za moja kwa moja, ikiwemo msaada nje ya saa za kazi pale huduma inapohitaji.

## Daraja 7: Msanidi mwandamizi wa programu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior developer**

> A senior developer delivers and integrates software to form a complete service.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - plan and lead development on sets of related stories
> - have an understanding of the whole system and take responsibility for teaching this to others
> - work with other disciplines to understand what needs to be built
> - coach and mentor more junior colleagues
> - operate the production services you build
> - find ways to improve system robustness, resilience and stability
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza usanifu wa kiufundi na utoaji wa vipengele na huduma muhimu.
- Kuweka na kutunza viwango vya uhandisi vya timu, ikiwemo majaribio, usalama na ufikivu.
- Kufanya maamuzi ya kiufundi yanayosawazisha mahitaji ya watumiaji, usalama wa kitabibu, usalama na gharama.
- Kuongoza uchunguzi wa matukio kwa huduma za timu na kusukuma marekebisho.
- Kufanya kazi na maafisa wa usalama wa kitabibu kuhakikisha udhibiti wa usalama umebuniwa ndani na kujaribiwa.
- Kuwaongoza na kuwaendeleza wasanidi katika timu.

## Daraja 8a: Msanidi kiongozi wa programu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead developer**

> A lead developer leads development on one or a small number of related project teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - guide the ways in which the team works
> - be proficient in a wide range of technical systems
> - be involved in identifying appropriate technology and approaches, deciding when software should be written
> - contribute to the developer community
> - provide technical leadership, coaching and mentoring your team
> - promote knowledge sharing and adoption of good practice
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza uhandisi wa bidhaa au kundi la timu, akiweka mwelekeo wa kiufundi.
- Kuhakikisha programu inabuniwa kwa usalama, ulinzi, ustahimilivu na ushirikiano wa mifumo.
- Kufanya kazi na wasanifu, mameneja wa bidhaa na viongozi wa kitabibu kuunda ramani ya njia ya bidhaa.
- Kuongoza jumuiya ya mazoezi ya uendelezaji na kuweka viwango katika timu.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja au kuongoza wasanidi, akisaidia maendeleo na utendaji wao.

## Daraja 8b: Msanidi mkuu wa programu

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal developer**

> A principal developer leads and plans development across large or multiple teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be an expert in many technical areas or a specialist with very deep knowledge in a particular technical area
> - use this knowledge, and work with related disciplines, to keep multiple teams working effectively
> - develop capability by facilitating internal mobility, shaping career paths and recruiting talent, ensuring they collaborate
> - identify, test and champion the adoption of emerging technologies
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mkakati, viwango na mazoezi ya uhandisi kwa uendelezaji wa programu wa taasisi.
- Kuwashauri viongozi wakuu kuhusu uchaguzi wa teknolojia, hatari na uwekezaji.
- Kuongoza utatuzi wa matatizo tata zaidi ya kiufundi katika huduma.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi katika jumuiya za uhandisi za taasisi mbalimbali na za chanzo huria.
- Kuongoza na kuendeleza wasanidi viongozi na kukuza uwezo wa uhandisi wa taasisi.
