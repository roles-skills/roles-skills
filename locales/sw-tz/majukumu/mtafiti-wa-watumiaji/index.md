# Mtafiti wa watumiaji

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Usanifu unaomlenga mtumiaji](../../#usanifu-unaomlenga-mtumiaji)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [User researcher](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/user-researcher/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [user experience analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/faed411a-f920-4100-86a8-b877928b429c) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Watafiti wa watumiaji huzisaidia timu kuelewa watu wanaotumia na kutoa huduma za afya na matunzo, wakiwemo wagonjwa, walezi, madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo. Hupanga na kufanya utafiti kwa usalama na kimaadili, hugeuza wanachojifunza kuwa uelewa ulio wazi, na kuhakikisha maamuzi kuhusu huduma za afya za kidijitali yanategemea ushahidi wa mahitaji halisi, ikiwemo mahitaji ya watu wanaopata huduma ngumu zaidi kuzitumia.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Washiriki wa utafiti mara nyingi ni wagonjwa, wana wasiwasi, au wako katika mazingira hatarishi, hivyo watafiti hupanga kwa makini ridhaa yenye ufahamu, ulinzi wa watu na ustawi wa washiriki.
- Utafiti unaweza kusikia kuhusu uzoefu wenye kuhuzunisha wa ugonjwa, kufiwa, au matunzo duni, hivyo watafiti wanahitaji msaada kudhibiti athari za kihisia kwao wenyewe na timu yao.
- Madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo wana muda mdogo mbali na wagonjwa, hivyo watafiti hupanga utafiti katika mazingira ya kitabibu bila kuvuruga matunzo, mara nyingi kupitia uchunguzi na vikao vifupi.
- Baadhi ya utafiti huhesabiwa kuwa utafiti rasmi wa afya au hutumia data za wagonjwa, hivyo watafiti wanahitaji kujua ni lini idhini ya maadili au ya usimamizi wa utafiti na ukaguzi wa ulinzi wa data vinatumika.
- Ukosefu wa usawa wa afya ni halisi, hivyo watafiti hufanya juhudi za makusudi kujumuisha watu wenye ujuzi mdogo wa kidijitali, walemavu, au wenye Kiingereza kidogo, na watu wanaotumia huduma kwa uchache zaidi.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> User researchers plan, design and carry out research activities with users that help teams get a deep understanding of the people that use government services. This research informs policy, proposition, service, content and interaction design so that services work well for users and achieve policy intent.

## Daraja 4: Mtafiti msaidizi wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate user researcher**

> As a trainee in an entry-level role, working with more experienced user researchers, you will need to have an understanding of the role and show potential, although you will need guidance and training to produce good work and develop your skills.

- Kujifunza mbinu za utafiti wa watumiaji kupitia mafunzo na kufanya kazi pamoja na watafiti wenye uzoefu.

- Kuhifadhi data za utafiti kwa usalama na kulingana na kanuni za usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Kusaidia kuchambua data za utafiti na kushirikisha matokeo na timu.

## Daraja 5: Mtafiti mdogo wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Junior user researcher**

> A junior user researcher is embedded in a multidisciplinary team to carry out user research activities.

> At this role level, you will:
> - have some practical experience but need regular guidance and training to produce your best work and develop your skills

- Kuchambua matokeo na kuyawasilisha kwa timu kama uelewa ulio wazi unaoweza kutekelezwa.
- Kuajiri washiriki wa aina mbalimbali, ikiwemo watu wanaopata huduma ngumu kuzitumia.

## Daraja 6: Mtafiti wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: User researcher**

> A user researcher is usually embedded in a multidisciplinary team and responsible for planning and carrying out user research activities. At this level, you will be expected to work independently on a team.

### Majukumu

- Kupanga na kufanya utafiti wa watumiaji kwa timu ya fani mbalimbali, akichagua mbinu zinazolingana na maswali na washiriki.
- Kufanya utafiti na madaktari na wafanyakazi wa matunzo katika mazingira yao ya kazi kuelewa mtiririko wa kazi za kitabibu na kazi zinazohusu usalama.
- Kupanga kwa usalama utafiti na watu walio katika mazingira hatarishi, na kuhukumu ni lini idhini ya maadili au ya usimamizi wa utafiti inahitajika.
- Kushirikisha uelewa unaosaidia timu kuweka vipaumbele, ikiwemo ushahidi kuhusu usalama na ufikivu.
- Kuleta timu kwenye utafiti ili waelewe watumiaji moja kwa moja.

## Daraja 7: Mtafiti mwandamizi wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior user researcher**

> A senior user researcher is an experienced practitioner who can plan and lead user research activities in larger teams and on more complex services.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build user-centred practices in new teams
> - align user research activities with wider plans to inform a service proposition
> - supervise and develop other user researchers to assure and improve research practice

### Majukumu

- Kupanga na kuongoza utafiti kwa huduma tata, kama zile zinazovuka mazingira kadhaa ya matunzo au kuwahudumia watu walio katika mgogoro.

- Kupanga utafiti salama na watu walio katika mazingira hatarishi, na kuandaa maombi ya maadili na usimamizi wa utafiti.
- Kusimamia na kuendeleza watafiti wa watumiaji, na kuhakikisha ubora na maadili ya kazi yao.
- Kujenga mazoezi yanayomlenga mtumiaji katika timu mpya na kwa wadau wa kitabibu.

## Daraja 8b: Mtafiti kiongozi wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Lead user researcher**

> A lead user researcher is an expert practitioner, leading and aligning user research activities across several teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - ensure that teams take a user-centred, evidence-based approach to service design and delivery
> - develop and assure good user research practice

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza na kuoanisha utafiti wa watumiaji katika timu kadhaa au programu nzima.
- Kuhakikisha timu zinachukua mtazamo unaomlenga mtumiaji na unaotegemea ushahidi, na kwamba utafiti unawafikia watu walioathirika zaidi na ukosefu wa usawa wa afya.
- Kuweka na kuhakikisha mazoezi ya utafiti, ikiwemo ridhaa, ulinzi wa watu, ushughulikiaji wa data na ustawi wa washiriki.
- Kujenga rasilimali za utafiti zinazoshirikiwa, kama vile hazina za utafiti na jopo la washiriki, ambazo timu zinaweza kuzitumia tena kwa usalama.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja na kuendeleza watafiti wa watumiaji.

## Daraja 8c: Mkuu wa utafiti wa watumiaji

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of user research**

> A head of user research leads user researchers in an organisation and attracts and builds talent.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be an expert practitioner who can define and assure best practice
> - influence organisational strategy and priorities
> - collaborate with colleagues across government

### Majukumu

- Kuhakikisha mazoezi ya utafiti katika taasisi na kuwa kituo cha kupeleka masuala juu kwa utafiti wenye hatari kubwa.
- Kuwakilisha mazoezi ya utafiti kwa vyombo vya maadili, ulinzi wa watu na usimamizi wa utafiti.
