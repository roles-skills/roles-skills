# Mchambuzi wa data

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Madaraja:** 5, 6, 7, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Data analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/data-analyst/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [data analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/d3edb8f8-3a06-47a0-8fb9-9b212c006aa2) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Muhtasari

Wachambuzi wa data hugeuza data za afya na matunzo kuwa taarifa zinazosaidia madaktari, mameneja na wapangaji kufanya maamuzi. Huandaa, huunganisha na kuchambua data kutoka vyanzo vya kitabibu, vya kiutendaji na vilivyoripotiwa na wagonjwa, na kuziwasilisha katika ripoti, dashibodi na maelezo mafupi. Huhakikisha uchambuzi wao ni sahihi, unaoweza kurudiwa, na unashughulikia data za siri za wagonjwa kwa usalama.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Data nyingi huelezea wagonjwa mmoja mmoja, hivyo wachambuzi hufanya kazi na data zenye majina bandia inapowezekana na hukagua matokeo kwa hatari ya ufichuzi kabla ya kuyatoa.
- Data za afya hurekodiwa kwa istilahi na uainishaji wa kitabibu kama SNOMED CT na ICD, hivyo wachambuzi wanahitaji kujua jinsi misimbo inavyotumika na mahali inapoweza kupotosha.
- Ubora wa data hutofautiana kati ya mazingira ya matunzo na mifumo, na mapengo mara nyingi huathiri baadhi ya makundi ya wagonjwa zaidi ya mengine, hivyo wachambuzi hukagua upendeleo kabla ya kufikia hitimisho.
- Uchambuzi unaweza kubadilisha jinsi huduma zinavyopangwa, kufadhiliwa na kutolewa, hivyo matokeo yanahitaji tahadhari wazi na hayapaswi kukuza uhakika kupita kiasi.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> A data analyst collects, manages, explores and shares data to support organisational objectives and deliver business impact.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - identify, collect and move data between a range of systems
> - apply data analysis and data visualisation tools and techniques
> - prepare, manage and model data, following appropriate standards and practices
> - communicate data, insights and conclusions in appropriate formats

## Daraja 5: Mchambuzi msaidizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Associate data analyst**

> An associate data analyst supports data analysis activities and projects. They develop their skills in data analysis by working closely with other data professionals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - support the development and delivery of data analysis and data projects
> - develop analytical approaches and data visualisations, under supervision
> - work with others to understand roles within a multi-disciplinary team and how data analysts work with them

### Majukumu

- Kutoa ripoti na dashibodi za kawaida kuhusu shughuli, muda wa kusubiri na matumizi ya huduma, akifuata mbinu zilizokubaliwa.
- Kusafisha na kuandaa dondoo kutoka kwenye mifumo ya utawala wa wagonjwa, ya kitabibu na ya kiutendaji chini ya usimamizi.
- Kukagua matokeo dhidi ya kanuni za ubora wa data na kuripoti matatizo kwa mmiliki sahihi wa data.
- Kutumia ukandamizaji wa nambari ndogo na kanuni nyingine za ufichuzi kabla ya kushirikisha matokeo.
- Kujibu maombi rahisi ya data kutoka kwa timu za kitabibu na za kiutendaji.

## Daraja 6: Mchambuzi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Data analyst**

> A data analyst contributes to the planning, analysis and implementation of data. They work to appropriate standards and ensure data is used to achieve the team's goals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - prepare and manage data
> - apply appropriate techniques, tools and processes to effectively analyse data
> - communicate data to stakeholders with different needs

### Majukumu

- Kubuni na kufanya uchambuzi unaojibu maswali kutoka kwa timu za kitabibu, za kiutendaji na za mipango.
- Kuunganisha data za kiwango cha mgonjwa kutoka vyanzo tofauti kwa usalama, kwa kutumia vitambulisho vyenye majina bandia na mbinu za uunganishaji zilizokubaliwa.
- Kuandika msimbo wa uchambuzi unaoweza kurudiwa na kuandika mbinu, dhana na vyanzo vya data.
- Kueleza matokeo na mipaka yake kwa madaktari na mameneja kwa lugha rahisi.
- Kukagua uchambuzi kwa upendeleo katika makundi ya wagonjwa, kama vile kwa umri, jinsia, kabila na umaskini.
- Kuwasaidia wachambuzi wasaidizi na kukagua kazi zao.

## Daraja 7: Mchambuzi mwandamizi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Senior data analyst**

> A senior data analyst leads data analysis in a team or across an area. They ensure data is appropriately prepared, managed and used, and that data analysis outcomes align with the organisation's goals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - guide others in appropriate practices for preparing, managing and using data
> - lead the application and improvement of processes and ways of working with data
> - lead communication of data and data projects with stakeholders, adapting to their needs and requirements

### Majukumu

- Kuongoza uchambuzi kwa eneo la huduma, kama vile huduma za dharura, saratani au afya ya akili, akikubaliana vipaumbele na viongozi wake.
- Kuweka mbinu na uhakikisho wa ubora kwa uchambuzi wa timu, ikiwemo ukaguzi wa wenzake wa msimbo na matokeo.
- Kuongoza miradi tata ya uunganishaji katika mazingira ya matunzo na kukubaliana msingi wake wa usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Kuwasilisha matokeo kwa vikundi vya waandamizi wa kitabibu na vya usimamizi na kushauri maana yake kwa huduma.
- Kufanya kazi na wahandisi wa data na wamiliki wa data kurekebisha visababishi vya ubora duni wa data.
- Kuwaongoza na kuwaendeleza wachambuzi katika timu.

## Daraja 8b: Mchambuzi mkuu wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal data analyst**

> A principal data analyst leads teams or significant areas of work in data analysis. They determine and implement appropriate standards, processes and structures to ensure data is prepared, managed and used effectively within the organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - influence organisational decision making on data analysis and the use of data
> - oversee continuous improvement of the organisation's data practices and management
> - represent the data analyst community inside and outside of the organisation

### Majukumu

- Kuweka viwango vya taasisi vya uchambuzi wa data, uhakikisho wa ubora na mazoezi ya uchambuzi yanayoweza kurudiwa.
- Kuwashauri viongozi watendaji na wa kitabibu kuhusu ushahidi ulio nyuma ya maamuzi makubwa kuhusu huduma na uwekezaji.
- Kuongoza jumuiya ya uchambuzi, ikiwemo mafunzo, zana na maendeleo ya taaluma.
- Kuunda mipango ya ushirikishaji na uunganishaji wa data na washirika wa afya na matunzo.
- Kusimamia moja kwa moja wachambuzi waandamizi na kuhakikisha timu ina uwezo na ujuzi inaohitaji.
