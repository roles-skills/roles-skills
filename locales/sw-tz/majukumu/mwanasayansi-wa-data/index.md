# Mwanasayansi wa data

> Hii ni wasifu wa marejeleo wa kielelezo kwa taasisi ya kawaida ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali. Si maelezo rasmi ya kazi kwa mwajiri yeyote, na alama za tathmini ya kazi si tathmini rasmi.

> Tafsiri hii ilifanywa kutoka Kiingereza na msaidizi wa akili bandia na bado haijakaguliwa na mzungumzaji asilia. Nukuu kutoka UK GDaD PCF (Mfumo wa Uwezo wa Taaluma ya Kidijitali na Data wa Serikali ya Uingereza) na ESCO zinabaki kwa Kiingereza.

**Familia:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Madaraja:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Jukumu la UK GDaD PCF:** [Data scientist](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/data-scientist/)  
**Taaluma za ESCO:** [data scientist](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/258e46f9-0075-4a2e-adae-1ff0477e0f30) (ISCO-08 2511); [statistician](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/ac8b3cd1-a127-4e6a-8208-5cfcf7111955) (ISCO-08 2120)

## Muhtasari

Wanasayansi wa data hutumia takwimu, kujifunza kwa mashine na mbinu nyingine za hali ya juu kupata mifumo katika data za afya na matunzo na kujenga miundo inayosaidia huduma kupanga, kutabiri na kuboresha matunzo. Kazi ya kawaida hujumuisha kutabiri mahitaji, kutambua wagonjwa wanaoweza kunufaika na msaada wa mapema, na kutathmini athari za mabadiliko ya huduma. Hufanya kazi kwa karibu na madaktari na wachambuzi, na kuhakikisha miundo ni halali, ya haki, na inatumika kwa usalama.

## Katika taasisi ya huduma za afya na matunzo ya kidijitali

- Muundo unaosaidia maamuzi kuhusu wagonjwa unaweza kusababisha madhara ukiwa si sahihi au una upendeleo, hivyo miundo huthibitishwa kwa watu watakaoitumia na kutathminiwa kwa usalama wa kitabibu.
- Data za afya zinaakisi ukosefu wa usawa katika kupata matunzo, hivyo miundo inaweza kurudia au kuzidisha ukosefu wa usawa wa afya isipokuwa upendeleo upimwe na kushughulikiwa kwa kila kundi la wagonjwa.
- Baadhi ya miundo, kama ile inayoongoza utambuzi au matibabu, inaweza kudhibitiwa kama vifaa tiba, na udhibiti wa AI unaweza pia kutumika.
- Sayansi ya data mara nyingi inahitaji data za kina za wagonjwa zilizounganishwa, hivyo kazi hufanyika katika mazingira salama, kwa data zenye majina bandia, chini ya msingi wa usimamizi wa taarifa uliokubaliwa.
- Madaktari na wagonjwa wanahitaji kuelewa na kuamini matokeo ya muundo, hivyo maelezo na mawasiliano kwa lugha rahisi ya kutokuwa na uhakika ni muhimu.

## Maelezo ya jukumu la UK GDaD PCF (asili ya Kiingereza)

> Data science is a broad and fast-moving field spanning maths, statistics, software engineering and communications. Data scientists will often work as part of a multidisciplinary team, using data and analytics to inform and achieve organisational goals.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - be inquisitive
> - explore and visualise data
> - make recommendations to address complex problems and to inform strategic and operational decision making
> - use data ethically and appropriately
> - be innovative and adaptable
> - explore existing and new data using a range of statistical tools and techniques, such as machine learning and predictive analytics
> - find patterns in data and transform them into organisational insight

## Daraja 4: Mwanasayansi wa data mwanafunzi

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Trainee data scientist**

> A trainee data scientist is inquisitive, curious about data and keen to learn and develop.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have a basic understanding of software development and analytical methods
> - interpret statistical outputs effectively
> - be aware of how data science techniques can be used, and have a basic knowledge of those key techniques, such as machine learning
> - work as part of a multidisciplinary team to develop data science solutions and outputs
> - prepare and manipulate data
> - present effectively
> - be aware of ethical considerations
> - be aware of the technologies used

### Majukumu

- Kujifunza mbinu za takwimu na kujifunza kwa mashine za timu, zana na njia salama za kufanya kazi kupitia mafunzo yaliyopangwa.
- Kuandaa na kuchunguza seti za data za afya zenye majina bandia chini ya usimamizi.
- Kuendesha na kufasiri uchambuzi rahisi wa takwimu, akikagua matokeo na mwanasayansi wa data mwenye uzoefu zaidi.
- Kufuata taratibu za usimamizi wa taarifa, udhibiti wa ufichuzi na maadili.
- Kujitahidi kupata uanagenzi wa sayansi ya data au sifa inayolingana.

> An associate data scientist is inquisitive, curious about data and keen to learn and develop.

## Daraja 6: Mwanasayansi wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Data scientist**

> A data scientist will work independently and have a good understanding of a range of topics, including data science techniques, delivery methods and stages (such as minimal viable products), tools and technologies.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - develop complex solutions using a range of data science techniques, whilst understanding any ethical considerations
> - understand the role and benefits of data science within the organisation
> - support capability building within the organisation
> - collaborate with others to develop data science solutions and outputs supporting the organisation
> - prepare and manipulate data, and perform complex analytics
> - present and communicate effectively

### Majukumu

- Kuendeleza miundo tata kwa kujitegemea, kama vile ugawaji wa hatari au utabiri wa muda wa kukaa hospitalini, akichagua mbinu zinazofaa.
- Kuthibitisha miundo pamoja na madaktari, akikagua kama ni sahihi, ya haki na yenye manufaa kwa data za wagonjwa wa eneo.
- Kuandika matumizi yaliyokusudiwa ya kila muundo, data, utendaji na mipaka, na kuchangia ushahidi kwa hoja za usalama wa kitabibu.
- Kutathmini athari za mabadiliko ya huduma kwa kutumia mbinu zinazofaa za takwimu.
- Kueleza miundo na kutokuwa na uhakika kwake kwa hadhira za kitabibu na zisizo za kiufundi.
- Kuwasaidia wanasayansi wasaidizi na wanafunzi wa data.

## Daraja 7: Mwanasayansi mkuu wa data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Principal data scientist**

> A principal data scientist is a leader of data science, quite often with responsibility for managing and developing teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have a broad knowledge of data science techniques, use cases and potential impact, as well as the tools and technologies
> - have extensive experience in scoping, designing and delivering data science outputs and products
> - work collaboratively with a range of experts in support of organisational objectives
> - communicate effectively and challenge delivery plans and priorities
> - appreciate and understand data ethics, data preparation and manipulation
> - appreciate and understand delivery methods, and how to deliver supported solutions at scale

### Majukumu

- Kubainisha wigo, kubuni na kuongoza miradi ya sayansi ya data inayounga mkono mipango ya huduma na matunzo ya wagonjwa.
- Kuamua ni mbinu, zana na data gani zinazofaa, na kupinga mipango isiyowezekana au isiyo salama.
- Kupanga uthibitishaji, tathmini ya upendeleo na ufuatiliaji wa miundo pamoja na wenzake wa usalama wa kitabibu na usimamizi wa taarifa.
- Kukubaliana na timu za bidhaa jinsi miundo itakavyosambazwa na kuungwa mkono kwa kiwango kikubwa.
- Kusimamia na kuendeleza timu ndogo ya wanasayansi wa data.

## Daraja 8b: Mkuu wa sayansi ya data

**Kiwango cha UK GDaD PCF: Head of data science**

> The head of data science has complete oversight of data science within their organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - set direction
> - build capability
> - oversee resourcing, budgeting, professionalism and outputs and products
> - support and enable future IT developments
> - understand and use a wide range of data science techniques, tools and technologies
> - lead on ethics
> - communicate and present data science and data ethics effectively to ministers and senior leaders
> - champion the role of data science in supporting organisational priorities, and in collaborative working across professions
> - represent the department on data science matters

### Majukumu

- Kuweka mwelekeo wa sayansi ya data na AI katika taasisi, kulingana na mkakati na vipaumbele vyake.
- Kuongoza maadili, usalama na uhakikisho wa miundo na AI ya taasisi, na kuishauri bodi kuhusu hayo.
- Kusimamia rasilimali, bajeti na ubora wa bidhaa za sayansi ya data.
- Kuiwakilisha taasisi kuhusu sayansi ya data na AI kwa washirika wa afya na matunzo, watafiti na wadhibiti.
- Kujenga uwezo na taaluma za sayansi ya data katika taaluma mbalimbali.
