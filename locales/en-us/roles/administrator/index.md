# Administrator

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Corporate services](../../#corporate-services)  
**Bands:** 2, 3, 4  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [administrative assistant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/044d78cc-f62f-4532-83a5-8e04f2889652) (ISCO-08 3343); [office clerk](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/6c999fc7-c6b7-4ef3-a4b9-af124a1783a2) (ISCO-08 4110)

## Summary

Administrators keep teams and services running by handling the organization's day-to-day administration: correspondence, diaries, meetings, records, data entry, ordering, and enquiries. In a digital health care organization they support digital, clinical, and corporate teams, and often handle confidential information about patients and staff.

## In a digital health care organization

- Administrators often see confidential patient and staff information, so they follow information governance rules closely.
- Enquiries may come from patients, care staff, and the public, and some callers may be anxious or distressed.
- Accurate records and data entry matter, because errors can flow into clinical systems, reports, and payments.
- Many administrative processes are moving to digital tools, so administrators help test and adopt new ways of working.

## Band 2: Administrative assistant

Carries out routine administrative tasks for a team, following set procedures with supervision close by.

### Responsibilities

- Answer calls and emails, take accurate messages, and pass enquiries to the right person.
- Enter and update data accurately in the team's systems.
- File and scan documents and keep shared folders in order.
- Book meetings and rooms, and send out papers.
- Order stationery and supplies within set procedures.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- Good literacy, numeracy, and keyboard skills.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Knows the routines and procedures of the work area; may hold an entry qualification.
- **Autonomy:** Works to set procedures, with supervision available.
- **Scope:** Own tasks within a team.
- **Leadership:** May show new starters routine tasks.
- **Accountability:** Quality and timeliness of own routine work.

## Band 3: Administrator

Provides a full administrative service to a team or service, handling a range of tasks, some non-routine, and organizing own workload.

### Responsibilities

- Manage diaries and arrange meetings, events, and travel for a team.
- Take minutes and action notes for team meetings and follow up actions.
- Handle routine correspondence and enquiries, drafting replies where needed.
- Maintain records, trackers, and databases, and produce simple reports.
- Process orders, invoices, and expenses within set limits.
- Show new staff the team's administrative procedures.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- A level 3 qualification in business administration, or equivalent experience.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Knowledge of a range of procedures, some non-routine; typically a level 3 qualification or equivalent experience.
- **Autonomy:** Works within procedures; decides how to organize own work; refers problems upward.
- **Scope:** Own work and some tasks for the wider team.
- **Leadership:** May allocate routine work or train others in procedures.
- **Accountability:** Accuracy of own work and of records kept.

## Band 4: Senior administrator

Leads the administration for a service or directorate, supervising other administrators and improving administrative processes.

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate the administrative service for a directorate, allocating work to administrators.
- Take formal minutes for management and programme meetings.
- Write and improve administrative procedures and templates.
- Produce reports from trackers and systems for managers.
- Monitor spending on administrative budgets and raise variances.
- Train and supervise administrators and apprentices.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- Substantial administrative experience, at a level equivalent to a foundation degree.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Detailed knowledge of the work area, typically from a foundation degree, apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
- **Autonomy:** Works within guidelines; resolves most day-to-day problems; manager available for advice.
- **Scope:** Own work and a defined service or process.
- **Leadership:** May supervise a small team or coordinate others' work.
- **Accountability:** Delivery of a defined service or process.
