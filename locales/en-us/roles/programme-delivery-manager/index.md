> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**ESCO occupations:** [programme manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/c94f3565-d987-431f-a46b-6f4e018e0bd7) (ISCO-08 1213)

## Summary

Program delivery managers are accountable for delivering complex digital health services that need several teams, suppliers, or organizations to work together, or that carry high technical, clinical, or public risk. They manage dependencies, budgets, commercial arrangements, and people across the program, and make sure it delivers its intended benefits for patients, clinicians, and care staff.

## In a digital health care organization

- Programs often change how care is delivered across several organizations, so program delivery managers coordinate clinical, operational, and digital leaders who have different priorities and governance.
- Clinical risk must be managed across the whole program, not only within each team, so they work with clinical safety officers to keep a program-level view of hazards and safety cases.
- Programs frequently involve large contracts with suppliers of clinical systems, so commercial and contract management is a major part of the role.
- Benefits in care, such as shorter waits, safer prescribing, or staff time released, must be defined, owned by the service, and tracked long after the program closes.

## Band 8b: Program delivery manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Program delivery manager**

> A programme delivery manager is accountable for the delivery of complex products and services that are delivered by multiple teams or have high technical or political risk.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage dependencies of varying complexity, potentially planning and feeding into larger programmes and portfolios
> - remove blockers and manage risks, commercials, budgets and people
> - balance objectives and redeploy people and resources as priorities change
> - have an in-depth knowledge of Agile and other methodologies
> - be responsible for understanding, managing and communicating to complex stakeholder groups, balancing priorities
> - be the initial escalation point for the programme
> - have an awareness of the bigger picture
> - support the programme director by overseeing the delivery of their vision for the programme
> - support and coach delivery managers

### Responsibilities

- Lead the delivery of a program of several teams, such as introducing a new digital service across a care pathway.
- Plan and manage dependencies between teams, suppliers, and partner health and care organizations.
- Keep a program-level view of clinical risk with the clinical safety officer, and make sure each release has the assurance it needs.
- Manage the program budget, contracts, and supplier performance.
- Report progress, risks, and benefits to the program board and senior responsible owner.
- Line manage delivery managers and build a healthy delivery culture across the program.

## Band 8c: Senior program delivery manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Program delivery manager**

> A programme delivery manager is accountable for the delivery of complex products and services that are delivered by multiple teams or have high technical or political risk.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage dependencies of varying complexity, potentially planning and feeding into larger programmes and portfolios
> - remove blockers and manage risks, commercials, budgets and people
> - balance objectives and redeploy people and resources as priorities change
> - have an in-depth knowledge of Agile and other methodologies
> - be responsible for understanding, managing and communicating to complex stakeholder groups, balancing priorities
> - be the initial escalation point for the programme
> - have an awareness of the bigger picture
> - support the programme director by overseeing the delivery of their vision for the programme
> - support and coach delivery managers

### Responsibilities

- Lead the delivery of the organization's largest or highest-risk programs, such as replacing the core electronic patient record or a system-wide shared care record.
- Coordinate delivery across several organizations in a care system, aligning their plans, governance, and go-live decisions.
- Lead commercial strategy and negotiation with major suppliers, and manage program budgets across several years.
- Make sure clinical safety, information governance, and business continuity are assured at program level before major go-lives.
- Lead and develop program and delivery managers, and set delivery standards across the portfolio.
- Advise executive leaders on program risk, options, and recovery.
