# Service owner

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Product and delivery](../../#product-and-delivery)  
**Bands:** 8b, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Service owner](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/service-owner/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [ICT product manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/a79170ad-0b7b-4697-864f-1748359aa10a) (ISCO-08 1330)

## Summary

Service owners are accountable leaders for a digital health service from end to end, across its digital, operational, and clinical parts. They bring together multidisciplinary teams and service partners, set the service's strategy and priorities, own its budget and performance, and make sure it delivers safe, high-quality outcomes and benefits for patients, clinicians, and care staff.

## In a digital health care organization

- A digital health service is part of a wider care service, so service owners work as equals with clinical and operational leaders and share accountability for the patient's whole experience.
- The service owner is accountable for the service's clinical risk being managed, working with the clinical safety officer and accepting residual risk at the right level.
- Services handle confidential health information, so service owners make sure information governance, data protection, and cyber security are in place and reviewed.
- Live services that support care must be resilient, so service owners make sure business continuity, incident response, and supplier support meet clinical needs.
- Benefits in care must be measured and reported, so service owners keep performance indicators that cover safety, quality, access, equity, and cost.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> A service owner is accountable for the quality, performance, benefits and outcomes of a service. They provide clear end-to-end direction, prioritisation, risk management and decision making for teams in line with department, policy and user outcomes.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - define success and quality measures for a service, driving continuous improvement towards these measures
> - inform and influence stakeholders, getting consensus that enables work to progress
> - ensure the delivery of change that realises the outcomes and benefits of your service

## Band 8b: Service owner

**UK GDaD PCF level: Service owner**

> A service owner is an accountable leader that brings together a multidisciplinary team to ensure delivery of a quality service. They advocate for their service and teams, and develop an open and trust-based culture.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - ensure a strategy is in place that delivers the intended vision, outcomes and benefits
> - prioritise areas of development, balancing user needs, business requirements and policy
> - ensure appropriate governance and assurance is in place
> - ensure appropriate funding is in place
> - create the culture and environment for the team to do their best work

### Responsibilities

- Be accountable for an end-to-end digital health service, such as online access to records or digital referrals, across its whole life cycle.
- Set the service strategy and priorities with clinical and operational leaders, balancing user needs, safety, policy, and cost.
- Own the service budget and make the case for investment based on benefits for patients and staff.
- Make sure clinical risk, information governance, accessibility, and cyber security are managed and assured.
- Monitor service performance and benefits, and lead improvement when the service falls short.
- Represent the service at governance boards and with partners across the health and care system.

## Band 8c: Senior service owner

**UK GDaD PCF level: Service owner**

> A service owner is an accountable leader that brings together a multidisciplinary team to ensure delivery of a quality service. They advocate for their service and teams, and develop an open and trust-based culture.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - ensure a strategy is in place that delivers the intended vision, outcomes and benefits
> - prioritise areas of development, balancing user needs, business requirements and policy
> - ensure appropriate governance and assurance is in place
> - ensure appropriate funding is in place
> - create the culture and environment for the team to do their best work

### Responsibilities

- Be accountable for a group of related digital health services, or for a critical service used across the care system, such as the electronic patient record.
- Set a multi-year strategy for the services with executive, clinical, and operational leaders.
- Own budgets and major supplier relationships across the services.
- Accept and escalate clinical, information, and operational risk at a senior level, working with the clinical safety officer and the executive owner of information risk.
- Lead service owners and multidisciplinary teams, and build a culture of openness and continuous improvement.
- Lead the response to major service incidents that affect patient care, and the learning that follows.
