# Head of legal services

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Legal](../../#legal)  
**Bands:** 8c, 8d, 9  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [lawyer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/974d4eab-fbee-4c52-b16e-73bdd8c25b53) (ISCO-08 2611); [legal consultant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/31854d78-3615-43a4-aa3a-c597ecb20ceb) (ISCO-08 2619)

## Summary

Senior legal leaders make sure the organization acts lawfully and manages its legal risk well. They lead the in-house legal team, manage external legal advisers, and advise the board and executive on the organization's most significant legal matters. In a digital health care organization this includes the legal basis for using health data, major technology and data contracts, intellectual property in software, and the organization's statutory duties.

## In a digital health care organization

- The organization is a public body or works with them, so its decisions can be challenged, and it must act within its powers.
- The use of health and care data at scale, including for AI and research, carries high legal and reputational risk.
- Major technology contracts are long, valuable, and hard to exit, so their legal terms shape the organization for years.
- Legal leaders work closely with the board, the data protection officer, clinical safety, and information governance colleagues.

## Band 8c: Deputy head of legal services

Deputy heads of legal services lead a major area of the legal function, such as commercial, technology, and data law, and deputize for the head of legal services.

### Responsibilities

- Lead a major area of legal work, such as commercial, technology, and data law, or disputes and employment.
- Advise directors on high-risk matters, including major procurements and data programs.
- Manage the panel of external lawyers and the budget for external legal advice.
- Set legal standards, templates, and training for the organization.
- Lead and develop several legal teams or specialists.
- Deputize for the head of legal services.

## Band 8d: Head of legal services

Heads of legal services lead the organization's legal function, set its approach to legal risk, and advise the executive and board.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the in-house legal function and the use of external lawyers.
- Advise the executive and board on legal risk, statutory duties, and major decisions.
- Set the organization's legal risk appetite and positions on key issues, such as data use and contracting.
- Lead legal work on the most significant matters, such as major disputes, public inquiries, and partnerships.
- Hold the budget for legal services and show value for money.
- Lead and develop the legal team and legal operations.

## Band 9: General counsel

General counsel are the organization's most senior lawyer, with corporate responsibility for legal services and legal risk, and often also lead corporate governance as board secretary.

### Responsibilities

- Hold corporate responsibility for the organization's legal affairs and legal risk.
- Advise the board and chair on the organization's powers, duties, and governance.
- Make sure major strategies, partnerships, and data programs are lawful.
- Lead the response to the most serious legal threats, such as judicial reviews and regulatory action.
- Lead legal services, and often corporate governance and the board secretariat.
- Represent the organization with regulators, partners, and government bodies.
