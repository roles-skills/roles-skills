# Head of people

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Human resources](../../#human-resources)  
**Bands:** 8c, 8d, 9  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [human resources manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/f605bcd2-90b6-45a0-a558-d05016d68a77) (ISCO-08 1212)

## Summary

The head of people and the people leadership team set the organization's people strategy and lead its HR, recruitment, learning, organisational development, and workforce functions. They advise the board on culture, workforce, and employment risk, and make sure the organization can attract, grow, and keep the people it needs to deliver safe digital health services.

## In a digital health care organization

- The organization competes with other sectors for scarce digital, data, and technology talent, so people leaders balance pay rules and job evaluation with flexible working, career pathways, and a strong purpose.
- Services that run 24/7 depend on safe staffing, sustainable rotas, and staff wellbeing, which the board expects people leaders to monitor and improve.
- Culture affects patient safety, so people leaders link staff experience, speaking up, and inclusion to the quality of care.
- Workforce plans are made with partners across the health and care system, and staff may move between organizations when services change.
- Banded pay with job evaluation means new and changing digital roles must be evaluated consistently, and pay decisions must stand up to equal pay scrutiny.

## Band 8c: Head of HR

Leads a large part of the people function, such as HR operations, employee relations, and business partnering, and sets its standards. Also known as assistant director of people.

### Responsibilities

- Lead HR operations, business partnering, and employee relations, managing several team leaders.
- Set people policies and the organization's approach to employment law and risk.
- Lead partnership working and negotiations with staff representatives.
- Oversee the most serious cases, including those involving senior staff or legal claims.
- Lead workforce reporting to committees, including equality and pay gap reporting.
- Hold the budget for the HR services you lead and improve their value for money.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- A recognized HR professional qualification at advanced level, or a master's degree in HR or a related subject, or equivalent experience.
- Extensive experience of leading HR services at senior level.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Expert knowledge, and wide organisational and sector understanding.
- **Autonomy:** Sets strategy for a function; accountable to a director.
- **Scope:** A function or department.
- **Leadership:** Leads a function through several management layers.
- **Accountability:** A function's performance, workforce, and budget.

## Band 8d: Deputy director of people

Deputizes for the director of people and leads several people functions, such as workforce planning, learning, organisational development, and reward, across the organization.

### Responsibilities

- Deputize for the director of people, including at board committees.
- Lead workforce planning, learning, organisational development, and reward across the organization.
- Lead the digital, data, and technology workforce plan, using skills gap analysis to set priorities for recruitment, development, and retention.
- Lead the organization's reward strategy, job evaluation, and equal pay assurance within the agreed pay framework.
- Lead culture, engagement, and inclusion programs and measure their effect.
- Hold budgets for several people services and lead their improvement.

## Band 9: Director of people

Is the executive lead for people, culture, and workforce, accountable to the chief executive and the board for the people strategy and the whole people function.

### Responsibilities

- Set the organization's people strategy and culture, and lead the people function.
- Advise the board on workforce, culture, employment risk, and the people impact of strategy.
- Lead the organization's response to scarce digital and data skills, with the chief digital officer.
- Make sure the organization meets its legal duties as an employer, including equality and health and safety at work.
- Represent the organization on people matters with health and care partners, regulators, and staff representatives.
- Hold the people directorate budget and set workforce cost controls with the director of finance.
