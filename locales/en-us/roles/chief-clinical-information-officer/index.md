# Chief clinical information officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Clinical informatics and clinical safety](../../#clinical-informatics-and-clinical-safety)  
**Bands:** 8d, 9  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [clinical informatics manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/ef909843-97c6-4c48-8038-2e7aefdfcad5) (ISCO-08 1342); [chief information officer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/82f90e87-de92-4678-adae-61d3e5f7e1e4) (ISCO-08 1330)

## Summary

The chief clinical information officer (CCIO) is the senior clinical leader for digital. They set the clinical direction for the organization's digital services, lead clinical informatics and clinical safety, and make sure that digital investment improves patient care, safety, and the working lives of clinicians and care staff. They are usually practicing clinicians who sit on, or advise, the executive team.

## In a digital health care organization

- The CCIO brings clinical credibility to digital decisions, so most posts require a registered clinician who still does some clinical work.
- They are the senior voice for patient safety in digital services, working with the head of clinical safety and the medical and nursing leaders.
- They lead clinicians through large-scale change, such as replacing an electronic health record, where adoption and safety depend on clinical leadership.
- They help balance digital ambition with the realities of clinical workload, workforce, and risk.

## Band 8d: Deputy chief clinical information officer

Leads major parts of the clinical digital agenda on behalf of the CCIO, such as a care setting, a professional group, or a large clinical system programme, and deputizes for the CCIO.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the clinical direction for a major programme, care setting, or professional group.
- Lead a team of clinical informaticians and clinical leads, setting priorities and standards.
- Advise programme boards and executives on the clinical risk and benefit of digital investment.
- Chair clinical design and safety groups and make clinical decisions within delegated authority.
- Build networks of clinical champions and lead clinical engagement for large-scale change.
- Deputize for the CCIO at executive meetings and with partner organizations.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- Current registration with a clinical professional body, with extensive clinical and clinical leadership experience.
- A master's degree in clinical or health informatics or leadership, or equivalent experience of leading clinical digital change.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Strategic knowledge across functions and the wider health and care system.
- **Autonomy:** Shapes organization-wide strategy; deputizes for a director.
- **Scope:** A major function or several functions.
- **Leadership:** Leads several functions or a large department.
- **Accountability:** Major functions, large budgets, and organization-wide risks.

## Band 9: Chief clinical information officer

The organization's senior clinical leader for digital, accountable for the clinical direction of its digital services and for leading clinical informatics, with corporate influence over clinical safety.

### Responsibilities

- Set the clinical digital strategy and make sure it supports the organization's clinical and care strategy.
- Advise the board and executive team on the clinical risk, benefit, and priority of digital investment.
- Lead the clinical informatics function and the clinical design authority, and oversee clinical safety for health IT.
- Lead clinical leaders across the organization and its partners through large-scale digital change.
- Make sure patient safety, clinical evidence, and staff experience shape every major digital decision.
- Represent the organization in professional, academic, and cross-organization clinical digital networks.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- Current registration with a clinical professional body, with extensive clinical and senior leadership experience.
- Extensive experience of leading clinical digital strategy and change at organization level.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Strategic expertise and system-wide leadership.
- **Autonomy:** Sets organization strategy with the executive team.
- **Scope:** The organization, and its partners across the system.
- **Leadership:** Leads a profession or directorate.
- **Accountability:** Organization-wide outcomes, budgets, and risks.
