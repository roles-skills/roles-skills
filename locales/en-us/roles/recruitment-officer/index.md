# Recruitment officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Human resources](../../#human-resources)  
**Bands:** 3, 4, 5, 6  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [recruitment consultant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/068370e0-9c27-4814-87d0-44fbb3140815) (ISCO-08 2423)

## Summary

Recruitment officers run the organization's recruitment service, helping managers attract, assess, and appoint the right people quickly, fairly, and safely. In a digital health care organization they compete with other sectors for scarce digital, data, and technology skills, so they combine efficient processes with active sourcing, a strong employer brand, and good use of skills frameworks.

## In a digital health care organization

- Digital, data, and technology specialists are in short supply and often paid more elsewhere, so recruiters sell the purpose of the work, flexible working, and career pathways, not only pay.
- Roles that access patient records or work with patients need pre-employment checks, such as identity, right to work, references, criminal records checks where the role needs them, occupational health clearance, and professional registration.
- Vacancies in services that run 24/7 can affect patient care, so recruiters prioritize critical roles and track time to hire.
- Fair, inclusive recruitment helps the workforce reflect the patients and communities it serves.
- Recruiters use the role profiles and skills in this reference to write consistent adverts and job descriptions and to design fair assessments.

## Band 3: Recruitment assistant

Provides accurate administrative support for recruitment campaigns and pre-employment checks, following set procedures with a recruitment officer close by.

### Responsibilities

- Publish adverts and manage vacancies in the applicant tracking system.
- Arrange interviews and assessments and send correspondence to candidates.
- Carry out pre-employment checks, such as identity, right to work, and references, and chase outstanding items.
- Answer routine questions from candidates and managers by phone and email.
- Keep recruitment records accurate, confidential, and in line with retention rules.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- A level 2 or 3 qualification, a business administration apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
- Experience of accurate administrative work and dealing with the public.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Knowledge of a range of procedures, some non-routine; typically a level 3 qualification or equivalent experience.
- **Autonomy:** Works within procedures; decides how to organize own work; refers problems upward.
- **Scope:** Own work and some tasks for the wider team.
- **Leadership:** May allocate routine work or train others in procedures.
- **Accountability:** Accuracy of own work and of records kept.

## Band 4: Recruitment officer

Runs recruitment campaigns from start to finish for a group of services, advising managers on the process and checking the work of recruitment assistants.

### Responsibilities

- Run recruitment campaigns from vacancy approval to start date, keeping managers and candidates informed.
- Advise managers on adverts, shortlisting, interviews, and fair selection.
- Make conditional offers, agree start dates, and resolve problems with pre-employment checks.
- Plan recruitment events and bulk campaigns, such as for apprentices or new services.
- Report on time to hire and campaign progress, and suggest improvements.
- Check the work of recruitment assistants and train them on the system.

## Band 5: Digital talent partner

Works with digital, data, and technology leaders to attract and appoint scarce specialists, using active sourcing, talent pools, and fair, skills-based assessment. Also known as senior recruitment officer.

### Responsibilities

- Partner with hiring managers in digital, data, and technology teams to plan hiring and agree what good looks like for each role.
- Source candidates directly through professional networks, communities, and talent pools.
- Write adverts and job descriptions using the role profiles and skills in this reference and the UK GDaD PCF.
- Design skills-based assessments, such as technical exercises and portfolio reviews, with hiring managers.
- Use pay and market data to advise on hard-to-fill roles and starting salaries within the pay rules.
- Grow early careers routes, such as digital apprenticeships and graduate schemes.
- Manage the agency and recruitment advertising budget for the campaigns you run.

## Band 6: Recruitment manager

Manages the recruitment service and team, sets its standards, and leads improvements to speed, quality, candidate experience, and inclusion.

### Responsibilities

- Manage the recruitment team and service, setting priorities and service standards.
- Lead improvements to the recruitment process and applicant tracking system, including automation and self-service.
- Analyze recruitment data, such as time to hire, offer acceptance, and diversity at each stage, and act on it.
- Shape the employer brand and recruitment marketing with the communications team.
- Manage recruitment agency use and the recruitment budget.
- Make sure recruitment follows employment law, safe recruitment rules, and the organization's policies.
