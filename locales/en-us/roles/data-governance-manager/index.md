## Summary

Data governance managers make sure the organization's health and care data is well managed, trustworthy, and used safely and lawfully. They set up and run the frameworks, policies, and standards for data ownership, quality, metadata, and data sharing, and work with data owners, information governance colleagues, and clinical leads so data can be used with confidence for care, planning, and research.

## In a digital health care organization

- Health data is highly sensitive personal data, so data governance works closely with information governance, data protection, and the duty of confidentiality.
- Data is shared with many health and care partners for direct care, planning, and research, and each use needs a clear lawful basis, agreements, and controls.
- Clinical data standards, such as SNOMED CT, ICD, and HL7 FHIR, and agreed data definitions are central to making data consistent across systems and organizations.
- Poor data quality can lead directly to patient harm, such as missed allergies or wrong patient matching, as well as to wrong decisions about services.
- Patients and the public expect openness about how their data is used, so governance includes transparency and respect for patients' choices about secondary use.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> A data governance manager plans, monitors and enforces the management of data assets.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - catalog, monitor and identify data assets that are essential to the organization’s operations or services
> - assign ownership of data, ensuring there is a clear RACI (responsible, accountable, consulted and informed) matrix in the organization
> - incorporate governance of data into routine processes
> - support or manage risk processes around data
> - advocate for better use and management of data within the organization
> - encourage better data literacy within the organization

## Band 7: Data governance manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Data governance manager**

> A data governance manager defines, implements and manages data governance frameworks, policies and standards.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work on the design and implementation of practical data governance solutions to support organisational needs
> - identify opportunities and make recommendations to improve the quality and trustworthiness of data throughout the organization
> - implement and establish wider data management competencies within the organization (such as data quality, metadata management, master data management, data modeling, and development of data standards and data strategy)
> - apply data governance to optimize the safety and value of data throughout the data life cycle
> - work within the organization to minimize data risks and increase data literacy

### Responsibilities

- Run the organization's data governance framework, including data ownership, stewardship, and decision-making groups.
- Maintain the data catalog, business glossary, and data standards, including agreed clinical and operational definitions.
- Monitor data quality for key data sets and work with data owners to fix root causes.
- Work with information governance colleagues on data sharing agreements and data protection impact assessments for new data uses.
- Record and manage data risks and issues, and escalate them to the right owner.
- Improve data literacy among staff who create and use health data.

## Band 8b: Lead data governance manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Lead data governance manager**

> A lead data governance manager undertakes complex work related to the principles and practices of data governance, and co-ordinates with data owners to lead wider data management competencies.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead the formation, implementation and management of data governance frameworks, policies and standards
> - implement and establish wider data management competencies within the organization (such as data quality assurance, metadata management, master data management, data modeling, and development of data standards and data strategy)
> - be responsible for understanding and managing the organisational data risks and issues, and co-ordinating with data owners to accept or resolve them

### Responsibilities

- Lead the design and running of data governance frameworks, policies, and standards across the organization.
- Coordinate data owners and stewards to manage data risks, accept or resolve them, and report them to senior committees.
- Lead wider data management work, such as master data for patients, staff, and sites, and reference data.
- Agree data governance arrangements for data shared with health and care partners and researchers.
- Advise programs on how new systems and data uses meet data standards and governance rules.
- Line manage data governance staff.

## Band 8c: Head of data governance

**UK GDaD PCF level: Head of data governance**

> The head of data governance works with the chief data officer across the organization and government, providing strategic oversight and leadership in all aspects of data governance and use. They ensure high standards are maintained in-line with regulations and ethical requirements.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with the chief data officer to provide strategic oversight of data governance throughout the organization
> - set and monitor the adherence to data governance policies, frameworks and standards
> - be accountable for understanding and managing the organisational data risks and issues, and co-ordinating with data owners to accept or resolve them
> - act as a senior advisor across government on the best practice of data security development, in accordance with the data enterprise architecture
> - maintain effective long-term relationships at a senior level with important decision-makers and influencers
> - advocate improved data risk management and practices within the organization
> - encourage better data literacy and understanding of data governance within the organization

### Responsibilities

- Work with the chief data officer and the executive owner of information risk to set the strategy for data governance and use.
- Set data governance policies and standards and monitor how well the organization follows them.
- Be accountable for managing the organization's data risks, and report on them to the board.
- Represent the organization on data governance with health and care partners, regulators, and patient groups.
- Promote openness with patients and the public about how their data is used.
- Lead and develop the data governance function.
