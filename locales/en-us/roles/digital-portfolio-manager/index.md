# Digital portfolio manager

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Product and delivery](../../#product-and-delivery)  
**Bands:** 5, 6, 8a, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Digital portfolio manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/digital-portfolio-manager/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [programme manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/c94f3565-d987-431f-a46b-6f4e018e0bd7) (ISCO-08 1213)

## Summary

Digital portfolio managers help the organization choose, track, and govern its portfolio of digital projects, programs, and products so that it invests in the work that does most for patients, clinicians, and care staff. They run portfolio governance and reporting, help leaders prioritize with good evidence, and make sure the portfolio delivers the benefits it promised.

## In a digital health care organization

- Digital work competes for funding with front-line care, so portfolio managers help leaders show the benefits of digital investment for patients and staff.
- Portfolio governance must include clinical safety, information governance, and cyber security assurance, so portfolio managers track these alongside time, cost, and scope.
- Many digital projects depend on capacity in clinical and operational teams, such as clinicians to test or staff to train, so portfolio managers watch for clashes in demand on services.
- Funding often comes from several sources with their own reporting rules and deadlines, so portfolio managers keep reporting accurate and on time.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> A digital portfolio manager co-ordinates and manages a group , or ‘portfolio’, of digital projects. In this role, you will use an iterative and flexible approach to help define governance processes for this portfolio.

## Band 5: Digital portfolio analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Digital portfolio analyst**

> A digital portfolio analyst supports the tracking, analysis and delivery of outcomes across a portfolio of digital projects. This role level may be called ‘associate digital portfolio manager’ in your organization.
> 
> As you will be learning basic portfolio management skills, you will report to a more senior portfolio manager.

### Responsibilities

- Collect and check status, risk, finance, and benefits data from projects across the portfolio.
- Maintain the portfolio register and dashboards, and spot gaps or inconsistencies in reporting.
- Prepare papers and reports for portfolio governance boards.
- Track assurance milestones, such as clinical safety sign-off and data protection impact assessments, across projects.
- Support portfolio managers with prioritization and planning analysis.

## Band 6: Digital portfolio manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Digital portfolio manager**

> A digital portfolio manager helps to co-ordinate and manage a portfolio of digital projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - help to create environments that enable teams to deliver
> - support the prioritization of a group of projects and programs
> - help teams to follow governance and delivery processes
> - help stakeholders understand the performance of work and any related risks
> - support more senior portfolio managers and leadership in making improvements

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate a portfolio of digital projects and programs, such as all digital work for one directorate.
- Help teams follow governance, assurance, and delivery processes, and make them proportionate.
- Support leaders to prioritize work using evidence of need, benefit, risk, and capacity.
- Report portfolio performance, risks, and benefits clearly to boards and stakeholders.
- Spot clashes in demand on clinical and operational teams across projects and help resolve them.

## Band 8a: Senior digital portfolio manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior digital portfolio manager**

> A senior digital portfolio manager is responsible for co-ordinating and managing a portfolio of digital projects.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - track, analyze and help deliver outcomes in your portfolio
> - give guidance and deal with issues to help teams to deliver faster
> - manage governance and assurance processes, and ensure standards are met
> - provide information to stakeholders on work performance and related risks

### Responsibilities

- Manage the organization's digital portfolio, or a large part of it, and its governance and assurance processes.
- Track and analyze outcomes and benefits across the portfolio, and act on issues to help teams deliver.
- Make sure the portfolio meets standards, including clinical safety, information governance, and cyber security assurance.
- Advise senior leaders on prioritization, investment, and trade-offs, using clear evidence.
- Manage portfolio reporting to funders and external bodies.
- Line manage portfolio analysts and managers.

## Band 8c: Head of digital portfolio

**UK GDaD PCF level: Head of portfolio**

> A head of portfolio leads a team of digital portfolio managers. You will ensure the portfolio of digital projects delivers the best possible outcomes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead the tracking and governing of digital projects
> - ensure the right controls are used
> - address risks and deal with, or escalate, issues
> - provide leaders with insights into the portfolio's future so they can make informed decisions
> - build and lead a diverse, skilled and motivated team of digital portfolio managers
> - ensure the portfolio team is meeting the needs of the organization
> - support the professional development of the portfolio community, including line management and coaching

### Responsibilities

- Lead the digital portfolio function and the team of portfolio managers.
- Set the portfolio strategy, controls, and governance, and make sure they are used.
- Give executive leaders insight into the portfolio's future, so they can make investment decisions.
- Make sure the portfolio's benefits for patients, staff, and the organization are realized and reported.
- Address portfolio-level risks and escalate issues to the executive team.
- Manage the function's budget and capability.
