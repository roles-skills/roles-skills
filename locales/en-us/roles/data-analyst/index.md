# Data analyst

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Bands:** 5, 6, 7, 8b  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Data analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/data-analyst/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [data analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/d3edb8f8-3a06-47a0-8fb9-9b212c006aa2) (ISCO-08 2511)

## Summary

Data analysts turn health and care data into information that helps clinicians, managers, and planners make decisions. They prepare, link, and analyze data from clinical, operational, and patient-reported sources, and present it in reports, dashboards, and briefings. They make sure their analysis is accurate, reproducible, and handles confidential patient data safely.

## In a digital health care organization

- Much of the data describes individual patients, so analysts work with pseudonymized data where they can and check outputs for disclosure risk before release.
- Health data is recorded with clinical terminologies and classifications such as SNOMED CT and ICD, so analysts need to know how codes are used and where they can mislead.
- Data quality varies between care settings and systems, and gaps often affect some patient groups more than others, so analysts check for bias before drawing conclusions.
- Analysis can change how services are planned, funded, and delivered, so findings need clear caveats and must not overstate certainty.

## Band 5: Associate data analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Associate data analyst**

> An associate data analyst supports data analysis activities and projects. They develop their skills in data analysis by working closely with other data professionals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - support the development and delivery of data analysis and data projects
> - develop analytical approaches and data visualisations, under supervision
> - work with others to understand roles within a multi-disciplinary team and how data analysts work with them

### Responsibilities

- Produce routine reports and dashboards on activity, waiting times, and service use, following agreed methods.
- Clean and prepare extracts from patient administration, clinical, and operational systems under supervision.
- Check outputs against data quality rules and report problems to the right data owner.
- Apply small number suppression and other disclosure rules before sharing results.
- Answer straightforward data requests from clinical and operational teams.

## Band 6: Data analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Data analyst**

> A data analyst contributes to the planning, analysis and implementation of data. They work to appropriate standards and ensure data is used to achieve the team's goals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - prepare and manage data
> - apply appropriate techniques, tools and processes to effectively analyse data
> - communicate data to stakeholders with different needs

### Responsibilities

- Design and carry out analyses that answer questions from clinical, operational, and planning teams.
- Link patient-level data from different sources safely, using pseudonymized identifiers and agreed linkage methods.
- Write reproducible analysis code and document methods, assumptions, and data sources.
- Explain findings and their limits to clinicians and managers in plain language.
- Check analysis for bias across patient groups, such as by age, sex, ethnicity, and deprivation.
- Support associate analysts and review their work.

## Band 7: Senior data analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior data analyst**

> A senior data analyst leads data analysis in a team or across an area. They ensure data is appropriately prepared, managed and used, and that data analysis outcomes align with the organisation's goals.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - guide others in appropriate practices for preparing, managing and using data
> - lead the application and improvement of processes and ways of working with data
> - lead communication of data and data projects with stakeholders, adapting to their needs and requirements

### Responsibilities

- Lead analysis for a service area, such as urgent care, cancer, or mental health, agreeing priorities with its leaders.
- Set methods and quality assurance for the team's analysis, including peer review of code and outputs.
- Lead complex linkage projects across care settings and agree their information governance basis.
- Present findings to senior clinical and management groups and advise on what they mean for services.
- Work with data engineers and data owners to fix the root causes of poor data quality.
- Coach and develop analysts in the team.

## Band 8b: Principal data analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Principal data analyst**

> A principal data analyst leads teams or significant areas of work in data analysis. They determine and implement appropriate standards, processes and structures to ensure data is prepared, managed and used effectively within the organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - influence organisational decision making on data analysis and the use of data
> - oversee continuous improvement of the organisation's data practices and management
> - represent the data analyst community inside and outside of the organisation

### Responsibilities

- Set the organization's standards for data analysis, quality assurance, and reproducible analytical practice.
- Advise executive and clinical leaders on the evidence behind major decisions about services and investment.
- Lead the analytical community, including its training, tools, and career development.
- Shape data sharing and linkage arrangements with health and care partners.
- Line manage senior analysts and make sure the team has the capacity and skills it needs.
