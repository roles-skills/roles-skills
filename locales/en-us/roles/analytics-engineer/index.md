**Family:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Bands:** 4, 6, 7, 8a, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Analytics engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/analytics-engineer/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [data engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2079755f-d809-49e6-8037-4de6180e54c0) (ISCO-08 2511); [business intelligence manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/207d7b18-6540-432e-8aa6-785ed434572f) (ISCO-08 2421)

## Summary

Analytics engineers turn raw health and care data into clean, tested, and documented data models that analysts, data scientists, and reporting tools can rely on. They sit between data engineering and analysis, applying software engineering practices such as version control, testing, and code review to the transformations behind the organization's reports and dashboards.

## In a digital health care organization

- Data models encode clinical and operational definitions, such as what counts as an admission, a readmission, or a breach of a waiting time standard, so definitions are agreed with clinical and operational leads.
- Clinical codes from terminologies and classifications such as SNOMED CT and ICD are grouped into reference sets for analysis, which need careful version control.
- Models used for performance and funding reports must be consistent and auditable, because errors can affect decisions about patient services.
- Models often hold pseudonymized patient-level data, so access, documentation, and data minimization are built in.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> An analytics engineer transforms data relevant to the organization into structures that enable analysis and decision-making.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - work with subject matter experts to understand organisational processes and translate these into data structures optimized for analysis
> - work with data users to design and build data models they can use for effective analysis and decision-making, using modeling techniques such as Kimball or Inmon
> - support data quality improvement
> - develop standards for data transformation
> - create and maintain data documentation
> - refine requirements in response to feedback from users and changes in the organization
> - provide ongoing support to users

## Band 4: Trainee analytics engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Trainee analytics engineer**

> A trainee analytics engineer attends training and develops skills on the job.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - spend time shadowing other analytics engineers
> - build your knowledge of the organization
> - learn skills for managing data
> - learn to use different applications, tools, templates and best practices
> - handle simple queries from users and document their data requirements
> - contribute to data documentation and user training

### Responsibilities

- Learn the team's data modeling tools and practices through training and pairing.
- Make small, well-defined changes to data models under supervision.
- Document data items and their definitions in the data catalog.
- Answer simple questions from data users and record their requirements.
- Follow information governance rules when working with patient data.

## Band 6: Analytics engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Analytics engineer**

> An analytics engineer develops and tests data models assigned by more senior analytics engineers to help people in a defined area access and use data.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - draft documentation of data that meets standards
> - work with other analytics engineers to resolve issues and risks
> - support trainee analytics engineers
> - provide training and support for users of data sets
> - work with more experienced analytics engineers to develop your skills

### Responsibilities

- Build and test data models for a service area, such as inpatient activity or community care, from agreed designs.
- Write automated tests that check data models against agreed clinical and operational definitions.
- Maintain reference sets of clinical codes used in analysis, with clear versions.
- Document models so analysts understand their meaning, sources, and limits.
- Train and support users of the team's data sets.

## Band 7: Senior analytics engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior analytics engineer**

> A senior analytics engineer oversees the development and testing of data models, including directing the work of other analytics engineers.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - oversee tasks and support less senior analytics engineers
> - build relationships with stakeholders within a defined area
> - coach and mentor less senior analytics engineers
> - ensure documentation of data meets standards
> - ensure issues and risks are resolved
> - oversee training and support for users of data sets
> - explore and develop new ways of working with data

### Responsibilities

- Oversee the design and testing of data models across several service areas.
- Agree definitions of key measures with clinical, operational, and finance leads, and make sure models apply them consistently.
- Make sure models that feed performance and funding reports are auditable and well tested.
- Resolve data issues and risks with data engineers and data owners.
- Coach and direct the work of other analytics engineers.

## Band 8c: Head of analytics engineering

**UK GDaD PCF level: Head of analytics engineering**

> A head of analytics engineering oversees the development of a range of data models that meet analysis needs across the organization.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build relationships with senior stakeholders across the organization to determine what the organization needs
> - ensure the activities of the analytics engineering team align with strategic priorities
> - ensure the team works to standards for communication, data models and documentation
> - advocate for the analytics engineering role to senior leadership and other organizations
> - build analytics engineering capability by providing technical leadership and career development for the community
> - ensure appropriate technology is available for the community

### Responsibilities

- Set the direction for the organization's analytical data models, so that analysis across the organization uses one trusted set of definitions.
- Make sure analytics engineering priorities support the organization's strategy and reporting duties.
- Advise senior leaders on the trustworthiness of the data behind major reports and decisions.
- Make sure the community has the tools and platforms it needs, and manage the related budget.
- Build analytics engineering capability through technical leadership and career development.

### Typical qualifications and experience

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Expert knowledge, and wide organisational and sector understanding.
- **Autonomy:** Sets strategy for a function; accountable to a director.
- **Scope:** A function or department.
- **Leadership:** Leads a function through several management layers.
- **Accountability:** A function's performance, workforce, and budget.
