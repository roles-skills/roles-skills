# Governance and risk manager

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Corporate services](../../#corporate-services)  
**Bands:** 5, 6, 7, 8b  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [corporate risk manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/b09acdda-6680-49d1-a411-129610ee3bc0) (ISCO-08 2412)

## Summary

Governance and risk staff make sure the organization is well run and in control of its risks. They support the board and its committees, run the risk management framework and registers, keep policies and governance registers in order, and coordinate assurance and audit, so that leaders can make sound decisions and show they meet their legal and regulatory duties.

## In a digital health care organization

- Health care organizations carry clinical, information, cyber, financial, and reputational risks, so the risk framework must bring these together for the board.
- Boards must show they are assured about patient safety and quality, so governance staff link clinical safety and quality reporting into committees.
- Regulators and auditors expect clear records of decisions, risks, declarations of interest, and policies.
- Serious incidents and service failures need fast, well-documented escalation through the governance structure.

## Band 5: Governance officer

Supports the governance cycle for committees and keeps risk, policy, and governance registers up to date.

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate committee meetings, forward plans, papers, and minutes.
- Maintain the risk register, prompting risk owners to review their risks.
- Keep governance registers up to date, such as declarations of interest, gifts, and hospitality.
- Maintain the policy register and track policy reviews.
- Coordinate evidence for audits and track audit recommendations.
- Answer routine governance questions from staff.

## Band 6: Senior governance and risk officer

Runs parts of the governance and risk framework, advising directorates on risk and supporting senior committees.

### Responsibilities

- Run the risk management process for directorates, challenging risk scores and controls.
- Prepare risk and assurance reports for senior committees.
- Support senior committees, including the audit and risk committee, with papers and formal minutes.
- Review policies for compliance with the policy framework, law, and equality duties.
- Train managers in risk management and governance processes.
- Coordinate the organization's response to regulator and audit requests.

## Band 7: Governance and risk manager

Manages the governance and risk service, including the board and committee cycle, the risk framework, and assurance mapping.

### Responsibilities

- Manage the board and committee cycle and the governance team.
- Run the organization's risk management framework and the board assurance framework.
- Advise directors on risks, risk appetite, and escalation.
- Map assurance across the organization's main risks and work with internal and external auditors.
- Keep standing orders, schemes of delegation, and the policy framework up to date.
- Coordinate the governance response to serious incidents and regulator enquiries.

## Band 8b: Head of corporate governance

Leads corporate governance and risk for the organization, often acting as board secretary, and advises the chair and board on governance.

### Responsibilities

- Design and lead the organization's governance framework, including the board and its committees.
- Act as board secretary, advising the chair and board on governance, conduct, and compliance.
- Own the risk management framework and risk appetite, and advise the board on strategic risks.
- Lead the annual governance statement and governance reporting.
- Lead the governance, risk, and assurance team.
- Represent the organization with regulators and auditors on governance matters.
