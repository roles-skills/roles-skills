# Finance officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Finance](../../#finance)  
**Bands:** 2, 3, 4, 5  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [accounting assistant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/bfe142b9-a049-4d41-89c4-fd2f34ac56b6) (ISCO-08 3313)

## Summary

Finance officers keep the organization's day-to-day finances accurate and under control. They process and record invoices, payments, income, and journals, reconcile ledgers and bank accounts, and help budget holders and suppliers with their queries. In a digital health care organization much of this work involves technology suppliers, software licenses, and cloud services, often paid from public funding with strict rules on how it is spent.

## In a digital health care organization

- The organization spends public money, so every payment must be properly approved, recorded, and able to stand up to audit.
- Many invoices are for software licenses, cloud hosting, and support contracts, which need checking against contract terms, usage, and the right capital or revenue code.
- Prompt and accurate payment matters, because small suppliers and care providers may depend on it.
- Finance staff see personal information, such as staff expenses and payments to patients or care providers, and must handle it under information governance rules.
- Fraud risks, such as false requests to change supplier bank details, target public bodies, so officers follow strict checks.

## Band 2: Finance assistant

Finance assistants process routine financial transactions, such as supplier invoices, receipts, and expense claims, following set procedures with supervision close by.

### Responsibilities

- Enter supplier invoices, credit notes, and receipts accurately into the finance system.
- Match invoices to purchase orders and goods receipts, and chase missing approvals.
- Check staff expense claims against policy and supporting receipts.
- Answer routine queries from suppliers and staff by phone and email.
- File and scan financial documents so they can be found for audit.
- Follow financial procedures and information governance rules, and report anything unusual.

## Band 3: Finance officer

Finance officers run a part of the transaction cycle, such as accounts payable, accounts receivable, or cash, resolving non-routine issues and reconciling their ledger.

### Responsibilities

- Process a range of transactions, including payment runs, refunds, sales invoices, and journals.
- Reconcile supplier statements, bank accounts, and control accounts, and clear differences.
- Check that technology invoices match contract terms, such as license numbers and billing periods.
- Carry out supplier bank detail change checks and other anti-fraud controls.
- Chase overdue debts and resolve disputed invoices with suppliers and budget holders.
- Show new finance assistants how to do routine tasks.

## Band 4: Senior finance officer

Senior finance officers handle the more complex financial processing and reconciliations, support month-end and year-end, and help budget holders understand their spending.

### Responsibilities

- Prepare month-end entries, such as accruals and prepayments for annual software and support contracts.
- Investigate and clear complex reconciling items and aged balances.
- Prepare sales tax returns and reconciliations under supervision.
- Produce standard budget reports and explain them to budget holders.
- Check that spending is coded correctly between capital and revenue, and raise questions with the accountants.
- Supervise and check the work of finance officers and assistants day to day.
- Suggest improvements to financial procedures and system workflows.

## Band 5: Finance team leader

Finance team leaders manage a transactional finance team or work as finance analysts, making sure processes are accurate, controlled, and efficient, and that reporting is ready on time.

### Responsibilities

- Lead a transactional finance team, planning its work to meet payment, debt, and month-end deadlines.
- Manage a ledger or financial process, and report its performance, such as payment times and aged debt.
- Test and improve financial controls, and provide evidence to internal and external auditors.
- Prepare balance sheet reconciliations and working papers for the annual accounts.
- Improve processes, for example by automating invoice matching or introducing self-service for budget holders.
- Train, support, and review the work of staff in the team.
