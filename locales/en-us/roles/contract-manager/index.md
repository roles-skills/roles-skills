# Contract manager

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Procurement and commercial](../../#procurement-and-commercial)  
**Bands:** 7, 8a, 8b  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [contract manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/2c9939ef-ebab-4111-8f27-605ae8782791) (ISCO-08 2619)

## Summary

Contract managers make sure the organization gets what it pays for after a contract is signed. They manage supplier performance, payments, changes, disputes, renewals, and exit, and build relationships with key suppliers. In a digital health care organization many of the most important contracts are for clinical systems, cloud hosting, and technology services that patients and staff rely on every day.

## In a digital health care organization

- Clinical system outages or failures can affect patient care, so contract managers monitor availability, incident response, and support levels closely and use contract remedies when needed.
- Suppliers often process patient data, so contracts are managed with information governance and security colleagues, including data protection terms, audits, and breach handling.
- Exit and data return must be planned from the start, so that patient records can move to a new system in a usable format and old copies are securely deleted.
- Contract changes must follow public procurement rules, so contract managers check whether a change needs a new procurement.
- Supplier releases can change how a clinical system behaves, so contract managers make sure suppliers meet clinical safety obligations.

## Band 7: Contract manager

Manages a portfolio of contracts, including technology and clinical system contracts, from mobilization to renewal, working within the contract management framework. Also known as contract officer.

### Responsibilities

- Manage a portfolio of contracts, keeping the contract register, documents, and key dates current.
- Run regular performance reviews with suppliers, using service levels and key performance indicators.
- Check invoices against contract terms and resolve billing disputes.
- Negotiate and process contract changes and variations within the procurement rules.
- Track license use, renewals, and notice periods, and plan renewals and re-procurements across the portfolio.
- Keep exit plans and data registers up to date for each contract.
- Work with information governance and security colleagues on supplier data protection and security checks.

## Band 8a: Senior contract manager

Manages the organization's strategic and high-risk contracts, such as the electronic patient record and cloud hosting, and leads relationships with key suppliers.

### Responsibilities

- Manage strategic contracts for clinical systems and technology services, including mobilization of new contracts.
- Lead strategic reviews with key suppliers, covering performance, roadmap, risks, and improvement plans.
- Use contract remedies, such as service credits and remediation plans, when performance falls short.
- Negotiate contract variations, renewals, and settlement of disputes.
- Write and test exit and transition plans, including data return in standard formats and secure deletion.
- Assess supplier risks, such as financial health, cyber security, and supply chain, and plan for supplier failure.
- Coach and supervise contract managers.

## Band 8b: Lead contract manager

Leads contract and supplier management across the organization, setting the contract management framework and managing the contract team.

### Responsibilities

- Set the organization's contract management framework, standards, and tools.
- Lead the contract management team and its portfolio of contracts.
- Lead the management of the most critical supplier relationships and resolve major disputes.
- Set standards for exit planning and data return, and lead exits from strategic contracts.
- Report contract performance, supplier risk, and savings to executive committees.
- Advise on termination, re-procurement, and supplier failure.
