# Management accountant

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Finance](../../#finance)  
**Bands:** 5, 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [accountant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/eda0d957-3c3c-4139-b89a-a18bc9e18897) (ISCO-08 2411); [budget analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/4e4a291d-1d38-4c5a-812a-5827d0691b11) (ISCO-08 2411)

## Summary

Management accountants give managers the financial information and advice they need to plan, control spending, and make good decisions. They set and monitor budgets, forecast spending, cost services, and build the financial case for investment. In a digital health care organization they work closely with digital programs and product teams, where getting capital and revenue, whole-life costs, and value for money right is central to good decisions.

## In a digital health care organization

- Digital programs are often funded from a mix of capital and revenue, ring-fenced, or time-limited funding, each with its own rules on what it can be spent on.
- The move from owned systems to cloud services and subscriptions shifts costs from capital to revenue, which affects budgets, business cases, and funding bids.
- Investment decisions must show value for money for public funds and benefits for patients, clinicians, and care staff, not only financial savings.
- Contracts for software, hosting, and support often run for several years, so forecasts must include running costs long after go-live.
- Budget holders in digital and clinical teams may not be finance specialists, so clear, practical advice is a large part of the job.

## Band 5: Trainee management accountant

Trainee management accountants, sometimes called finance analysts, produce budget reports and analysis for a set of budget holders while working towards a recognized accountancy qualification.

### Responsibilities

- Prepare monthly budget reports for a group of budget holders and explain the main variances.
- Post month-end journals, accruals, and corrections to make sure reports are accurate.
- Check that spending is coded correctly between cost centers, projects, and capital and revenue.
- Collect forecast information and update forecasts with budget holders.
- Build cost information for business cases under guidance.
- Work towards a recognized accountancy qualification.

## Band 6: Management accountant

Management accountants are qualified accountants who run budget setting, monitoring, and forecasting for a directorate or group of services, and advise budget holders on their finances.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the monthly reporting and forecasting cycle for a directorate or group of services.
- Advise budget holders on spending, risks, and actions to stay within budget.
- Prepare annual budgets, including staff costs, contracts, and licences.
- Apply capital and revenue rules to digital spending, such as software development, licences, and hardware.
- Build financial models for business cases and options appraisals.
- Supervise and develop trainee accountants.

## Band 7: Senior management accountant

Senior management accountants lead the financial management of a large directorate or major digital programme, including complex forecasting, costing, and business cases.

### Responsibilities

- Lead budget setting and forecasting for a large directorate or major digital programme.
- Model scenarios, such as funding changes, delivery delays, and the shift from capital to cloud subscription costs.
- Lead the financial case for significant investments, including whole-life costs, benefits, and value for money.
- Build service and product costing, such as the full cost of running a digital service.
- Present financial performance and risks to senior managers and programme boards.
- Manage a small team of accountants and trainees.

## Band 8a: Finance business partner

Finance business partners work as part of a directorate's leadership, often digital, data, and technology, turning financial information into decisions and making sure investment delivers value for money.

### Responsibilities

- Act as the senior finance adviser to a directorate's leadership team, such as digital, data, and technology.
- Lead the directorate's financial plan, linking spending to priorities, workforce, and investment.
- Challenge and support leaders on value for money, including supplier spending, license use, and cloud costs.
- Lead business cases for major digital investments and represent finance at investment boards.
- Make sure digital spending meets funding rules, spending controls, and capital and revenue limits.
- Line manage management accountants who support the directorate.

## Band 8b: Head of management accounts

Heads of management accounts lead the organization's budgeting, forecasting, and management reporting, and the team of business partners and accountants who deliver it.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the organization's annual budget setting and in-year forecasting.
- Design management reporting, costing, and performance information for the board and executive.
- Set business case and investment appraisal standards, including for digital investment.
- Advise directors on financial risks, savings plans, and the choices needed to stay within funding.
- Lead and develop the business partnering and management accounts teams.
- Improve budgeting and reporting tools, including self-service reporting for budget holders.
