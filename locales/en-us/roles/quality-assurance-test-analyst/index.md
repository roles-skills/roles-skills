# Quality assurance test analyst

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Quality assurance testing](../../#quality-assurance-testing)  
**Bands:** 4, 5, 6, 8a  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Quality assurance test analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/quality-assurance-test-analyst/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [ICT test analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/0ab5c12c-c1c1-4772-8345-aa287d0e391d) (ISCO-08 2519)

## Summary

Quality assurance test analysts check that the organization's digital health services do what patients, clinicians, and care staff need, safely and reliably. They analyze requirements and designs to find risks early, design and run functional and non-functional tests, and report defects clearly. In health care this includes testing clinical workflows, data accuracy, and the controls that protect patients from harm.

## In a digital health care organization

- Defects in clinical systems can harm patients, for example through a wrong medication dose, a missed result, or a record shown for the wrong patient, so testing is prioritized by clinical risk.
- Testing provides evidence for clinical safety cases and hazard logs, so test plans trace to identified hazards and their controls.
- Test data must never expose real patients, so analysts use synthetic or properly de-identified test data.
- Many services exchange data with other health and care systems, so tests check messages against standards such as HL7 FHIR and the meaning of clinical codes.
- Users include clinicians under time pressure, patients with a wide range of needs, and assistive technology users, so usability and accessibility testing matter.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> A quality assurance test analyst interprets testing needs, creating and executing appropriate tests to ensure quality and alignment with requirements.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - plan, design and execute tests
> - apply testing techniques and standards to learn about systems and mitigate risk
> - analyze artefacts and prototypes
> - test outcomes and record defects
> - seek to improve testing practices to enhance quality and reliability
> - recommend testing approaches

## Band 4: Associate quality assurance test analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Associate quality assurance test analyst**

> An associate quality assurance test analyst works with other test professionals to learn quality assurance testing and analysis techniques.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - follow and apply test approaches, plans and strategies, under supervision
> - analyze artefacts, such as user stories, prototypes, processes and designs, with support
> - use basic functional and non-functional testing techniques
> - increase your understanding of how to design and execute basic tests, develop reports, record outcomes and support the resolution of defects

### Responsibilities

- Run test scripts for clinical and administrative workflows under supervision, recording results accurately.
- Raise clear defect reports, including the steps, data, and screens involved.
- Use synthetic test data and never use real patient data in test environments.
- Learn the team's test approaches, tools, and the clinical safety process.
- Take part in team ceremonies such as stand-ups, planning, and retrospectives.

## Band 5: Quality assurance test analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Quality assurance test analyst**

> A quality assurance test analyst designs and executes appropriate functional and non-functional tests. They apply multiple testing techniques to inform the approach to mitigate risk.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - apply the most appropriate test approaches and plans based on risk
> - analyze artefacts, such as user stories, prototypes, processes and designs
> - determine what to test following an agreed approach
> - ensure test coverage aligns with business risk
> - follow appropriate standards to mitigate risk early
> - execute tests, develop reports, record outcomes and support the resolution of defects

### Responsibilities

- Analyze user stories, designs, and clinical workflows to decide what to test, based on risk to patients and services.
- Design and run functional, accessibility, and usability tests, including end-to-end clinical scenarios.
- Trace tests to hazards and safety controls, and provide test evidence for clinical safety cases.
- Check data shown to users against source systems and agreed clinical codes.
- Create and maintain realistic synthetic test data.
- Track defects through to resolution and retest fixes.

## Band 6: Senior quality assurance test analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior quality assurance test analyst**

> A senior quality assurance test analyst influences and guides others in test analysis, coordinating quality assurance activities in a defined area. They assess the business impact of testing outcomes and use insights to improve quality assurance processes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - use the most appropriate tools and techniques to analyze artefacts such as products, services, processes and change, to identify risks early
> - work with others to support teams in developing approaches, plans and strategy, considering environments, data, tools and resources
> - communicate chosen approaches, tools, techniques and outcomes to stakeholders
> - guide others in quality assurance tools and techniques
> - contribute to, and enable others to follow, quality assurance and testing standards

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate quality assurance for a product or area, agreeing test approaches, environments, data, and tools.
- Identify clinical and operational risks early by reviewing requirements, designs, and changes with clinicians and product teams.
- Plan and lead user acceptance testing with clinicians and care staff.
- Report test outcomes and residual risks to product owners and clinical safety officers, so they can make release decisions.
- Guide other test analysts and help them follow testing and clinical safety standards.

## Band 8a: Lead quality assurance test analyst

**UK GDaD PCF level: Lead quality assurance test analyst**

> A lead quality assurance test analyst sets the strategy for test analysis and influences test analysis practices across a broad area. They develop, monitor and evaluate test analysis standards and methods, and make strategic decisions that improve test analysis in the organization.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead on setting standards and good practices across the test analysis community
> - lead and guide teams across the organization in test analysis
> - define and implement test strategies
> - mentor and coach other quality assurance test analysts
> - negotiate and escalate major risks, issues and defects

### Responsibilities

- Set test analysis standards and good practice across the organization, including risk-based testing of clinical systems.
- Define test strategies for major programs, such as electronic patient record changes or new patient-facing services.
- Make sure testing produces the evidence needed for clinical safety cases and, where relevant, medical device technical files.
- Negotiate and escalate major risks, issues, and defects with senior product, clinical, and supplier leads.
- Mentor and coach test analysts and lead the test analysis community.
