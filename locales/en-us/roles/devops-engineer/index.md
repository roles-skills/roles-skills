# DevOps engineer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organization. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Software development](../../#software-development)  
**Bands:** 4, 5, 6, 7, 8b, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Development operations (DevOps) engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/development-operations-devops-engineer/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [cloud DevOps engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/cc867bee-ab5c-427f-9244-f7a204d9574b) (ISCO-08 2512); [cloud engineer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/349ee6f6-c295-4c38-9b98-48765b55280e) (ISCO-08 2512)

## Summary

DevOps engineers build and run the tools, pipelines, environments, and platforms that let the organization's teams deliver digital health services safely and often. They automate build, test, release, and infrastructure, make services observable and resilient, and help teams keep clinical systems available when patients and clinicians need them.

## In a digital health care organization

- Many services support care around the clock, so deployments, monitoring, and recovery must keep downtime to a minimum, including out of hours.
- Release pipelines produce evidence for clinical safety cases, such as test results, change records, and the exact version deployed.
- Environments may hold confidential health information, so engineers control access, protect secrets, and keep live data out of test environments.
- Health services are common targets for cyber attacks, so pipelines and platforms build in security scanning, patching, and tested backups.
- Some services must meet strict standards for availability and disaster recovery, agreed with clinical and operational leads.

## UK GDaD PCF role description (English original)

> Commonly referred to as ‘DevOps engineers’, development operations engineers support the development and operation of software through tools, environments and practices.
> 
> In this role, you will be responsible for underpinning good development processes including managing tools and testing environments, central code control, maintaining development standards and writing software that automates systems.

## Band 4: Apprentice DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Apprentice DevOps engineer**

> An apprentice DevOps engineer attends certified training and develops skills on the job.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - spend a significant proportion of your time shadowing others
> - be involved in implementation but not planning
> - build on your knowledge

### Responsibilities

- Learn the team's pipelines, cloud platforms, and tools through structured training and shadowing.
- Make small, well-defined changes to scripts, pipelines, and configuration under close supervision.
- Follow change, security, and information governance procedures for every environment.
- Help monitor services and report alerts to experienced colleagues.
- Work towards an apprenticeship or equivalent qualification.

## Band 5: Junior DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Junior DevOps engineer**

> A junior DevOps engineer learns on the job by building software components.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - assist in the building of a complex story
> - work under supervision
> - be involved in implementation but not planning

### Responsibilities

- Build and maintain parts of build, test, and deployment pipelines with guidance.
- Write infrastructure as code and automation scripts, and test them before use.
- Keep test environments free of live patient data and manage secrets safely.
- Respond to monitoring alerts and support incident response under guidance.
- Record deployments and changes so they can support clinical safety and audit evidence.

## Band 6: DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: DevOps engineer**

> A DevOps engineer delivers automated software components that form part of a development operations toolchain.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - transform technical requirements into DevOps processes
> - build complex stories without additional support
> - manage live test environments
> - identify and resolve issues that are preventing delivery

### Responsibilities

- Design and build pipelines, environments, and platform components independently.
- Automate security checks, such as dependency and container scanning, and act on the results.
- Build monitoring, logging, and alerting so teams can see problems before they affect patients and staff.
- Plan and test backups, failover, and recovery for services that support care.
- Investigate and fix problems in live services, including out-of-hours support where the service needs it.
- Mentor junior and apprentice DevOps engineers.

## Band 7: Senior DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior DevOps engineer**

> A senior DevOps engineer delivers and integrates software to form a complete DevOps toolchain. They are responsible for planning and designing large groups of stories.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - transform technical requirements into an effective DevOps toolchain to enable product delivery
> - provide coaching and mentoring to more junior colleagues
> - ensure that deployment strategies for products are repeatable, scalable and highly available
> - have deep technical knowledge, providing support to delivery teams and solving complex problems
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the design of the delivery toolchain and platform for a product or group of services.
- Set standards for releases, so that deployments to clinical services are repeatable, reversible, and well evidenced.
- Agree availability, recovery time, and recovery point targets with service owners and clinical leads, and design to meet them.
- Lead technical incident response and post-incident reviews for the platforms the team runs.
- Make sure platforms meet security and information governance requirements, including access control and audit logging.
- Coach and develop DevOps engineers in the team.

## Band 8b: Lead DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Lead DevOps engineer**

> A lead DevOps engineer leads one or a small number of related project teams and contributes to the development of the strategic direction.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - act as a technical product owner, developing medium to long term strategies for product lines
> - provide technical leadership and guidance through coaching and mentoring
> - lead the sharing of knowledge and good practice
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Responsibilities

- Lead DevOps and platform engineering for a group of teams, acting as technical owner of the shared platform.
- Set the medium-term roadmap for the organization's delivery tooling, hosting, and observability.
- Make sure shared platforms are resilient, secure, and able to meet the availability needs of clinical services.
- Manage platform costs and capacity with finance and service owners.
- Lead the DevOps community of practice and line manage or lead DevOps engineers.

## Band 8c: Principal DevOps engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Principal DevOps engineer**

> A principal DevOps engineer leads and plans development across large or multiple teams, defining the strategic vision for delivery.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - identify, test and champion the adoption of emerging technologies
> - ensure that security, stability and capacity are embedded in the development and deployment of services
> - develop a team’s capability by rewarding high performers
> - shape career paths and recruit the right talent
> - identify skills gaps and important dependencies within technical teams
> There are 2 different roles at this level - a technical role and a management role.

### Responsibilities

- Set the strategy for how the organization builds, releases, hosts, and runs its digital health services.
- Advise senior leaders on hosting, resilience, cyber security, and platform investment.
- Make sure business continuity and disaster recovery plans for critical clinical services are realistic and tested.
- Evaluate and champion new technologies and practices, balancing innovation against safety and stability.
- Grow the organization's DevOps capability, including recruitment, career paths, and skills plans.
