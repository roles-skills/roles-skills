# Procurement officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Procurement and commercial](../../#procurement-and-commercial)  
**Bands:** 3, 4, 5, 6  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [procurement support officer](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/c9b2b0a7-0adc-4ca7-bf7c-dc95e18aeac7) (ISCO-08 3343); [purchaser](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/29fa406d-de89-4515-be8d-0fe7bda993f1) (ISCO-08 3323)

## Summary

Procurement officers help the organisation buy the goods and services it needs, lawfully and at good value, from routine purchases to formal tenders. In a digital health care organisation much of this spending is on software, cloud services, licences, devices, and technology services, so they work closely with digital, information governance, and clinical safety colleagues as well as with budget holders.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Public procurement rules apply to the organisation's spending, so every purchase must follow the right route, keep a clear audit trail, and treat suppliers fairly.
- Many purchases are digital, such as software licences, cloud subscriptions, and devices, and need security, data protection, and clinical safety checks before contract.
- Suppliers of clinical systems can hold patient data, so contracts need clear terms on data protection, data ownership, and data return.
- Buying through frameworks and dynamic purchasing systems built for the public sector can save time, but call-offs must still follow each framework's rules.
- Public spending must show value for money and social value, such as local jobs, skills, and lower carbon.

## Band 3: Procurement assistant

Provides purchasing and administrative support, following set procedures, with a procurement officer close by.

### Responsibilities

- Raise and amend purchase orders in the purchasing system, checking them against contracts and approvals.
- Resolve routine invoice and receipting queries with suppliers, budget holders, and finance.
- Get quotes for low-value purchases following the organisation's rules.
- Keep supplier records, catalogues, and the contract register up to date.
- Answer routine questions from staff about how to buy.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- A level 2 or 3 qualification, a procurement apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
- Experience of accurate administrative or finance work.

### Band outline

- **Scope:** Own work and some tasks for the wider team.
- **Leadership:** May allocate routine work or train others in procedures.

## Band 4: Buyer

Buys goods and services through quotations and framework call-offs, including standard software, devices, and licences, within set procedures.

### Responsibilities

- Run quotations and further competitions under frameworks, using standard templates and the e-tendering system.
- Buy standard software, devices, and licences, tracking renewals and licence numbers.
- Check that standard security, data protection, and accessibility requirements are included before purchase.
- Negotiate prices and terms for routine purchases.
- Keep a clear audit trail and publish contract award notices where the rules need them.
- Analyse spend data to find off-contract spending and suggest better routes.

## Band 5: Procurement officer

Runs procurements of moderate value and complexity, including formal tenders, from planning to contract award, with a senior procurement officer available for advice.

### Responsibilities

- Run formal tenders and framework competitions from planning to award, managing timetables and clarification questions.
- Help budget holders write clear, outcome-based specifications, including technical and data protection requirements.
- Set up evaluation, check bids for compliance, support evaluators, and record moderation.
- Write award reports and feedback to bidders, and manage the standstill period.
- Include social value and sustainability questions that fit the contract.
- Hand over contracts to contract managers with clear mobilisation and exit plans.
- Train budget holders in how to buy and supervise procurement assistants.

## Band 6: Senior procurement officer

Leads complex procurements, such as clinical systems, cloud services, and technology services, and advises on the right route to market, working with broad freedom within procurement policy.

### Responsibilities

- Lead complex and high-value procurements, choosing the right route to market and procedure.
- Lead early market engagement and requests for information, keeping competition fair.
- Design evaluation criteria and weightings with technical, clinical, and information governance leads, and lead moderation.
- Make sure contracts for technology include interoperability, security, data ownership, exit, and data return terms.
- Negotiate with bidders where the procedure allows it, and agree final terms.
- Advise on contract changes, extensions, and direct awards, and the risk of challenge.
- Coach and check the work of procurement officers and buyers.
