## Ringkasan

Manajer produk memastikan produk kesehatan digital organisasi memenuhi kebutuhan pasien, tenaga klinis, dan tenaga perawatan dan memberi nilai bagi sistem kesehatan dan perawatan. Mereka menetapkan visi dan peta jalan produk, memprioritaskan pekerjaan tim multidisiplin berdasarkan bukti, dan bertanggung jawab atas hasil yang dicapai produk mereka, termasuk dampaknya pada perawatan.

## Di organisasi layanan kesehatan digital

- Keputusan produk dapat memengaruhi keselamatan pasien, sehingga manajer produk memiliki risiko klinis produknya bersama petugas keselamatan klinis dan memastikan bahaya dikelola sebelum dirilis.
- Produk melayani banyak kelompok pengguna dengan kebutuhan berbeda, seperti pasien, pendamping, tenaga klinis, dan staf administratif, dan manajer produk harus menyeimbangkannya dengan adil.
- Sebagian produk dapat diatur sebagai alat kesehatan, sehingga manajer produk perlu tahu kapan regulasi berlaku dan merencanakannya.
- Hasil dalam perawatan mencakup keselamatan, mutu, akses, kesetaraan, dan waktu staf, sehingga manajer produk mendefinisikan ukuran keberhasilan yang dikenali pimpinan klinis dan operasional.
- Produk sering bergantung pada standar data kesehatan nasional dan regional serta platform bersama, sehingga manajer produk bekerja melintasi batas organisasi.

## Deskripsi peran UK GDaD PCF (aslinya dalam bahasa Inggris)

> A product manager makes sure that their products provide value and achieve the right outcomes by balancing user and business needs.
> 
> In this role, you will:
> - work with multidisciplinary teams, bringing together their views, user needs and business needs, to frame the problem
> - work with multidisciplinary teams to provide the right outcomes for users and the organisation
> - make decisions on priorities for your teams
> - ensure teams comply with appropriate standards

## Pita 6: Manajer produk asosiat

**Tingkat UK GDaD PCF: Associate product manager**

> An associate product manager is responsible for features, components or part of a product, contributing to the delivery of value.
> 
> This can be an entry-level role for civil servants who are looking to move into a digital role or those on emerging talent schemes.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - work with product managers to advocate for user needs throughout the organisation, prioritising delivery of greatest value
> - manage a smaller product with support, or a subset of features of a larger product
> - learn product management approaches and methods

### Tanggung jawab

- Memiliki backlog untuk suatu fitur atau bagian produk, bekerja dengan manajer produk.
- Mengubah bukti dari riset pengguna dengan pasien, tenaga klinis, dan tenaga perawatan menjadi cerita pengguna yang diprioritaskan.
- Bekerja dengan petugas keselamatan klinis untuk mencatat dan meninjau bahaya pada fitur yang Anda miliki.
- Melacak ukuran produk, seperti penerimaan, tingkat penyelesaian, dan panggilan dukungan, dan melaporkan apa yang ditunjukkannya.
- Mewakili produk pada show and tell dan di hadapan pemangku kepentingan operasional.

## Pita 7: Manajer produk

**Tingkat UK GDaD PCF: Product manager**

> A product manager is responsible for product quality and value, working with the team to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They might recruit and manage people in the product profession, ensuring people with the right skills are hired, supported and developed.
> 
> This can be an entry-level role for new product managers who have been working in other digital roles.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead a product through different phases of the product life cycle, from discovery, through development, delivery and continuous improvement, to retirement
> - support lead and senior product managers
> - prioritise work for your teams and be able to measure outcomes

### Tanggung jawab

- Menetapkan visi, peta jalan, dan ukuran keberhasilan untuk produk kesehatan digital.
- Memprioritaskan pekerjaan tim, menyeimbangkan kebutuhan pengguna, keselamatan klinis, kebijakan, kendala teknis, dan biaya.
- Menerima risiko klinis untuk perubahan produk bersama petugas keselamatan klinis, dan menghentikan rilis yang tidak aman.
- Bekerja dengan pimpinan klinis dan operasional agar produk sesuai dengan jalur perawatan dan diadopsi.
- Memastikan produk memenuhi standar aksesibilitas, tata kelola informasi, dan layanan.
- Mengukur hasil produk, termasuk manfaatnya bagi pasien dan staf.

## Pita 8a: Manajer produk senior

**Tingkat UK GDaD PCF: Senior product manager**

> A senior product manager can work on a group of products that share a value proposition, or a single product that is complex, high risk or sensitive. They are responsible for product quality and value, working with teams to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They might also be involved in parts of programme management activity.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead products through different phases of the product life cycle, from discovery, through development, delivery and continuous improvement, to retirement
> - make decisions based on the overall quality and value of your products
> - manage associate product managers and product managers
> - be involved in recruiting associate product managers, product managers and contractors

### Tanggung jawab

- Memimpin sekelompok produk terkait, atau satu produk yang kompleks, berisiko tinggi, atau sensitif, seperti yang dipakai dalam perawatan darurat.
- Menyusun strategi produk dari bukti tentang kebutuhan pasien, praktik klinis, serta kebijakan kesehatan dan perawatan.
- Memastikan produk memenuhi regulasi alat kesehatan jika berlaku.
- Mengelola anggaran produk dan menyampaikan alasan investasi dengan manfaat bagi perawatan.
- Membimbing dan mengelola secara langsung manajer produk.

## Pita 8b: Manajer produk pimpinan

**Tingkat UK GDaD PCF: Lead product manager**

> A lead product manager is responsible for a product portfolio or end-to-end service, and the delivery of product quality and value. They work with teams to identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics for their products. They are a member of the management team for their product area and provide product leadership and direction across multiple teams.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - manage people and manage products
> - maintain the high level roadmap
> - be involved in a range of programme management activities relevant to the product portfolio
> - be involved in recruiting product managers at all levels and contractors
> - provide leadership in your programmes and across the local community, working closely with the head of product, where they exist

### Tanggung jawab

- Memimpin portofolio produk atau layanan ujung ke ujung, seperti semua produk yang mendukung satu jalur perawatan.
- Menetapkan arah produk di beberapa tim dan memastikan produk bekerja bersama bagi pasien dan staf.
- Bekerja dengan pimpinan klinis dan petugas keselamatan klinis untuk mengelola risiko klinis di seluruh portofolio.
- Memegang anggaran portofolio dan melaporkan manfaat kepada dewan tata kelola senior.
- Memimpin dan mengembangkan manajer produk di bidang produk.

## Pita 8c: Kepala produk

**Tingkat UK GDaD PCF: Head of product**

> A head of product provides support to all product managers in the organisation and to the product community across government. They lead on product strategy, working closely with lead product managers and their business units. They are responsible for setting and raising standards for product management in their organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - coach others in product management
> - work with organisation management and other communities to represent product community interests
> - oversee every member of the community, including their role, assignments, development, pay and performance
> - manage recruitment, development, moves and exits from the community, covering civil servants and contractors
> - line manage senior and lead product managers

### Tanggung jawab

- Menetapkan strategi produk organisasi, bekerja dengan pimpinan eksekutif, klinis, dan operasional.
- Menetapkan dan menaikkan standar manajemen produk, termasuk bagaimana produk mengelola risiko klinis dan mengukur hasil.
- Memimpin profesi produk, termasuk rekrutmen, jalur karier, dan pembangunan kapabilitas.
- Memutuskan prioritas investasi antarproduk, memakai bukti kebutuhan, nilai, dan kesetaraan.
- Mewakili produk organisasi di hadapan mitra di seluruh sistem kesehatan dan perawatan.
