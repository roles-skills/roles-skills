# Dadansoddwr perfformiad

> Proffil cyfeirio enghreifftiol yw hwn ar gyfer sefydliad gofal iechyd digidol cyffredinol. Nid yw'n swydd-ddisgrifiad swyddogol ar gyfer unrhyw gyflogwr, ac nid yw ei sgoriau gwerthuso swyddi yn werthusiad ffurfiol.

> Cyfieithwyd y testun hwn o'r Saesneg gan gynorthwyydd deallusrwydd artiffisial, ac nid yw siaradwr Cymraeg brodorol wedi'i adolygu eto. Mae'r derminoleg yn dilyn TermCymru, cronfa dermau Llywodraeth Cymru. Mae dyfyniadau o Fframwaith Gallu Proffesiwn Digidol a Data Llywodraeth y DU ac o ESCO yn aros yn Saesneg.

**Teulu:** [Data](../../#data)  
**Bandiau:** 4, 6, 7, 8a, 8b  
**Rôl UK GDaD PCF:** [Performance analyst](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/performance-analyst/)  
**Galwedigaethau ESCO:** [data analyst](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/d3edb8f8-3a06-47a0-8fb9-9b212c006aa2) (ISCO-08 2511); [business intelligence manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/207d7b18-6540-432e-8aa6-785ed434572f) (ISCO-08 2421)

## Crynodeb

Mae dadansoddwyr perfformiad yn mesur pa mor dda mae gwasanaethau iechyd digidol y sefydliad yn gweithio i gleifion, defnyddwyr gwasanaethau, clinigwyr, a staff gofal. Maen nhw'n sefydlu fframweithiau mesur, yn casglu ac yn dadansoddi data defnydd a chanlyniadau, ac yn helpu timau cynnyrch ac arweinwyr gwasanaethau i ddeall a yw newidiadau'n gwella mynediad, profiad, diogelwch, ac effeithlonrwydd.

## Mewn sefydliad gofal iechyd digidol

- Mae mesurau llwyddiant yn mynd y tu hwnt i'r defnydd digidol, i gynnwys effeithiau ar ofal, fel apwyntiadau a gollwyd, amseroedd aros, brysbennu diogel, ac amser staff a ryddhawyd.
- Gall dadansoddeg ac olrhain ar wasanaethau iechyd ddatgelu gwybodaeth sensitif, fel pa gyflwr y chwiliodd person amdano, felly mae'n rhaid i fesur barchu cydsyniad, rheolau cwcis, a chyfrinachedd.
- Rhaid i wasanaethau digidol beidio ag ehangu anghydraddoldebau iechyd, felly mae dadansoddwyr yn edrych ar ddefnydd a chanlyniadau ar gyfer grwpiau sy'n llai tebygol o ddefnyddio gwasanaethau digidol.
- Gall data perfformiad gael ei adrodd i fyrddau, comisiynwyr, a rheoleiddwyr, felly mae'n rhaid i ddiffiniadau fod yn gyson ac wedi'u dogfennu'n dda.

## Disgrifiad rôl UK GDaD PCF (y gwreiddiol Saesneg)

> A performance analyst conducts analysis. In this role, you will develop performance measurement frameworks - Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), goals, user needs and benefits - and analyse the performance of a service or product against these, adapting your approach and framework appropriately and in line with any changes.

## Band 4: Dadansoddwr perfformiad cyswllt

**Lefel UK GDaD PCF: Associate performance analyst**

> An associate performance analyst is an entry-level role requiring numeracy and some experience in analysis or data handling.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have the aptitude to learn to use analytical tools and produce standardised reports and charts
> - start by working on projects with limited scope under the guidance of performance analysts and more senior role levels, replicating existing analysis and data techniques
> - progress to greater independence (where appropriate)
> - have the opportunity to develop an understanding of the wider analytics context, tools and techniques
> - be responsible for the accuracy and quality of data and analysis, and how they are used
> - engage with the performance analysis community

### Cyfrifoldebau

- Cynhyrchu adroddiadau rheolaidd ar sut mae cleifion a staff yn defnyddio gwasanaethau digidol, fel cofrestriadau, cwblhau, a phwyntiau gadael.
- Gwirio data dadansoddeg ac olrhain am fylchau a gwallau, a rhoi gwybod amdanynt i'r tîm.
- Dilyn rheolau'r sefydliad ar gydsyniad a chyfrinachedd wrth gasglu a rhannu data defnydd.
- Helpu timau cynnyrch i ateb cwestiynau syml am ddefnydd gwasanaethau.

## Band 6: Dadansoddwr perfformiad

**Lefel UK GDaD PCF: Performance analyst**

> A performance analyst has a strong understanding of a wide range of sources and analytical tools needed for the project they are working on.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - be responsible for the performance analysis in your given area
> - help the project team measure and assess outcomes (for example, what good looks like and how it will be measured)
> - help to plan and ensure project delivery
> - find opportunities to share information with the project team, community colleagues and others
> - work independently, but know to seek guidance when necessary
> - be adaptable in applying analysis techniques to new contexts, especially when acting as the sole representative for performance analysts on a project
> - create a performance measurement framework for a project with the wider team, including identifying reporting requirements, advising on procurement, implementation and validation of tools to deliver against these requirements
> - be responsible for the accuracy and quality of data and analysis, and how they are used
> - be engaged with, and actively contribute to, the performance analysis community

### Cyfrifoldebau

- Cytuno gyda thimau cynnyrch ac arweinwyr clinigol sut olwg sydd ar lwyddiant ar gyfer gwasanaeth, gan gynnwys effeithiau ar ofal ac ar staff.
- Sefydlu a dilysu dadansoddeg, fel bod olrhain yn gywir, yn gymesur, ac yn parchu cydsyniad.
- Cyfuno dadansoddeg ddigidol â data gweithredol, fel apwyntiadau ac atgyfeiriadau, i ddangos effaith newidiadau.
- Dadansoddi defnydd a chanlyniadau ar gyfer gwahanol grwpiau o gleifion a thynnu sylw at risgiau allgáu digidol.
- Cyflwyno canfyddiadau i dimau cynnyrch ac arweinwyr gwasanaethau ac argymell gwelliannau.

## Band 7: Uwch ddadansoddwr perfformiad

**Lefel UK GDaD PCF: Senior performance analyst**

> A senior performance analyst looks to delegate, automate or upskill others in analysis where possible and appropriate, provide leadership within their team and proactively use analysis to improve services.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - potentially manage one or more performance analysts
> - be proficient in a wide range of tools
> - ensure the team is equipped with the required skills according to the strategy set out by the head of performance analysis
> - be skilled and experienced at carrying out the day-to-day activities of a performance analyst, including setting up performance measurement frameworks
> - carry out increasingly complex analysis, working with other teams where necessary
> - actively contribute to the performance analysis community within your department or organisation, while forging links with other analytical disciplines
> As a 'technical specialist senior performance analyst', you would look to develop tools and techniques to aid others in analysis. You would be responsible for the accuracy and quality of data and analysis, and for how data and analysis are used.

### Cyfrifoldebau

- Arwain mesur perfformiad ar gyfer grŵp o wasanaethau neu raglen fawr, fel cleifion allanol digidol neu frysbennu ar-lein.
- Dylunio mesurau buddion gydag arweinwyr clinigol, cyllid, a gweithredol, a'u holrhain drwy'r cyflawni.
- Dewis a ffurfweddu offer dadansoddeg sy'n bodloni gofynion llywodraethiant gwybodaeth a diogelwch.
- Cyflwyno tystiolaeth perfformiad i fyrddau rhaglen ac uwch arweinwyr.
- Hyfforddi dadansoddwyr a gall reoli un neu fwy o ddadansoddwyr perfformiad yn llinell.

## Band 8a: Dadansoddwr perfformiad arweiniol

**Lefel UK GDaD PCF: Lead performance analyst**

> A lead performance analyst manages junior performance analysts at any level, co-ordinating teams or groups of related analysts.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - assist, take forward and influence the strategy as determined by the head of performance analysis, including data implementation and data architecture
> - remove blockers and act as an escalation point for important issues
> - be an ambassador for analytics across communities, departments and government
> - be responsible for the accuracy and quality of data and analysis, and of how data and analysis are used
> - proactively share knowledge and promote a data-driven culture, as a leader in the community
> - coach and mentor senior performance analysts, performance analysts and associate performance analysts

### Cyfrifoldebau

- Arwain tîm o ddadansoddwyr perfformiad ar draws portffolio o wasanaethau iechyd digidol.
- Cytuno ar ddiffiniadau cyson ar gyfer mesurau perfformiad allweddol, fel y gellir cymharu adroddiadau i fyrddau a phartneriaid dros amser.
- Sicrhau bod data dadansoddeg yn gywir, yn cael ei ddefnyddio'n briodol, ac yn cael ei drin yn unol â rheolau llywodraethiant gwybodaeth.
- Defnyddio tystiolaeth perfformiad i ddylanwadu ar flaenoriaethau a buddsoddiad y portffolio.
- Hyfforddi a mentora dadansoddwyr ar bob lefel.

## Band 8b: Pennaeth dadansoddi perfformiad

**Lefel UK GDaD PCF: Head of performance analysis**

> The head of performance analysis leads the performance analysis community, acting as the point of contact between the community and the business.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - remove blockers and act as an escalation point for important issues
> - set the performance analysis strategy with input from lead and senior performance analysts
> - be responsible for defining a framework for quality assurance within performance analysis
> - champion performance analysis and performance analysts, working with colleagues across government and other Government Digital and Data communities and analytical professions
> - manage the organisation’s performance analysis capability through business planning, development and recruitment

### Cyfrifoldebau

- Gosod y strategaeth ar gyfer mesur perfformiad a buddion gwasanaethau digidol y sefydliad.
- Diffinio'r fframwaith sicrwydd ansawdd ar gyfer dadansoddi perfformiad ac adrodd ar berfformiad.
- Cynghori arweinwyr gweithredol ar yr hyn y mae tystiolaeth perfformiad yn ei ddangos am wasanaethau digidol a chanlyniadau cleifion.
- Gweithio gyda phroffesiynau dadansoddol eraill, fel dadansoddi data a gwyddor data, i rannu dulliau a data.
- Cynllunio gallu'r gymuned dadansoddi perfformiad, gan gynnwys recriwtio a datblygu.
