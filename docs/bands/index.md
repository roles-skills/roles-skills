# Roles by band

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

## Band 1

*Entry. SFIA level 1. Job evaluation points 0–160.*

- **Knowledge:** Basic literacy and numeracy; learns simple routine tasks on the job.
- **Autonomy:** Follows clear instructions; work is checked often.
- **Scope:** Own routine tasks.
- **Leadership:** None.
- **Accountability:** Accurate completion of routine tasks.

## Band 2

*Support. SFIA level 1. Job evaluation points 161–215.*

- **Knowledge:** Knows the routines and procedures of the work area; may hold an entry qualification.
- **Autonomy:** Works to set procedures, with supervision available.
- **Scope:** Own tasks within a team.
- **Leadership:** May show new starters routine tasks.
- **Accountability:** Quality and timeliness of own routine work.

## Band 3

*Support. SFIA level 2. Job evaluation points 216–270.*

- **Knowledge:** Knowledge of a range of procedures, some non-routine; typically a level 3 qualification or equivalent experience.
- **Autonomy:** Works within procedures; decides how to organise own work; refers problems upward.
- **Scope:** Own work and some tasks for the wider team.
- **Leadership:** May allocate routine work or train others in procedures.
- **Accountability:** Accuracy of own work and of records kept.

- **Leadership:** May supervise a small team or coordinate others' work.

- **Scope:** A product, service, or workstream.
- **Leadership:** May lead a small team or mentor colleagues.
- **Accountability:** Outcomes of own workstream and quality of advice given.

- **Scope:** Several products or services, or a specialist function.
- **Leadership:** Leads a team or a professional practice area.
- **Accountability:** Delivery of a service or specialist function, and its budget if held.
