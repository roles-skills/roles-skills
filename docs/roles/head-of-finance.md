# Head of finance

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Finance](../index.md#finance)  
**Bands:** 8c, 8d, 9  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [financial controller](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/5e5b1b61-1351-4d3b-a293-deb3661715dd) (ISCO-08 2411); [financial manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/30f3ea93-882a-4525-841c-1d5b4b64076f) (ISCO-08 1211)

## Summary

Senior finance leaders are accountable for the organisation's financial health, financial control, and use of public money. They lead financial strategy and planning, advise the board on affordability and value for money, and lead the finance, payroll, and often procurement functions. In a digital health care organisation they shape how investment in technology and data is funded, approved, and shown to deliver benefits for patients and the health and care system.

## In a digital health care organisation

- The organisation is funded with public money, so finance leaders are accountable to funders and oversight bodies for regularity, propriety, and value for money.
- Technology investment is a large share of spending, and the balance between capital, revenue, and cloud subscription costs shapes what the organisation can afford.
- Finance leaders must balance financial targets with patient safety and the quality of services that clinicians and care staff rely on.
- Large technology and data contracts carry long-term commitments and supplier risks that the board must understand.
- The organisation may work in partnerships across health and care, with pooled budgets, shared services, or hosted programmes.

## Band 8c: Financial controller

Financial controllers, sometimes called assistant directors of finance, lead a major part of the finance function, such as financial accounting and control or financial planning, and deputise for senior finance leaders.

### Responsibilities

- Lead financial accounting, financial control, treasury, and transactional services, or financial planning and business partnering.
- Make sure the annual accounts are delivered on time with a clean audit opinion.
- Maintain an effective system of financial control, and report on it to the audit committee.
- Lead the financial planning cycle and in-year financial reporting to the executive.
- Advise directors on major financial decisions, including large technology contracts.
- Lead and develop several finance teams.

## Band 8d: Deputy director of finance

Deputy directors of finance lead the finance function day to day, deputise for the director of finance, and lead the organisation's financial strategy and medium-term financial plan.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the medium-term financial plan and annual financial plan, linking them to strategy and workforce plans.
- Lead the business case and investment approval process, including for major digital programmes.
- Advise the executive and board committees on financial performance, risk, and savings plans.
- Represent the organisation with funders and partners on financial matters.
- Deputise for the director of finance, including at the board and audit committee.
- Lead and develop the finance, payroll, and financial systems teams.

## Band 9: Director of finance

Directors of finance are executive board members with corporate responsibility for the organisation's finances, financial control, and use of public money.

### Responsibilities

- Hold corporate responsibility for the organisation's finances, financial control, and financial sustainability.
- Set the financial strategy and lead the board's decisions on affordability, investment, and value for money.
- Make sure the organisation spends public money lawfully, within its funding, and in line with spending controls.
- Advise the board on the financial effect of strategy, including the shift to digital and cloud-based services.
- Lead relationships with funders, oversight bodies, and external auditors.
- Lead the finance, payroll, and procurement functions, and often other corporate services.
