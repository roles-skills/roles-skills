# Head of procurement

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Procurement and commercial](../index.md#procurement)  
**Bands:** 8b, 8c, 8d  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [procurement department manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/91f93dc4-1b71-4053-b6fb-c579ec19303a) (ISCO-08 1213); [purchasing manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/377865f0-c327-4599-8c7e-d428c6588edf) (ISCO-08 1324)

## Summary

The head of procurement and the commercial leadership team lead how the organisation buys goods and services and manages its suppliers. They set procurement policy and commercial strategy, make sure spending is lawful and gives value for money, and advise the board on commercial risk. In a digital health care organisation they give particular attention to technology and clinical system contracts, supplier resilience, and exit.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Large technology and clinical system contracts can shape services for a decade, so commercial leaders plan for interoperability, portability, and future competition from the start.
- Critical suppliers support services that run 24/7, so supplier failure and cyber incidents in the supply chain are board-level risks.
- Public procurement rules apply, and the organisation must be able to defend its procurement decisions if challenged.
- The organisation is expected to deliver social value and carbon reduction through its spending.
- Commercial leaders often work with other health and care organisations on collaborative buying and shared contracts.

## Band 8b: Head of procurement

Leads the procurement function, setting procurement procedures and standards and managing the procurement and purchasing teams.

### Responsibilities

- Lead and manage the procurement and purchasing teams.
- Set the organisation's procurement procedures, templates, and thresholds, in line with public procurement rules and financial rules.
- Assure the most complex and high-value procurements and their award decisions.
- Manage the procurement pipeline and make sure procurements start in time.
- Report procurement compliance, savings, and social value to the audit committee.
- Hold the procurement team budget and develop the team's skills.

## Band 8c: Head of commercial

Leads procurement, category management, and contract management together as one commercial function, and sets the commercial strategy.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the commercial function, including procurement, category management, and contract management.
- Set the commercial strategy, including the approach to technology and cloud contracts and lock-in.
- Lead negotiations with strategic suppliers, such as clinical system and cloud providers.
- Lead the response to supplier failure and serious contract disputes.
- Advise executive directors on commercial risk, value for money, and the commercial case for major investments.
- Lead collaborative commercial work with other health and care organisations.

## Band 8d: Director of procurement and commercial

Is the organisation's senior commercial leader, accountable for procurement and commercial policy, and advises the board on commercial strategy and risk.

### Responsibilities

- Set the organisation's procurement and commercial policy and strategy, approved by the board.
- Advise the board and executive team on commercial strategy, risk, and the largest contracts and investments.
- Assure the organisation's compliance with public procurement rules and its readiness to defend challenges.
- Lead executive relationships with strategic suppliers and public sector buying organisations.
- Set social value and sustainable procurement goals and report outcomes to the board.
- Lead and develop the commercial leadership team and the organisation's commercial skills.
