# Delivery manager

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Product and delivery](../index.md#product-and-delivery)  
**Bands:** 5, 7, 8a, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Delivery manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/delivery-manager/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [ICT project manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/8b6388a4-4904-471b-9331-d3b1211f5525) (ISCO-08 1330)

## Summary

Delivery managers help multidisciplinary teams deliver digital health services that work for patients, clinicians, and care staff. They create the conditions for teams to work well, remove blockers, manage risks and dependencies, and keep delivery moving at a sustainable pace, using agile and lean ways of working.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Releases to services that support patient care must pass clinical safety and information governance checks, so delivery managers build these into the team's plans and definition of done.
- Delivery often depends on clinicians and care staff who have limited time away from patients, so delivery managers plan research, testing, and training around clinical rotas.
- Changes to live clinical services need careful timing, so delivery managers plan go-lives with operational teams to avoid peak clinical periods and to have support in place.
- Teams often depend on suppliers of clinical systems and on other health and care organisations, so delivery managers manage external dependencies and contracts.

## Band 5: Associate delivery manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Associate delivery manager**

> At this role level, you will:
> - work on a small or mature Agile team
> - learn on the job
> - potentially work alongside a delivery manager on a larger team, shadowing and providing support, or working to deliver an element under guidance and mentorship

### Responsibilities

- Facilitate team ceremonies such as stand-ups, planning, show and tells, and retrospectives.
- Keep the team's board, risk and issue log, and dependency records up to date.
- Track the clinical safety, information governance, and accessibility tasks needed before each release.
- Arrange sessions with clinicians, care staff, and patients for research and testing, working around clinical rotas.
- Shadow and support a delivery manager on a larger team, delivering parts of the work under guidance.

## Band 7: Delivery manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Delivery manager**

> A delivery manager is accountable for the performance of the team.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - build and maintain teams, ensuring they are motivated, collaborating and working well
> - identify obstacles and help the team to overcome them
> - focus the team on what is most important to the delivery of products and services
> - encourage and facilitate continuous improvement of the delivery team
> - coach and mentor both team members and others to apply the most appropriate Agile and Lean tools and techniques
> The complexity or breadth of products or teams will vary in this role, depending on the context.

### Responsibilities

- Lead the delivery of a digital health product or service through discovery, alpha, beta, and live.
- Plan releases with operational and clinical teams, including go-live support, training, and rollback plans.
- Make sure clinical safety, information governance, and accessibility assurance happens in step with delivery, not at the end.
- Manage the team's budget, supplier contracts, and resourcing.
- Report progress, risks, and benefits to governance boards in plain terms.
- Build a healthy team culture and coach team members in agile ways of working.

## Band 8a: Senior delivery manager

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior delivery manager**

> A senior delivery manager is accountable for the effective delivery of complex, high risk products and services. The role is similar to a delivery manager role, but senior delivery managers have more experience across a range of products and services, throughout the entire product life cycle, and have greater responsibility and accountability as the main point of escalation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - have strong communication skills and engage senior stakeholders
> - coach and mentor delivery managers

### Responsibilities

- Lead the delivery of complex or high-risk services, such as those used in urgent care or that replace a core clinical system.
- Act as the main point of escalation for delivery risks, issues, and dependencies across several teams.
- Plan large-scale rollouts across sites and organisations, with clinical safety officers and operational leads.
- Manage budgets, commercial arrangements, and supplier performance for the service.
- Line manage and develop delivery managers.

## Band 8c: Head of delivery management

**UK GDaD PCF level: Head of (Agile) delivery management**

> A head of (Agile) delivery management is an experienced practitioner who exemplifies what good looks like across the delivery roles.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - represent and champion the role within your department, across government and in industry
> - lead the community of practice for this role and build capability and excellence (in an Agile, Lean practice)
> - be responsible for the recruitment of the right people to the right teams
> - support professional development and continuous improvement of your community
> - work with other heads of roles to promote effective cross-functional delivery
> - be a skilled team leader who can confidently communicate the value of the role to digital and non-digital stakeholders
> - be credible and influential across departments

### Responsibilities

- Lead the delivery management profession and set the organisation's standards for agile delivery.
- Recruit, develop, and deploy delivery managers across the portfolio.
- Make sure delivery approaches meet clinical safety, information governance, and assurance requirements without slowing teams needlessly.
- Advise executive and clinical leaders on delivery risks, capacity, and priorities.
- Manage the profession's budget and workforce plan.
