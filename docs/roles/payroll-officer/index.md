# Payroll officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Finance](../../#finance)  
**Bands:** 3, 4, 5, 6  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [payroll clerk](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/ade351da-7bda-424e-8682-bbdcab2a25be) (ISCO-08 4313)

## Summary

Payroll officers make sure every member of staff is paid accurately and on time. They process starters, leavers, and changes, calculate pay and deductions, run payroll checks, and answer pay queries. In a digital health care organisation the payroll may include clinical, digital, and corporate staff on different contracts and pay rules, and it is often run on a shared or outsourced payroll system that the team must check and assure.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Clinical staff may have complex pay, such as unsocial hours, on-call, and multiple contracts, which payroll must calculate correctly.
- Pay rules and annual pay awards are often set nationally or by funders, and must be applied promptly and accurately.
- Payroll is usually the organisation's largest single cost, so errors and fraud have a big effect on public money and on staff trust.
- Payroll data is highly sensitive personal information, including health, family, and bank details, so information governance is central to the job.
- Payroll depends on accurate data from workforce and rostering systems, so officers work closely with HR and system teams.

## Band 3: Payroll officer

Payroll officers process pay changes and calculate pay for a group of staff, following procedures and checking their work before each payroll closes.

### Responsibilities

- Enter starters, leavers, and contract changes into the payroll system from authorised forms.
- Calculate pay, including overtime, enhancements, sickness, and family leave payments.
- Apply tax, pension, and other deductions correctly.
- Answer pay queries from staff and managers tactfully and confidentially.
- Check payroll outputs for errors and correct them before the payroll closes.
- Follow payroll controls and information governance rules.

## Band 4: Senior payroll officer

Senior payroll officers handle complex payroll cases, check the work of payroll officers, and help run payroll reconciliations and returns.

### Responsibilities

- Resolve complex pay cases, such as back pay, multiple contracts, and salary sacrifice.
- Recover overpayments sensitively and in line with policy.
- Reconcile payroll to the ledger, and prepare tax and pension reconciliations.
- Check the work of payroll officers and sign off sections of the payroll.
- Test pay award and rule changes in the payroll system before they go live.
- Train payroll officers and give basic pay advice to managers.

## Band 5: Payroll team leader

Payroll team leaders manage the payroll team day to day, run the full payroll cycle, and make sure statutory reporting and pension returns are accurate and on time.

### Responsibilities

- Plan and run the payroll cycle, making sure cut-offs, checks, and payment deadlines are met.
- Sign off payroll checks and investigate significant variances.
- Manage statutory payroll reporting and pension returns.
- Manage the payroll team day to day, including workload, training, and quality checks.
- Work with HR and rostering teams to improve the quality and timeliness of pay data.
- Answer escalated pay queries and complaints.

## Band 6: Payroll manager

Payroll managers are responsible for the organisation's payroll service, whether run in house or by a provider, and assure leaders that pay is accurate, lawful, and well controlled.

### Responsibilities

- Manage the payroll service and its team, and report on performance and accuracy.
- Manage the contract and relationship with any payroll provider or shared service.
- Set payroll controls, such as approval limits, exception reports, and separation of duties.
- Plan for changes in pay rules, pay awards, pensions, and tax, and make sure they are applied correctly.
- Lead payroll system changes and upgrades, working with system and HR teams.
- Provide payroll information for budgets, forecasts, and the annual accounts.
- Lead the response to payroll failures and serious errors.
