# Clinical informatician

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Clinical informatics and clinical safety](../../#clinical-informatics)  
**Bands:** 6, 7, 8a, 8c  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [clinical informatics manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/ef909843-97c6-4c48-8038-2e7aefdfcad5) (ISCO-08 1342)

## Summary

Clinical informaticians are registered clinicians, such as doctors, nurses, pharmacists, and allied health professionals, who use their clinical knowledge to shape the organisation's digital health services. They make sure that clinical systems, data, and decision support fit the way care is delivered, are safe to use, and improve outcomes for patients and the experience of clinicians and care staff.

## In a digital health care organisation

- The role needs current clinical knowledge, so most posts require registration with a clinical professional body and some ongoing clinical practice.
- Clinical informaticians give specialised advice on how systems affect patient care, so their judgement directly affects patient safety.
- They work closely with clinical safety officers, contributing clinical expertise to hazard identification and safety cases.
- Good clinical data depends on consistent use of clinical terminologies such as SNOMED CT, so they help design structured records and decision support.
- They act as a bridge between clinicians, care staff, and digital teams, and often lead clinical engagement for system changes.

## Band 6: Clinical informatics specialist

Brings clinical expertise to the design, configuration, testing, and adoption of clinical systems, working within a product or programme team under the guidance of a senior clinical informatician.

### Responsibilities

- Map clinical workflows with clinicians and care staff and turn them into clear requirements.
- Design and test system content such as forms, order sets, and care plans against clinical scenarios.
- Take part in hazard workshops and give clinical opinions on risks and controls.
- Support clinicians and care staff to adopt new systems safely, including training and go-live support.
- Check that clinical data is recorded consistently using agreed clinical terminologies.
- Gather feedback and data on system use and propose improvements.

## Band 7: Senior clinical informatician

Leads the clinical input to a major clinical system, product, or programme, giving specialised advice on how digital services affect patient care and safety.

### Responsibilities

- Lead clinical design for a major clinical system or product, setting content and configuration standards.
- Give specialised advice to product teams on clinical workflows, decision support, and safety.
- Work with the clinical safety officer to identify hazards, agree controls, and provide clinical evidence for safety cases.
- Lead clinical engagement and adoption across services, championing safe use among clinical peers.
- Plan and carry out evaluations and clinical audits of system use and benefits.
- Mentor clinical informatics specialists and clinicians new to digital work.

## Band 8a: Lead clinical informatician

Leads clinical informatics for a portfolio of clinical systems or a care setting, setting clinical design standards and leading a team of clinical informaticians.

### Responsibilities

- Set clinical design standards and principles for clinical systems across a portfolio.
- Lead a team of clinical informaticians, setting priorities and supporting their professional development.
- Advise programme boards and senior leaders on clinical risk, benefit, and priorities for clinical systems.
- Lead clinical decisions on decision support, alerts, and structured data across services.
- Build networks of clinical champions and lead clinical engagement for large-scale change.
- Commission and lead evaluations and use the evidence to shape the roadmap.

## Band 8c: Head of clinical informatics

Leads the organisation's clinical informatics function and its clinical design authority, making sure that all clinical systems are designed, adopted, and improved with strong clinical leadership.

### Responsibilities

- Set the clinical informatics strategy and lead the clinical design authority for the organisation.
- Lead and develop the clinical informatics function, including its workforce plan and budget.
- Give highly specialised advice to the executive team on the effect of digital investment on patient care.
- Make sure that clinical systems meet clinical safety, terminology, and data standards.
- Represent the organisation with health and care partners, professional bodies, and academic networks.
- Lead research and evaluation partnerships to build the evidence for digital health.
