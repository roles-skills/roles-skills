# Clinical safety officer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Clinical informatics and clinical safety](../index.md#clinical-informatics)  
**Bands:** 7, 8a, 8b  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [clinical informatics manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/ef909843-97c6-4c48-8038-2e7aefdfcad5) (ISCO-08 1342); [regulatory affairs manager](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/779d13be-60b4-48f3-a339-fe483be96c7f) (ISCO-08 2619)

## Summary

Clinical safety officers are registered clinicians who make sure the health IT systems the organisation builds, buys, and deploys are safe for patients. They run the clinical risk management process, lead hazard identification, own hazard logs, and write and sign off clinical safety case reports, using clinical risk management standards such as ISO 14971 and IEC 80001-1.

## In a digital health care organisation

- The role must be held by a registered clinician, so that safety judgements rest on current clinical knowledge and professional accountability.
- Health IT can harm patients through wrong, missing, delayed, or misleading information, so the role assesses risks that are often hidden in data and workflows.
- Clinical safety work runs through the whole life of a system, from design and procurement to deployment, change, and decommissioning.
- Some products are also regulated as medical devices, so the role works with regulatory and quality colleagues where safety cases and device files overlap.
- Safety incidents involving health IT may cause serious harm, so the role investigates incidents and makes sure lessons are learned.

## Band 7: Clinical safety officer

Runs the clinical risk management process for a set of products or changes, leading hazard workshops, maintaining hazard logs, and writing clinical safety case reports for approval.

### Responsibilities

- Lead hazard identification workshops with product teams, clinicians, and suppliers.
- Assess clinical risks and agree controls, then check that the controls work through testing and evidence.
- Write and maintain hazard logs and clinical safety case reports for products and changes.
- Review supplier safety documentation for systems the organisation buys.
- Investigate safety incidents involving health IT and recommend improvements.
- Advise digital teams on the clinical risk management process and safety by design.

## Band 8a: Senior clinical safety officer

Leads clinical safety for a major programme or a portfolio of high-risk systems, signing off safety cases and guiding other clinical safety officers.

### Responsibilities

- Lead clinical risk management for high-risk systems such as electronic health records, prescribing, and decision support.
- Approve hazard logs and clinical safety case reports within delegated authority.
- Advise programme boards on clinical risk and whether a system is safe to go live.
- Lead investigations of serious safety incidents involving health IT.
- Coach and quality-check the work of clinical safety officers and safety-trained clinicians.
- Help improve the organisation's clinical risk management system and templates.

## Band 8b: Head of clinical safety

Owns the organisation's clinical risk management system for health IT, leads the clinical safety team, and gives the board assurance that its digital services are clinically safe.

### Responsibilities

- Own the clinical risk management system, policy, and processes for all health IT.
- Lead and develop the clinical safety team and the wider network of safety-trained clinicians.
- Assure safety cases for major systems and make the final clinical safety recommendation on deployment.
- Lead the response to serious safety incidents involving health IT, including with partners and suppliers.
- Report clinical safety risks and assurance to the board and its committees.
- Build a culture of clinical safety across digital, clinical, and supplier teams.
