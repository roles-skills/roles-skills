# Legal assistant

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Legal](../index.md#legal)  
**Bands:** 3, 4, 5  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** none (this reference defines the role)  
**ESCO occupations:** [legal assistant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/929ef41d-afd0-4d5a-b85b-69eab68fa368) (ISCO-08 3411); [legal administrative assistant](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/0264b794-3771-47aa-a675-78bf13b62ee7) (ISCO-08 3342)

## Summary

Legal assistants and paralegals support the organisation's lawyers so that legal work is organised, accurate, and on time. They manage matters and documents, prepare contracts and bundles, research legal questions, and handle routine legal tasks. In a digital health care organisation much of the work involves technology contracts, data sharing and data processing agreements, information requests, and claims that may involve sensitive patient information.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Legal files often contain confidential health information, such as clinical records in claims, so strict information governance applies.
- Many agreements are for software, cloud services, and data sharing, so staff need to recognise the clauses that matter for patient data and service continuity.
- Deadlines in claims, inquests, and procurement challenges are short and fixed, and missing them can be costly.
- The organisation spends public money on external lawyers, so legal spending must be tracked and controlled.

## Band 3: Legal administrator

Legal administrators run the legal team's administrative processes, such as matter opening, document management, registers, and invoices, following set procedures.

### Responsibilities

- Open, update, and close matters in the matter management system.
- File, format, and retrieve legal documents, keeping them secure and confidential.
- Maintain contract, deed, and signing registers.
- Manage diaries and key dates, and remind lawyers of deadlines.
- Check and process invoices from external lawyers.
- Answer routine enquiries and route requests for legal advice.

### Typical qualifications and experience

- Good administrative and keyboard skills, gained through education or work experience.
- A legal administration qualification is useful but not essential.

### Band outline

- **Knowledge:** Knowledge of a range of procedures, some non-routine; typically a level 3 qualification or equivalent experience.
- **Autonomy:** Works within procedures; decides how to organise own work; refers problems upward.
- **Scope:** Own work and some tasks for the wider team.
- **Leadership:** May allocate routine work or train others in procedures.
- **Accountability:** Accuracy of own work and of records kept.

## Band 4: Legal assistant

Legal assistants support lawyers on a range of matters, preparing documents from templates, carrying out defined research, and managing the practical steps of contracts and cases.

### Responsibilities

- Prepare standard agreements, such as non-disclosure and data sharing agreements, from approved templates.
- Prepare bundles, chronologies, and signing packs for contracts and cases.
- Carry out defined legal research and summarise the results for lawyers.
- Collect documents and evidence for claims and information requests.
- Track external legal spending against budgets and report on it.
- Train legal administrators in routine tasks.

## Band 5: Paralegal

Paralegals handle their own caseload of routine legal matters under a lawyer's supervision, such as standard contract reviews, information requests, and claims support.

### Responsibilities

- Review routine agreements, such as software licences, services, and data processing agreements, against the organisation's standards.
- Manage a caseload of routine claims or disputes, preparing documents and liaising with external lawyers and indemnity schemes.
- Research legal questions and draft advice for a lawyer to review.
- Support responses to complex information requests and data subject rights requests.
- Keep templates, precedents, and legal guidance up to date.
- Coordinate the work of legal assistants and administrators.
