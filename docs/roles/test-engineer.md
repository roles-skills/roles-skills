# Test engineer

> This is an illustrative reference profile for a generic digital health care organisation. It is not an official job description for any employer, and its job evaluation scores are not a formal evaluation.

**Family:** [Quality assurance testing](../index.md#quality-assurance-testing)  
**Bands:** 4, 6, 7, 8a  
**UK GDaD PCF role:** [Test engineer](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/test-engineer/)  
**ESCO occupations:** [software tester](http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/106f79e4-6264-45f1-9e7a-297435cd684b) (ISCO-08 2519)

## Summary

Test engineers build the automated tests, tools, and frameworks that let teams test the organisation's digital health services quickly and often. They write code to test functions, integrations, performance, security, and resilience, and build testing into delivery pipelines, so that changes to clinical and patient-facing systems can be released safely.

## In a digital health care organisation

- Clinical systems are safety critical, so automated regression tests protect the safety controls recorded in hazard logs and are kept as evidence for safety cases.
- Integrations with other health and care systems need automated tests against standards such as HL7 FHIR and HL7 version 2, often using simulated partner systems.
- Clinical services run around the clock and peak at predictable times, so performance and resilience testing models real clinical demand.
- Test environments must not hold real patient data, so engineers generate realistic synthetic data, including rare and edge clinical cases.
- Software regulated as a medical device needs traceable, repeatable testing as part of its life cycle under standards such as IEC 62304.

## UK GDaD PCF role description

> A test engineer designs, builds, automates and executes comprehensive, robust and maintainable test suites. They apply test engineering standards, perform exploratory testing and use diverse techniques to identify risks and improve testing efficiency and quality.
> 
> In this role you will:
> - maintain automated tests in continuous integration, continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines
> - use, develop and standardise reusable frameworks and tools following engineering practices and standards
> - analyse and test artefacts such as products, services and business processes
> - promote quality considerations throughout the development life cycle
> - support the resolution of technical issues

## Band 4: Associate test engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Associate test engineer**

> An associate test engineer works closely with other test professionals to learn test engineering activities and techniques.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - contribute to and maintain technical test suites under supervision
> - follow engineering practices and standards to apply test approaches, plans and strategies under supervision
> - analyse artefacts such as user stories, prototypes, processes and designs with support
> - support the development of reports, recording of outcomes and resolution of defects
> - understand the technical tooling and engineering approach to design and execute tests

### Responsibilities

- Write and maintain simple automated tests under supervision, following the team's engineering standards.
- Run automated and manual tests and record outcomes accurately.
- Help investigate and report defects, including in integrations with clinical systems.
- Use synthetic test data and follow information governance rules in test environments.
- Learn the team's test tools, code practices, and clinical safety process.

## Band 6: Test engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Test engineer**

> A test engineer develops solutions to enable more efficient testing. They follow engineering standards to design and execute appropriate technical tests.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - determine test scope and estimate the effort required
> - select and use the most appropriate test approaches and techniques to mitigate risk
> - use technical tooling and engineering approaches to design and execute tests
> - develop and maintain technical test suites
> - develop reports, record outcomes and support the resolution of defects
> - contribute to and follow engineering practices and standards

### Responsibilities

- Design and build automated functional, integration, and API tests for clinical and patient-facing services.
- Build tests that check messages against HL7 FHIR profiles and other interoperability specifications.
- Generate synthetic patient data that covers realistic and edge clinical cases.
- Add tests to delivery pipelines so that safety-related checks run on every change.
- Estimate test effort, report outcomes, and support the resolution of defects.

## Band 7: Senior test engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Senior test engineer**

> A senior test engineer is responsible for test engineering in their area. They influence, coach and guide others in test engineering, sharing best practice and standards.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - select, use and guide others in using the most appropriate technical tooling, engineering approaches, test types and techniques to identify and address risks early
> - extend, standardise and build reusable frameworks and tools that support testing
> - communicate and document chosen approaches, tools, techniques and outcomes to the team and appropriate stakeholders
> - contribute to and agree engineering standards

### Responsibilities

- Lead test engineering for an area, choosing tools, frameworks, and test types that address its risks early.
- Build reusable test frameworks and simulators for clinical systems and partner integrations.
- Plan and run performance, load, and resilience tests based on real clinical demand.
- Make sure automated tests trace to hazards and safety controls and produce evidence for safety cases.
- Coach and guide other test engineers, and agree engineering standards with developers.

## Band 8a: Lead test engineer

**UK GDaD PCF level: Lead test engineer**

> A lead test engineer sets the strategy for test engineering and influences test engineering practices across a broad area. They develop, monitor and evaluate quality engineering standards, and make strategic improvements to quality engineering in the organisation.
> 
> At this role level, you will:
> - lead a broad area in technical tooling, engineering approaches and test types and techniques to address risks early
> - define engineering standards, enabling others to follow them
> - lead and guide teams in quality engineering strategies and practices
> - lead and guide test engineers
> - escalate risks to senior stakeholders
> - lead and implement continuous testing, identifying opportunities to test earlier

### Responsibilities

- Set the test engineering strategy and standards across a broad area, including continuous testing in delivery pipelines.
- Lead and guide test engineers and teams in quality engineering practice.
- Make sure testing of regulated and safety-critical software meets the required standards and is traceable.
- Escalate quality and safety risks to senior product, clinical, and supplier leads.
- Find opportunities to test earlier and more often, and measure the effect on quality.
