# Abbreviations

On each page, write every abbreviation in full words the first time it appears, followed by the abbreviation in parentheses. After that, the abbreviation alone is fine.

If the abbreviation is regional, give the region first, also in full words with its own abbreviation.

Examples:

- "GDaD" becomes "United Kingdom (UK) Government Digital and Data (GDaD)".
- "ESCO" becomes "European Skills, Competences, Qualifications, and Occupations (ESCO)".

## Rules

- **Each page counts on its own.** A reader may land on any page from a search engine or a shared link, so every website page and every Markdown document starts afresh. A page includes its title, headings, body, tables, and self assessment.
- **First occurrence.** Expand the abbreviation where it first appears in the page's running text. A short label, such as the page title, a navigation link, a button, a table header, or a table cell, may use the abbreviation alone, as long as the page expands it in its first paragraph or in a note close by.
- **Full words, then the abbreviation.** Write "Health Level Seven (HL7)", not "HL7 (Health Level Seven)".
- **Region first.** If the abbreviation belongs to a country or region, start with that region: "United Kingdom (UK) Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework (UK GDaD PCF)". A global abbreviation, such as "International Organization for Standardization (ISO)", needs no region.
- **Nested abbreviations.** Expand each part once. After "United Kingdom (UK)", later text on the same page may use "UK" alone.
- **Keep the official spelling.** Use the owner's own capitalisation, such as "GDaD", and the owner's own expansion where one exists.
- **Quotations stay word for word.** Do not change text quoted from the UK GDaD PCF or ESCO. Expand the abbreviation in this reference's own text before the quotation, or in a note next to it.
- **Data and generated pages.** The rule applies to the data in `data/`, the documents generated in `docs/` and `locales/`, and the website. Where a page is assembled from data, such as a role level page, the page template or the build adds the first expansion, so the data itself does not need to repeat it in every field.
- **Translations.** Translate the full words into the locale's language, and keep the abbreviation in its original form, because it is a name: "Royaume-Uni (UK)", "المملكة المتحدة (UK)". Where a locale has its own well-known abbreviation, it may use that instead, but keep it the same throughout the locale.
- **Not abbreviations.** Product and brand names, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, SvelteKit, and Lily Design System, are names, not abbreviations. Units and version numbers, such as "v3.0", are not abbreviations either.
