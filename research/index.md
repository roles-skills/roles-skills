# Research

Notes on the frameworks and methods behind this reference.

- [UK GDaD PCF](uk-gdad-pcf/): structure, roles, levels, skills, grades, and licence
- [ESCO](esco/): structure, version, data used, and licence
- [Framework comparison](framework-comparison/): the UK GDaD PCF and ESCO compared, and the skills crosswalk
- [Band mapping](band-mapping/): how bands relate to PCF levels, Civil Service grades, SFIA, and job evaluation
- [Job evaluation](job-evaluation/): the 16 factors, scoring principles, and calibration
- [SFIA](sfia/): levels of responsibility

Data tables:

- [`pcf-role-levels.tsv`](pcf-role-levels.tsv): every UK GDaD PCF role level, with Civil Service grades and suggested band
- [`pcf-skills.tsv`](pcf-skills.tsv): every UK GDaD PCF skill, with its id for role files
- [`pcf-esco-candidates.tsv`](pcf-esco-candidates.tsv): ESCO search candidates for each PCF skill, before curation
