# Digital health roles: a sample of real job adverts

To ground-check the role profiles, a sample of 75 publicly advertised jobs was gathered and read in full on 6 October 2026. The adverts came from:

- national health bodies and national health IT bodies
- hospital, mental health, and community health providers
- health authorities
- health technology companies

Most were from public health systems that use a Band 1 to 9 scale with 8a to 8d, and 67 stated a band. The rest were on other pay scales or gave only a salary.

The raw sample names real employers, so it is kept as private working data and is not published here. This note gives only generalised findings.

## Coverage

| Family group | Adverts |
| --- | --- |
| Software development, architecture, data, testing, IT operations, and cyber security | 25 |
| Product and delivery, user-centred design, clinical informatics and clinical safety, and information governance | 25 |
| Finance, legal, human resources, procurement and commercial, and corporate services | 25 |

Bands stated: Band 3 (4 adverts), 4 (2), 5 (9), 6 (10), 7 (15), 8a (14), 8b (7), 8c (3), 8d (2), and 9 (1). Director-level roles were often on a separate executive pay scale, not on the band scale.

## What the adverts ask for

How often each theme appears in the duties, skills, and person specification:

| Theme | Adverts |
| --- | --- |
| A professional qualification or registration (for example accountancy, HR, procurement, project management, IT service management, or clinical registration) | 31 |
| Stakeholder management | 20 |
| A master's degree or equivalent experience (mostly at Band 8a and above, sometimes Band 7) | 18 |
| Electronic patient records and other clinical systems | 12 |
| Clinical safety and patient safety | 11 |
| Information governance and data protection | 11 |
| Agile ways of working | 11 |
| Budget responsibility | 8 |
| Interoperability standards (HL7 FHIR, HL7 version 2, DICOM) and integration | 7 |
| IT service management (ITIL) | 6 |
| Line management | 5 |
| On-call or 24/7 cover for clinical systems | 3 |

This supports the health care skills in this reference: clinical risk management, health data interoperability, information governance, and understanding health and care services. It also supports the qualification lines, which move from "level 3 or equivalent" at Bands 3 to 4, to "degree or equivalent" at Band 5, to "master's level or equivalent" from Band 7 or 8a.

Every clinical informatics and clinical safety advert required current professional registration. Every clinical safety advert named a clinical risk management standard for health IT.

## How the adverts compare with this reference

Each advert was matched to the nearest role level in this reference by title and duties.

**Mostly consistent.** Most adverts sat at the same band as the matching role level, or one band away. Examples:

- software developer at Band 6
- senior infrastructure engineer at Band 7
- test analyst at Band 5
- information and performance analyst at Band 6
- programme manager at Band 8b
- clinical safety officer at Band 7 to 8b
- content designer at Band 6
- senior management accountant at Band 7
- risk manager at Band 7
- health records manager at Band 5
- head of information governance at Band 8a

**One band higher in the adverts.** Several practitioner roles in national digital bodies were advertised one band above this reference:

- user researcher and service designer at Band 7
- product manager and delivery manager at Band 8a
- data scientist at Band 7
- service desk analyst at Band 5
- HR business partner at Band 8a

These are within the normal spread between employers. The reference keeps its bands, because a band reflects the job evaluation of the work, and employers in competitive digital labour markets often band higher.

**Changed after this review.** Two families were consistently lower in this reference than in the adverts, so they were re-banded and re-scored:

- **Architecture** was two to three bands too low. Adverts placed technical architects at Band 8a, solution architects at 8b, and enterprise architects at 8c. The Civil Service grades published by the UK GDaD PCF had suggested lower bands. Architecture role levels now start at Band 6 or 7 for associates, and run to 8c or 8d for principals.
- **Senior procurement and commercial** roles were about one band too low. Adverts placed category managers at Band 8a, heads of procurement at 8c, and heads of commercial or contract management at 8d. These role levels now sit one band higher.

## Patterns worth knowing

- **Titles and bands do not map one to one.** The same title, such as "cyber security analyst", appeared at Band 7 and at 8a in different organisations. Some employers advertise under one title and appoint under another.
- **Combined roles are common in small teams**, for example a product manager who is also the scrum master, or a user researcher who also onboards suppliers.
- **Senior commercial roles sit high**, at Band 8d, in national bodies that manage large technology contracts.
- **Clinical leadership in digital spans a wide range**, from a clinical lead for digital medicines at Band 8a, to a chief clinical information officer at Band 8c in a single provider, to an executive post in a national body.
- **Labour market premiums exist.** One security operations post carried a recruitment and retention premium on top of its band, and some health technology companies paid graduate developers more than Band 8a.
